PTE Core vs IELTS General Training for Canada PR

According to IRCC, both PTE Core and IELTS General Training are approved English tests for Express Entry. Both convert to CLB levels, and results must be less than 2 years old. The main difference is format: PTE Core is computer-based and scored automatically, while IELTS has a human speaking examiner. Choose the format you score higher in.

Are PTE Core and IELTS General both accepted for Canada PR?

Yes. According to IRCC, the approved English tests for Express Entry are IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General and PTE Core.

Be careful with the version. PTE Academic and IELTS Academic are different tests and are not accepted for Express Entry. Many people lose time and a test fee by booking the wrong one.

IRCC treats the approved tests the same way: your scores are converted to CLB levels in each skill, and those CLB levels are what count for eligibility and CRS points. Your result must be less than 2 years old when you create your profile and when you submit your application. For the full rules, read Canada PR language requirements.

What are the differences between PTE Core and IELTS General?

The documented differences are about how you take the test and how it is marked, not how much IRCC values it.

How do PTE Core and IELTS General scores convert to CLB?

IRCC converts each skill score into a CLB level using its official equivalency charts. You need to check your own score in each skill, not just the overall.

PTE Core to CLB

IRCC publishes a separate PTE Core table in the same language test equivalency charts. We don't copy those numbers here, because PTE Core is newer to Express Entry and the table is best read at the source. Look up your target CLB in each skill before you set a score goal.

Also considering CELPIP? Its levels map one-to-one to CLB. See CELPIP vs IELTS for Canada.

Should you take PTE Core or IELTS General?

Take a timed practice test of each. Choose the one where you score higher in your weakest skill.

Choose PTE Core if…

  • You are comfortable typing and working fully on a computer.
  • You speak fluently at a steady pace but get nervous in front of an examiner.
  • You want results quickly to meet a profile or application deadline.
  • You are happy to learn how short, integrated task types are scored.

Choose IELTS General if…

  • You prefer a real conversation with a human examiner for Speaking.
  • You want the choice of paper or computer.
  • You already know the IELTS format, or have an earlier IELTS score to build on.
  • Your Writing is strong in letters and essays rather than short timed tasks.

Whichever you choose, plan your test date so the result is still under 2 years old when you submit your application.

Frequently asked questions

Are PTE Core and IELTS General Training both accepted for Express Entry?
Yes. According to IRCC, IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General and PTE Core are the approved English tests for Express Entry. Both PTE Core and IELTS General results are converted to CLB levels.
Is PTE Academic accepted for Canada PR?
No. For Express Entry, IRCC lists PTE Core, not PTE Academic. Book the version IRCC names.
How long are PTE Core and IELTS General results valid for Canada PR?
For Express Entry, your result must be less than 2 years old when you create your profile and when you submit your application.
What PTE Core score equals CLB 7?
IRCC publishes the PTE Core to CLB conversion in its language test equivalency charts. Scores can be updated, so check the current chart on canada.ca rather than relying on a copied number.
Is PTE Core easier than IELTS General?
Neither is easier for everyone. PTE Core is computer-based and scored automatically; IELTS has a face-to-face speaking test with a human examiner. Take a practice test of each and choose the one where you score higher.
Will a higher CLB guarantee me PR?
No. A higher CLB can add CRS points, but invitations depend on IRCC draws and your full profile. PrepEve provides language coaching, not immigration advice.
