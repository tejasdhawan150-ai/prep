How to Send Documents to WES from India: Step by Step

To get a WES ECA for Canada PR from India, you upload a copy of your degree certificate to your WES account, and your mark sheets or transcript reach WES in a sealed envelope from the university that conducted your exams. The envelope must be sealed, with a stamp or signature across the back flap, and show your university's name and your WES reference number. Since August 2021, WES usually accepts the envelope even if you courier it yourself, as long as it is unopened. Some Indian universities can send your records to WES electronically instead.

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · Written by the PrepEve team

What you upload
A copy of your degree or provisional certificate
What must come sealed
Mark sheets for every year, or a consolidated transcript
Who issues them
The university that conducted your exams (Controller of Examinations or Registrar), not your college
Envelope
Sealed, with a stamp or signature across the back flap, plus the university name and your WES reference number
Who can post it
Your university, or you, as long as the envelope is unopened
WES ECA fee
C$264, plus delivery and tax
Timing
About 2 weeks to verify and 1 to 2 weeks to evaluate, after your documents arrive

Step 1: Open a WES account and get your reference number

Choose the Educational Credential Assessment (ECA) for Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), add every degree you want assessed, and pay the fee. WES gives you a reference number. Your account then shows a Required Documents list for your exact degree and university. Follow that list: it is the final word on what WES needs from you.

Step 2: Upload your degree certificate

Upload a clear, complete copy of your degree certificate, or your provisional certificate if the final one has not been issued. WES says its upload feature is for degree certificates and translations only. Do not upload your mark sheets or transcript: those must reach WES from the university.

Step 3: Get your mark sheets in a sealed envelope

Apply to your university for your mark sheets for every year (or semester) of study, or a consolidated transcript. WES accepts either, as long as they are issued by a degree-granting institution and sent in a sealed envelope. Ask the university to:

  • put the documents in an envelope and seal it;
  • sign or stamp across the back flap, so WES can see it has not been opened;
  • write the university's name and your WES reference number on the envelope.

If you studied at more than one university, each one sends its own envelope. WES will not accept an envelope that has been opened, or one without the stamp or signature across the flap.

Step 4: Send the envelope to WES

Your university can post the envelope to WES. If it will not, WES says that since August 2021 it usually accepts a sealed envelope that you send yourself, as long as it is unopened and properly stamped or signed. Use a tracked courier, and send it to the WES address shown in your account, not one copied from a forum. Once the courier confirms delivery, WES adds your documents to its queue.

Can my university send documents to WES online?

Possibly. WES has digital partnerships with a growing number of Indian institutions, which let them send academic records to WES electronically. If your university is one of them, you do not need a paper envelope. Check the Required Documents list in your WES account and ask your university's examination office before you pay for a paper transcript.

University tips: Anna University, Mumbai University and affiliated colleges

  • Affiliated colleges: your records must come from the university that conducted your exams, through its Controller of Examinations or Registrar, not from your college principal.
  • University of Mumbai: WES only accepts documents issued and attested by the designated official at the institution that conducted the exam, such as the Controller of Examinations or the Registrar's office. Attestation by a professor or a notary is not accepted.
  • Anna University: transcripts come from the Office of the Controller of Examinations. Ask for them in a sealed, signed envelope with your WES reference number on it, and check the current application form and fee with the COE office before you pay.
  • Master's degree holders: WES usually needs your bachelor's documents too. The exceptions it lists are Master of Education, Master of Engineering, Master of Philosophy and Master of Technology.

Mistakes that delay a WES evaluation from India

  • Uploading mark sheets instead of having them sent in a sealed envelope.
  • Opening the envelope to check it, or a flap with no stamp or signature across it.
  • Mark sheets missing for a year or semester, including supplementary exams.
  • Documents attested by the college, a professor or a notary instead of the university's examination office.
  • No WES reference number on the envelope, so WES cannot match it to your file.

What happens after WES receives your documents

WES says verification typically takes about 2 weeks and the evaluation about 1 more week, or up to 2 weeks for complex cases, once all required documents have arrived. Your ECA is valid for 5 years for Express Entry. Add its details to your Express Entry profile to get CRS points for your education. Frequently asked questions

How do I send documents to WES from India?
Upload a copy of your degree certificate to your WES account. Then have your university put your mark sheets or transcript in a sealed envelope, stamped or signed across the back flap, with your WES reference number on it, and courier it to the WES address in your account.
Can I send my own documents to WES from India?
Usually yes. WES says that since August 2021 it accepts a sealed envelope sent by you, as long as it is unopened and properly stamped or signed by the university across the back flap.
Do I need to post my degree certificate to WES?
No. You upload a copy of your degree or provisional certificate. Only your mark sheets or transcript must reach WES in a sealed envelope from the university.
Does WES accept a consolidated transcript instead of mark sheets?
Yes. WES accepts either a consolidated transcript or yearly mark sheets, as long as they are issued by a degree-granting institution and sent in a sealed envelope.
How do I get Anna University transcripts for WES?
Apply to the Office of the Controller of Examinations at Anna University and ask for your transcript in a sealed, signed envelope with your WES reference number. Check the current form and fee with the COE office.
Do I need bachelor's documents for a master's degree ECA?
Usually yes. WES lists exceptions for Master of Education, Master of Engineering, Master of Philosophy and Master of Technology, where bachelor's documents are not needed.
How long does WES take after receiving documents?
WES says verification typically takes about 2 weeks and the evaluation about 1 to 2 weeks, once all required documents have arrived.
