How do you book the IELTS test in Ambala?

IDP runs every IELTS test in India, and you book on its website with your passport.

IDP has an IELTS office in Ambala, but its Ambala page does not name a test venue. When you choose Ambala on the booking site, it shows whether a session is running there on your dates. If none is, these are the nearest IDP centres we could confirm:

  • Chandigarh: two centres, in Sector 17 and Sector 34. IDP notes that the Sector 34 centre has better access for candidates with disabilities.
  • Karnal: Shyam Nagar, on Old GT Road.

These centres run IELTS on computer, are approved for IELTS for UKVI and offer One Skill Retake. The fee is ₹19,000 in 2026 wherever you sit the test. For the steps, see how to book the IELTS exam.

Sources: IDP's Ambala page and its list of UKVI-approved centres, checked on 10 November 2026.
