Canada PR Requirements 2026: Eligibility Checklist for Indian Applicants

To get Canada PR through Express Entry from India, you need at least a year of skilled work experience, an approved language test (CLB 7 in each skill for the Federal Skilled Worker Program), an educational credential assessment for your Indian degree, proof of funds, and a CRS score high enough to be invited. You also need a medical exam and police certificates.

Which Canada PR program do you qualify for?

Most applicants from India use the Federal Skilled Worker Program. If you already work in Canada, the Canadian Experience Class has fewer requirements, tradespeople can use the Federal Skilled Trades Program, and a provincial nomination adds 600 CRS points.

Proof of funds is needed for the Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades programs, unless you can already work in Canada and have a valid job offer, but not for the Canadian Experience Class. All three Express Entry programs are for people who plan to live outside Quebec, which runs its own selection system.

Federal Skilled Worker Program requirements

This is the usual route for applicants in India with no Canadian experience. You need:

  • Work experience: at least 1 year of continuous paid skilled work (1,560 hours, full-time or the same amount part-time) in one TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3 occupation in the last 10 years.
  • Language: CLB 7 in all four skills, which is IELTS General Training 6.0 in each. See CLB 7 in IELTS.
  • Education: a completed secondary or post-secondary credential. If you studied outside Canada, you need an ECA.
  • Proof of funds: settlement money for you and your family, shown with official bank letters.
  • 67 points: on IRCC's 100-point grid for language, education, work experience, age, a job offer and adaptability. Check yours with the 67 points calculator.

The 67 points only let you into the Express Entry pool. Your CRS score decides whether you are invited.

Canadian Experience Class requirements

The Canadian Experience Class is for people who already have skilled work experience in Canada, such as graduates working on a post-graduation work permit. You need at least 1 year (1,560 hours) of paid skilled work in Canada in a TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3 job in the last 3 years, gained while you were allowed to work, plus CLB 7 for TEER 0 or 1 jobs or CLB 5 for TEER 2 or 3. There is no education requirement and no proof of funds. See Canadian Experience Class eligibility and study in Canada to PR.

Federal Skilled Trades Program requirements

The Federal Skilled Trades Program is for people in skilled trades, such as electricians, plumbers and welders. You need at least 2 years of full-time work in your trade in the last 5 years, and either a full-time job offer for at least 1 year or a certificate of qualification in your trade from a Canadian province, territory or federal authority. The language minimum is CLB 5 in listening and speaking and CLB 4 in reading and writing. There is no education requirement. You need proof of funds unless you can already work in Canada and have a valid job offer.

Provincial Nominee Program requirements

Each province and territory sets its own requirements for its nominee streams. A nomination through a stream linked to Express Entry adds 600 CRS points, which in practice leads to an invitation in a later round. Base streams work outside Express Entry, through a separate application to IRCC. Every stream needs a language test, at a level the province sets. See the PNP guide and the Ontario, British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba and New Brunswick programs.

What CRS score do you need for Canada PR?

There is no fixed pass mark. IRCC invites the highest-ranked candidates in each round, and every round sets its own cut-off. General rounds, category-based rounds and French-language rounds can have very different cut-offs, so check the latest Express Entry draws and estimate your score with the CRS calculator.

Since 3 November 2026, CRS points for Canadian work experience and job offers work differently: a new labour market integration factor gives up to 150 points (140 with a spouse or partner). Age, education, language and French points did not change. See Express Entry changes from 3 November 2026.

For most applicants in India, language is the factor they can still improve. Moving from CLB 7 to CLB 9 in all four skills, which is IELTS 8777, adds 56 CRS points for a single applicant, and French at NCLC 7 in all four skills can add up to 50 more. See how to increase your CRS score.

Language requirements for Canada PR

You need an approved language test that is less than 2 years old when you create your profile and when you apply. In English: IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General or PTE Core. In French: TEF Canada or TCF Canada. IELTS Academic is not accepted for Express Entry. IRCC converts each skill to a CLB (English) or NCLC (French) level on its own, so one weak skill can lower your score. See Canada PR language requirements and Canada PR without IELTS.

How much money do you need for Canada PR?

Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades applicants must show settlement funds; Canadian Experience Class applicants do not. IRCC's current table asks for CAD 15,263 for one person, CAD 19,001 for a family of two and CAD 28,362 for a family of four. You show it with official bank letters, and you keep the money: it is not a fee. You are also exempt if you can already work in Canada and have a valid job offer.

The fees are separate: CAD 1,590 for each adult and CAD 270 for each dependent child, plus biometrics (CAD 85 per person, or CAD 170 for a family). See proof of funds and the total cost of Canada PR from India.

Is there an age limit for Canada PR?

No. There is no maximum age. But age is worth up to 110 CRS points for a single applicant aged 20 to 29, and those points fall from 30, drop faster after 40 and reach zero at 45. On the Federal Skilled Worker grid, age gives up to 12 points from 18 to 35. Older applicants usually make up the gap with higher language scores, French, Canadian work experience or a provincial nomination. See the Canada PR age limit.

Your Canada PR checklist from India, step by step

  1. Find your NOC code and check that your work experience fits a TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3 job: see the NOC code guide.
  2. Take an approved language test. In India, IELTS General Training is booked with IDP: see how to book IELTS.
  3. Get your ECA. Your university sends sealed transcripts to WES or another designated organisation: see how to send documents to WES.
  4. Arrange proof of funds if your program needs it, with letters from your banks.
  5. Create your Express Entry profile and check your CRS score.
  6. After an invitation, apply within 60 days: a police certificate through the Passport Seva portal, a medical exam with an IRCC panel physician in your city, and biometrics at a VFS Global centre. See next steps after an ITA and the documents checklist.

The whole process is explained in Canada PR process from India.

What about your spouse or partner?

Your spouse or partner does not have to meet the program requirements, but their results can add CRS points. Their language test alone can add up to 20 points, and their education can add more. They also need a medical exam and police certificates. See spouse IELTS score for Canada PR.

Frequently asked questions

What are the basic requirements for Canada PR?
For Express Entry you need at least 1 year of skilled work experience, an approved language test less than 2 years old, an educational credential assessment for education outside Canada, proof of funds for the Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades programs, and a CRS score above the cut-off of an invitation round. You also need a medical exam and police certificates.
Is IELTS compulsory for Canada PR?
You need an approved language test, but it does not have to be IELTS. IRCC also accepts CELPIP-General and PTE Core in English, and TEF Canada and TCF Canada in French. IELTS Academic is not accepted for Express Entry.
Do I need a job offer for Canada PR?
No, not for the Federal Skilled Worker Program or the Canadian Experience Class. The Federal Skilled Trades Program needs a job offer or a Canadian certificate of qualification. Since 3 November 2026, a job offer adds CRS points only if it is in an eligible high-wage occupation.
Is there an age limit for Canada PR?
No. There is no maximum age, but CRS age points start falling at 30 and reach zero at 45.
What is the minimum CRS score for Canada PR?
There is no fixed minimum. IRCC invites the highest-ranked candidates in each round, and every round sets its own cut-off depending on the type of round and the candidates in the pool.
How much money do I need for Canada PR?
Federal Skilled Worker and Federal Skilled Trades applicants must show settlement funds: CAD 15,263 for one person under IRCC's current table, and more for a family. IRCC's fees are separate: CAD 1,590 for each adult.
Do I need a degree for Canada PR?
Not always. The Federal Skilled Worker Program needs a completed secondary or post-secondary credential, with an ECA if you studied outside Canada. The Canadian Experience Class and the Federal Skilled Trades Program have no education requirement, but education still adds CRS points.
Can I apply for Canada PR without a consultant?
Yes. Anyone can apply directly to IRCC. If you pay someone to help, they must be licensed: a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant, a Canadian lawyer or paralegal, or a Quebec notary.
