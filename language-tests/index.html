Which language test fits your goal?

Start with what you need the score for, not with the test you have heard of. Each government, university and regulator publishes its own list of accepted tests. The table shows the tests commonly used for each goal and where to start reading.

Goal, tests commonly used, and where to start
GoalTests commonly usedStart here
Canada PR (Express Entry)English: IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General, PTE Core. French: TEF Canada, TCF CanadaCanada PR language requirements
Australia PR / skilled visaIELTS, PTE Academic and other tests accepted by Home AffairsEnglish tests for Australia PR
UK visaA Secure English Language Test (SELT), e.g. IELTS for UKVIUK English requirements
University admissionIELTS Academic, TOEFL, PTE Academic (each university decides)TOEFL vs IELTS
French for CanadaTEF Canada, TCF CanadaFrench for Canada PR

Two rules apply to almost every goal. First, the exact version of the test matters: IELTS General Training is not IELTS Academic, and PTE Core is not PTE Academic. Second, results expire. For Canada PR, IRCC only accepts results less than 2 years old. Check the rule for your own goal on the official page before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Which English test is accepted for Canada PR?
According to IRCC, Express Entry accepts IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General and PTE Core. IELTS Academic and PTE Academic are not accepted for Express Entry.
Which French tests count for Canada PR?
According to IRCC, the approved French tests are TEF Canada and TCF Canada. Results convert to NCLC levels.
Is IELTS accepted for Australia PR?
Yes. According to the Department of Home Affairs, IELTS and PTE Academic are among several accepted tests. The score you need depends on the English level your visa requires, so check the official page.
Do I need a special IELTS for a UK visa?
For visa routes that need an English test, UK Visas and Immigration requires a Secure English Language Test (SELT) from an approved provider. IELTS for UKVI is one. Universities set their own admission rules.
How long are language test results valid?
It depends on who is asking. For Canada PR, IRCC only accepts results less than 2 years old. Other governments and universities set their own rules.
Does PrepEve give immigration advice?
No. PrepEve provides language coaching, not immigration advice. For your own case, check the official government page or speak to a licensed adviser.
