How to Check Your IELTS Result Online

In India, you check your IELTS result online through IDP’s candidate login, using the details from your booking. For IELTS on computer, IDP says results typically come within a few days of the test; for IELTS on paper, after about 13 days. The online result is provisional until your official Test Report Form (TRF) is issued, which you can download as an eTRF.

How to check your IELTS result: step by step

  1. Wait for the release day. See the timing table below. Checking earlier will not show anything.
  2. Go to IDP’s candidate login. Open ieltsidpindia.com and go to the candidate login.
  3. Log in with your booking details. Use the account details you created when you booked. Some IDP pages also mention your passport number, which must be the same one you registered with. Follow the fields shown on the login screen.
  4. Open your results. You will see a band for Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, and your overall band.
  5. Download your eTRF. Save the electronic Test Report Form as soon as it is available, and keep a copy somewhere safe.

If you cannot log in, use the password reset on the login page, or contact IDP with your name, test date and passport number.

When will my IELTS result come?

When IELTS results are released
Test formatWhen results are available
IELTS on computerTypically within 1 to 5 days after the test (some IDP India pages say 1–2 days)
IELTS on paperAbout 13 days after the test (some IDP India pages say 7 days in India)

Release times differ between IDP pages, so treat these as typical, not guaranteed. If your result has not appeared well after the expected date, contact IDP rather than booking a new test.

Your Test Report Form (TRF), explained

The TRF is your official result. It shows:

  • Your four section bands and your overall band, from 0 to 9 in half bands.
  • Which test you took: Academic or General Training.
  • Your personal details as entered at booking, which must match your passport.
  • A unique reference number that universities and immigration authorities use to verify that the result is genuine.

IDP says the result you see online is provisional until the official TRF is issued. Organisations rely on the TRF or on verifying it directly with IELTS, not on screenshots of the online result.

How your overall band is worked out

Your overall band is the average of the four section bands, rounded to the nearest half band. If the average ends in .25 it rounds up to the next half band, and if it ends in .75 it rounds up to the next whole band. For example, 6.5, 6.5, 6.0 and 6.0 average 6.25, which becomes an overall 6.5.

Many universities and immigration programmes also set a minimum for each skill. Check your four section bands against those minimums, not just the overall band.

What your band score means

IELTS describes each band in plain words. These are the official descriptions for the bands most people aim for:

IELTS band descriptions
BandOfficial descriptionWhat it usually means for your plans
9Expert userAbove almost every requirement
8Very good userMeets most high requirements, including top immigration points levels
7Good userCommon requirement for professional registration and competitive universities
6Competent userCommon minimum for many undergraduate courses and basic migration levels
5Modest userUsually below university and skilled migration requirements

The gap between 6 and 7 is where most people get stuck, and it is usually Writing or Speaking that holds the overall band down.

Sending your result to universities and immigration

  • You can choose organisations to receive your result, up to five free of charge, usually when you register.
  • Official IELTS guidance says electronic delivery to organisations that accept it has no extra fee. Additional paper TRFs are charged.
  • Many organisations verify results directly with IELTS using your TRF reference number, so enter your details exactly as on your passport.

How long is an IELTS result valid?

Most universities and immigration authorities accept IELTS results for 2 years from the test date. Some organisations set their own rules, so check the one you are applying to. For Canada PR, for example, the result must still be valid on the day you submit your permanent residence application.

Not happy with your score? Request a re-mark (EOR)

If you believe a section was marked too low, you can ask for an Enquiry on Results (EOR):

  • Deadline: within 6 weeks of your test date.
  • What is re-marked: the whole test or specific sections. Writing and Speaking are the sections most often re-marked.
  • Fee: IDP India lists ₹14,250 for IELTS on paper or computer and ₹14,400 for IELTS for UKVI, inclusive of taxes. It is refunded in full if your band score goes up.
  • How: apply online through the EOR option in the candidate login. IDP no longer accepts paper applications.
  • Time: IDP says re-mark results take from 2 hours to 21 days.

A re-mark is worth it when one section is clearly out of line with your practice scores, such as a Writing band well below your trainer-marked essays. It rarely helps when every section is close to what you expected.

One Skill Retake

If one section kept you below your target, you may be able to retake just that section instead of the whole test:

  • Available for IELTS on computer only.
  • Must be taken within 60 days of your original test.
  • Allowed once per original test.
  • IDP India’s fee page lists ₹12,650. Check the current fee when you book.

If you also want a re-mark, IDP says to request the EOR first and wait for the outcome before booking a One Skill Retake. Not every organisation accepts One Skill Retake results, so check before you book it.

What to do after you get your result

  • Met your target? Download the eTRF, send it to your organisations and note its validity date.
  • Missed by half a band in one skill? Compare a re-mark with a One Skill Retake, and look at what caused the drop in that skill.
  • Missed in several skills? Prepare properly before booking again. Retaking without changing how you prepare usually gives a similar score.
