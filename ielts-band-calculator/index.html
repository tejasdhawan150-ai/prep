How the overall band is rounded

Your overall band is the average of your four bands, rounded to the nearest half band. An average ending in .25 rounds up to .5. An average ending in .75 rounds up to the next whole band.

  • 6.5, 6.5, 5.0, 7.0 → average 6.25 → 6.5
  • 7.0, 7.0, 6.5, 6.5 → average 6.75 → 7.0
  • 6.5, 6.5, 5.5, 6.0 → average 6.125 → 6.0
