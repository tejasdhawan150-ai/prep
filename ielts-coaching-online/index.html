Questions

Before you get started

Is the IELTS coaching online?
Yes. All classes are live and online, so you can join from anywhere in India on your phone or laptop. There is no centre to travel to.
Is this suitable for beginners?
Yes. Your counsellor checks your current level first, then suggests the plan and study plan that fits where you are starting from.
Can I join if I have already taken IELTS?
Yes. Many of our students have already sat IELTS and missed their band. We start by finding which skill cost you marks, then focus there.
Do you provide study material?
Yes. You get class notes for every module, and the Self-Paced plan includes 30 recorded lectures covering all four modules.
Will I get Speaking and Writing feedback?
Yes. In Live Coaching a trainer marks your writing (unlimited evaluations) and runs 1-on-1 speaking mock interviews. 1-on-1 Mentorship adds priority feedback within 4 hours.
How does the free counselling work?
Submit the form. An IELTS counsellor contacts you on the number you gave, asks about your target band, test date and current level, and recommends the right plan. There is no payment and no obligation.
What is the course duration?
Each plan runs for 30 days. Your counsellor can suggest how many months you need based on your current level and test date.
What are the fees?
Self-Paced is ₹4,999, Live Coaching is ₹14,999 and 1-on-1 Mentorship is ₹19,999. Your counsellor will confirm current batches and any offers when they contact you.
How do I get started?
Fill in the short form above and tap Get Free Counselling. That's it — we take it from there.
