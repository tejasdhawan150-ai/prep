The PrepEve Method

Five steps. One job: find the mistakes behind your score and remove them.

Most preparation gives you more material. This is a loop that finds where your marks go, fixes it with a trainer, and checks the fix under exam conditions.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Find out where you are losing marks now, module by module.

    Your real starting point

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Pinpoint the specific weaknesses behind your score.

    A short list of what to fix first

  3. 03

    Train

    Targeted practice on those weaknesses, not generic lessons.

    Time spent only where marks are

  4. 04

    Correct

    A trainer reviews your essay or answer and tells you exactly what to change.

    Feedback on your work, by a person

  5. 05

    Repeat

    Practise again under real exam conditions until the mistake is gone.

    Proof the fix holds under pressure

  6. →

    Exam ready

    You walk in knowing what you fixed, not hoping.
