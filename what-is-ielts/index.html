IELTS Full Form and Meaning: What Is the IELTS Exam?

IELTS stands for International English Language Testing System. It is an English test for study, work and migration, jointly owned by the British Council, IDP and Cambridge University Press & Assessment, and launched in 1980. It tests four skills (Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking) and scores each on a band scale from 0 to 9. There are two main versions: Academic, for university study, and General Training, for work and migration, including Canada PR. In India, IDP runs the test and the fee is ₹19,000.

IELTS meaning, in simple words

IELTS is a test that shows how well you use English in real life. Universities, employers, professional bodies and immigration departments in countries such as Canada, the UK, Australia and New Zealand use it to decide whether your English is good enough. Your result is a band score for each skill and an overall band.

For Canada PR you need General Training. See IELTS General vs Academic for Canada PR.

The four parts of the IELTS exam

  • Listening: about 30 minutes, 40 questions.
  • Reading: 60 minutes, 40 questions.
  • Writing: 60 minutes, 2 tasks.
  • Speaking: 11 to 14 minutes, a face-to-face interview with an examiner.

How IELTS is scored

Frequently asked questions

What is the full form of IELTS?
International English Language Testing System.
What is the IELTS exam?
An English test of Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, used for study, work and migration. Each skill is scored from band 0 to 9.
Who conducts IELTS in India?
IDP runs all IELTS tests in India. IELTS is jointly owned by the British Council, IDP and Cambridge University Press & Assessment.
Which IELTS is needed for Canada PR?
IELTS General Training. IRCC does not accept IELTS Academic for Express Entry.
How long is an IELTS score valid?
Most organisations accept IELTS results for 2 years from the test date, as IELTS recommends.
