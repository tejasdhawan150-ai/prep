IELTS for PR from Alberta

In Alberta, three routes use your IELTS General Training score, converted to CLB levels:

  • Express Entry, often through the Canadian Experience Class once you have a year of skilled work in Canada. The most language points come at CLB 9.
  • Alberta's nominee program (AAIP): minimums differ by stream, so check the official program page for yours. A nomination adds 600 CRS points: see how provincial nomination works.
  • A post-graduation work permit after the University of Calgary, Mount Royal University, SAIT or Bow Valley College: IRCC asks for CLB 7 from university graduates and CLB 5 from college graduates. See study in Canada to PR.

Planning citizenship later? Adults aged 18 to 54 need CLB 4 in speaking and listening.

For Express Entry, IELTS 8777 (CLB 9) is where language points jump. Each skill is converted on its own, so a 6.5 in Writing keeps that skill at CLB 8 even with 8.0 everywhere else.

Booking the test in Calgary

IDP's Canada site lists several IELTS venues in Calgary (checked 10 November 2026):

  • Downtown: three venues, on 6, 7 and 9 Avenue SW.
  • Northwest: Brentwood, which IDP says is easy to reach by transit and has parking on site.
  • Northeast: 63 Avenue NE. This is the centre IDP names for its Writing on Paper option in Calgary, where you handwrite the Writing test and take Listening and Reading on computer.

Most sessions are IELTS on computer, and one downtown listing gives results in about 48 hours. Book General Training for PR, and choose the venue and date on IDP's test finder, because both change.

IRCC also accepts CELPIP-General and PTE Core. CELPIP has test centres downtown and in the northeast: read CELPIP vs IELTS before you choose. Results from any of them are valid for two years for IRCC.
