Is this for IELTS General Training?
Yes. This session is built for IELTS General Training, the module used for Canada PR. We cover General Writing Task 1 letters and General Reading, along with Listening and Speaking.
What IELTS scores do I need for CLB 7 or CLB 9?
Under IRCC's equivalency, CLB 7 needs 6.0 in each of Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking. CLB 9 needs Listening 8.0 and Reading, Writing and Speaking 7.0. Each skill is converted separately, so one low skill can hold your level down.
