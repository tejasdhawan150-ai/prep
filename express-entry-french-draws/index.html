Express Entry French Draws: Who Qualifies and How to Prepare

IRCC runs category-based Express Entry rounds for French-language proficiency, and the category has been renewed for 2026. To be eligible, you need NCLC 7 or higher in all four skills in TEF Canada or TCF Canada, and you must qualify for the Federal Skilled Worker, Federal Skilled Trades or Canadian Experience Class program. French at NCLC 7 also adds a 50-point CRS bonus when your English is CLB 5 or higher.

How French category draws work

Express Entry invites candidates in rounds. Besides general rounds, IRCC holds category-based rounds that invite only candidates who meet a specific category, such as strong French. In a French-language round, everyone invited has NCLC 7 or higher in all four skills.

Because only eligible candidates compete in these rounds, the cut-off score for a French round can be very different from a general round. IRCC publishes every round, with its cut-off score, on its rounds of invitations page.

Who is eligible?

  • French test: TEF Canada or TCF Canada results at NCLC 7 or higher in Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking.
  • Results under 2 years old when you create your profile and when you apply.
  • A federal program: you must be eligible for the Federal Skilled Worker, Federal Skilled Trades or Canadian Experience Class program.
  • An English test is still useful. Most candidates also take IELTS General Training, CELPIP or PTE Core. English at CLB 5 or higher is what unlocks the full 50-point French bonus.

What NCLC 7 means in test scores

NCLC 7 is the same level as CLB 7, measured in French. For TCF Canada, IRCC lists these scores:

TCF Canada scores for NCLC 7
SkillTCF Canada score for NCLC 7
Listening458–502
Reading453–498
Writing10–11
Speaking10–11

TEF Canada uses its own scoring scale, and IRCC publishes the TEF conversion table on its language test page. Check which table applies to your test date before you set a target.

How many CRS points does French add?

CRS points French can add
French level (all four skills)What it adds
NCLC 7 or higher, with English CLB 5 or higher50 additional points, plus second-language points
Second-language points3 per skill at NCLC 7–8 and 6 per skill at NCLC 9 or higher, up to 24 (22 with a spouse)
French category eligibilityNCLC 7 or higher in all four skills

For many candidates, French is worth more than any other single change. A candidate who reaches NCLC 7 can gain the 50-point bonus and second-language points, and also becomes eligible for French category rounds.

TEF Canada or TCF Canada?

IRCC accepts both for Express Entry, and both test all four skills. The format and timing differ. Many learners take a practice test in each and choose the one that suits how they perform. Prepare for one specific test, because the task types are different.

How long does it take to reach NCLC 7?

It depends on where you start and how many hours a week you can study. Someone with school French needs far less time than a complete beginner. A trainer can give you an honest estimate after assessing your speaking and comprehension, so you can decide whether the French route fits your timeline.

Will French draws continue?

The French-language category is part of IRCC’s 2026 Express Entry categories. IRCC is consulting on categories for 2027, so check its category page for the latest before you plan around it.

Frequently asked questions

What are Express Entry French draws?
Category-based rounds of invitations for candidates with French-language proficiency: NCLC 7 or higher in all four skills.
What French level do I need for the French category?
NCLC 7 or higher in Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, in TEF Canada or TCF Canada.
How many CRS points does French give?
At NCLC 7 or higher in all four skills, with English at CLB 5 or higher, you get 50 additional points, plus second-language points of up to 24 (22 with a spouse).
Do I still need IELTS if I take French?
Usually yes. English at CLB 5 or higher unlocks the full 50-point French bonus, and your first-language points normally come from your English test.
Is the French category still open in 2026?
Yes. IRCC renewed the French-language category for 2026 and is consulting on 2027.
TEF or TCF: which is better for Express Entry?
Both are accepted. Choose the one whose format suits you, and prepare for that specific test.
