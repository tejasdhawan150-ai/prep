Can you get a Canada work permit from Chandigarh without a job offer?

Usually not. Open work permits exist, but mainly for eligible spouses of some students and workers, and for graduates on a PGWP. What is real:

  • Spouse of a student: usually only if the student is in a master's program of 16 months or more, a doctoral program or certain professional programs.
  • Spouse of a worker: mainly if the worker is in a TEER 0 or 1 job with at least 16 months left on the permit. Spouses of TEER 4 or 5 workers are not eligible under that measure since January 2025.
  • Employer-specific permits need a real Canadian job offer, usually with an LMIA the employer pays for.

Which Canada route fits you: study, visit, work or PR?

Most Tricity applications fall into one of four routes, and each needs different proof.

Canada routes from Chandigarh and what each needs
Your goalWhat you need
StudyAn acceptance letter from a designated learning institution, usually a provincial attestation letter (PAL or TAL), proof of funds for tuition and living costs, and the English score your college asks for. The Student Direct Stream ended in November 2024. See Canada study permit.
Visit familyA visitor visa (usually up to 6 months per visit), or a super visa for parents and grandparents (up to 5 years per entry, with at least CAD 100,000 of medical insurance). See super visa.
WorkUsually a real Canadian job offer, often with an LMIA. Open work permits are mainly for eligible spouses and graduates, as explained above.
PRExpress Entry: at least a year of skilled work, CLB 7 in each skill for the Federal Skilled Worker Program, an ECA and proof of funds (CAD 15,263 for one person), then a CRS score high enough to be invited. See Canada PR requirements.

Which Canada language tests can you take in Chandigarh?

IELTS, CELPIP and TEF Canada are all offered in Chandigarh. For PR you can use IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General, PTE Core, TEF Canada or TCF Canada. In the city, IELTS is at IDP in Sector 34 or 17, CELPIP has had a centre since 2018, and the full TEF Canada at Alliance Française Chandigarh costs ₹26,000. How much does a spouse open work permit cost?
CAD 255: the CAD 155 work permit fee plus the CAD 100 open work permit holder fee, plus CAD 85 for biometrics. Eligibility depends on your partner's program or job in Canada.
How much does Canada PR cost from Chandigarh?
IRCC charges CAD 1,590 for each adult (a CAD 990 processing fee plus the CAD 600 right of permanent residence fee) and CAD 270 for each dependent child, plus CAD 85 per person for biometrics at VFS Chandigarh. You also pay for a language test, an ECA, a medical exam and police certificates.
What does a Canada study visa cost for a student from Chandigarh?
IRCC charges CAD 150 for the study permit plus CAD 85 for biometrics. You also need an acceptance letter from a designated learning institution, usually a PAL or TAL, proof of funds and the English score your college asks for.
Can my parents in Chandigarh get a Canada super visa?
Yes, if you are a Canadian citizen or permanent resident and meet IRCC's minimum income for your household. Parents can stay up to 5 years per entry, need at least CAD 100,000 of private medical insurance and pay CAD 100 plus CAD 85 for biometrics.
