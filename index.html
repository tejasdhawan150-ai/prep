FAQ

IELTS Coaching Questions, Answered Straight

How much does online IELTS coaching cost at PrepEve?

Our Self-Paced course is ₹4,999, Live Coaching (batches of 5) is ₹14,999, and 1-on-1 Mentorship is ₹19,999. Every price is a one-time payment for a 30-day program with no hidden or registration fees. See full pricing details.

How long does it take to reach Band 7 or higher?

Students with some preparation typically reach their target in 30–45 days of coaching. Complete beginners usually need 60–90 days. After your free diagnostic test, your trainer gives you a realistic, personalised timeline — we won't promise 30 days if your starting level needs more.

Is online IELTS coaching as effective as classroom coaching near me?

For IELTS, often more effective: the exam itself is increasingly computer-based, our batches are capped at 5 students (local centres often run 20–50), every session is recorded for revision, and you get trainers matched to your target — not whoever happens to be nearby. You also save daily travel time you can spend practising.

What is the Exam-Cleared Guarantee, exactly?

If you complete all sessions, submit all practice tasks and attend all mock tests but still don't clear your exam, we refund your full fee. The guarantee applies to Live Coaching and 1-on-1 Mentorship plans. The conditions exist for one reason: the guarantee works when you do the work.

How are the live classes conducted?

Live classes run on Zoom, 1.5 hours per session, on alternate days. Every session is recorded, batches are capped at 5 students, and each batch gets a WhatsApp group for daily updates and doubt-solving between classes.

Do you coach both IELTS Academic and General Training?

Yes — we run dedicated batches for both. Academic is typically for university admission; General Training is common for Canada PR, UK visas and Australian migration. Unsure which one your pathway requires? Ask us on WhatsApp — it takes two minutes.

Can I join if I'm a complete beginner in English?

Absolutely. You start with a free diagnostic test, and we build a study plan for your actual level. Beginners typically need 60–90 days to reach Band 6.5–7; we'll tell you honestly what your timeline looks like before you pay anything.

What are the class timings? I work full-time.

We run morning and evening batches specifically for working professionals and students, and 1-on-1 Mentorship offers fully flexible slots. Every live session is also recorded, so a missed class never sets you back.

Yes. Our certified trainers coach IELTS, PTE Academic, the Duolingo English Test, CELPIP and TOEFL. If you're not sure which exam your visa or university pathway accepts, message us and we'll help you choose before you book anything.
