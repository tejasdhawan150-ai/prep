The PrepEve Method

Five steps. One job: find the mistakes behind your score and remove them.

Most preparation gives you more material. This is a loop that finds where your marks go, fixes it with a trainer, and checks the fix under exam conditions.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Find out where you are losing marks now, module by module.

    Your real starting point

  2. 02

    Diagnose

    Pinpoint the specific weaknesses behind your score.

    A short list of what to fix first

  3. 03

    Train

    Targeted practice on those weaknesses, not generic lessons.

    Time spent only where marks are

  4. 04

    Correct

    A trainer reviews your essay or answer and tells you exactly what to change.

    Feedback on your work, by a person

  5. 05

    Repeat

    Practise again under real exam conditions until the mistake is gone.

    Proof the fix holds under pressure

  6. →

    Exam ready

    You walk in knowing what you fixed, not hoping.

FAQ

The questions to ask before you pay anyone

Straight answers. Anything else, ask a trainer on WhatsApp.

Will this actually help me improve?
It helps when the problem is found and fixed, not just practised. Every PrepEve student goes through the same loop: we assess you, pinpoint the specific weaknesses behind your score, train those, a trainer corrects your work, and you repeat under exam conditions until the mistake is gone. How far you move depends on your starting level and the practice you put in, and in your free assessment class a trainer will tell you honestly what it will take.
Will someone look at my individual mistakes?
Yes. On Live Coaching and 1-on-1 Mentorship, a trainer marks every essay you submit (unlimited evaluations) and shows you the exact sentences costing you marks. Speaking is practised in 1-on-1 mock interviews with a trainer, not with an app.
Is this just another recorded course?
No. Live Coaching and Mentorship are taught live by a trainer, with alternate-day sessions of 1.5 hours, 1-on-1 speaking mocks and trainer-marked writing. Sessions are recorded so a missed class is never lost. If you only want recorded lessons, the Self-Paced program is the lower-cost option, and we will tell you honestly if it fits.
Who gives the feedback?
PrepEve's own trainers: Sanjita (founder and trainer, a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant), Lesancy Sharma (master trainer) and Khushpreet (trainer). No anonymous graders, and you can message them on WhatsApp between sessions.
How do I know the trainers are good?
Judge them on evidence. PrepEve is rated 4.7 on Google from 300+ reviews, students like Taranjeet (6.5 to 8.5) and Pratishtha (6.0 to 8.0) tell their stories on video, and the free assessment class lets you sit a live session with a trainer before you pay anything.
Do students actually get high scores?
Yes, and they are named on this page: Taranjeet moved from 6.5 to 8.5 for Canada PR, Pratishtha from 6.0 to 8.0 for UCL, Deepanshu from 6.5 to 7.5, and Google reviewers Anooj (8.5), Yashaswini (8.0) and Shashikanth (7.5) link to their original reviews. Every student starts from a different place, so we never promise a band. We show you the method and the results instead.
Is this right for my level?
Yes, whether you are starting out or you missed your band by 0.5. The method starts with an assessment, so your plan is built from where your marks are going now. Beginners usually need a longer runway, and we will tell you honestly in the free assessment class how much time to plan for.
Do you coach IELTS Academic and General Training?
Yes. The same method (assess, diagnose, train, correct, repeat) runs for IELTS Academic, for study abroad, and IELTS General Training, for Canada PR, UK and Australia visas. Tell us your visa or study goal on WhatsApp and we will help you pick the right module.
Why PrepEve instead of another provider?
Because of the method, not a claim. Most preparation gives you more material. PrepEve finds the specific mistakes behind your score, has a trainer correct your work, and makes you repeat under real exam conditions until those mistakes are gone. Sit the free assessment class and judge it yourself.
I work full-time. Will the timings fit?
We run morning and evening slots for working professionals and students. Every live session is recorded, so a missed class is never lost. Message us on WhatsApp to check current slots.
