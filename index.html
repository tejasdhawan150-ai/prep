FAQ

Honest answers before you spend a rupee

The questions students ask us most. Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp.

What if I don't improve? How does the Exam-Cleared Guarantee work?
The Exam-Cleared Guarantee: attend every session, submit every practice task and take every mock test — if you still don't clear your exam, we refund your full fee. It applies to Live Coaching and 1-on-1 Mentorship. It does not apply to the Self-Paced program.
Does online coaching actually work for IELTS?
It works when the class is small enough for the trainer to hear you. Our live batches have at most 5 students, so you speak in every class, your mistakes get corrected on the spot, and every session is recorded so you can replay it.
Will I get personal feedback, or just lectures?
Personal feedback. On Live Coaching, a trainer marks every essay you submit (unlimited evaluations) and shows you the exact sentences costing you marks. Speaking is practised in 1-on-1 mock interviews with a trainer, not with an app.
I've already taken IELTS and missed my band. Can you help?
Yes. Many of our students come to us after missing their band by 0.5 to 1.0. In the free demo we look at where your marks went, then your plan focuses on those gaps instead of starting from zero.
I'm a complete beginner. Can I join?
Yes. After you enrol you take a free diagnostic test and we build a study plan from your level. Beginners usually need a longer runway than students who have prepared before, so we'll tell you honestly in the demo how much time to plan for.
I work full-time. Will the timings fit?
We run morning and evening slots for working professionals and students. Every live session is recorded, so a missed class is never lost. Message us on WhatsApp to check current slots.
Why pay when there are free IELTS videos on YouTube?
Videos teach the format. They can't tell you why your essay scored 6.0 or which speaking habit is dragging your band down. You pay for a trainer who marks your work, hears you speak and tells you what to fix first. We post free classes on YouTube too, so you can watch before you decide.
Can I reach Band 7?
It depends on where you start and how much practice you put in. Take the free band estimator on this page or book a demo, and a trainer will tell you honestly what it will take from your current level.
Do you teach IELTS Academic or General Training?
Both. We coach IELTS Academic for study abroad and General Training for Canada PR, UK and Australia visas.
How do I enrol? Can I do it on WhatsApp?
Yes. Most students enrol on WhatsApp. Message +91 98777 14284 and our team will answer your questions and help you pick the right plan in one chat.
Do you also coach PTE, Duolingo, CELPIP or TOEFL?
Yes. Our trainers also coach PTE, the Duolingo English Test, CELPIP and TOEFL. Tell us your visa or immigration pathway on WhatsApp and we'll help you pick the right exam.
