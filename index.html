What is the Exam Cleared Guarantee?
If you complete all sessions, submit all practice tasks, and attend all mock tests but still don't clear your exam, we give you a full refund — no questions asked. This applies to our Live Coaching and 1-on-1 Mentorship plans.
How do I enroll? Can I do it on WhatsApp?
Yes! Most of our students enroll directly through WhatsApp. Just message us on +91 98777 14284 and our team will guide you through the process, answer any questions, and help you pick the right plan — all in a quick chat.
How are live classes conducted?
All live classes are on Zoom, 1.5 hours each, Monday to Friday. Every session is recorded. Batches are capped at just 5 students for maximum personal attention. You'll be added to a WhatsApp group for your batch for daily updates and doubt-solving.
Can I join if I'm a complete beginner?
Absolutely! After enrollment, you take a free diagnostic test and we build a personalised study plan. Beginners typically need 60–90 days; those with some prep can see results in 30–45 days. WhatsApp us and we'll assess your level for free.
Do you offer General Training IELTS?
Yes — we coach both IELTS Academic and General Training, including General Training for Canada PR, UK and Australia visas.
Do you offer PTE, Duolingo, CELPIP or TOEFL coaching?
Yes! Our certified trainers coach for IELTS, PTE, Duolingo English Test, CELPIP, and TOEFL. WhatsApp us to discuss which exam is best for your visa or immigration pathway.
What are the class timings?
We offer flexible morning and evening slots to suit working professionals and students. Once you enrol, your trainer will coordinate a schedule that works for you. WhatsApp us to check available slots.
Why choose PrepEve over other IELTS coaching platforms?
Most coaching platforms charge ₹20,000–50,000 for batch classes 2–3 times a week with limited personal feedback. PrepEve runs daily live sessions in batches capped at 5 students, gives certified trainer feedback on every submission, and backs it with an exam-cleared-or-refund guarantee — starting from ₹4,999.
