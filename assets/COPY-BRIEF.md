# PrepEve copy brief

Single source of truth for all site copy. Every page rewrite must follow it. Not linked from any page.

## Who we're talking to
18–40, mostly Indian, preparing for IELTS for Canada PR, UK/Australia visas or study abroad. Many are working, many have already sat the exam once and missed their band by 0.5–1.0. English is often a second language: write short, plain sentences.

What they're really buying: the band their visa or university needs, on the next attempt, without wasting more months or another exam fee. Underneath that: fear of failing again, time pressure, money spent, family plans on hold. Acknowledge it; never exploit it.

## Core positioning
**Most IELTS students don't need more material. They need someone to show them exactly where they're losing marks — and fix it with them.**

PrepEve does that with:
1. Live classes in batches of max 5 → a trainer actually hears you and corrects you.
2. Trainer-marked writing (unlimited evaluations on Live) → you know why Writing is stuck.
3. 1-on-1 speaking mocks → you rehearse the real test with a human.
4. Full-length mock tests → no surprises on exam day.
5. WhatsApp access to your trainer → doubts don't wait for the next class.
6. The Exam-Cleared Guarantee (conditions below) → the risk sits with us if you do the work.

Hierarchy on every page: PROMISE → REASON TO BELIEVE → PROOF → BENEFITS → FEATURES → CTA.
Translate every feature: FEATURE → so what (benefit) → so what (outcome) → what it feels like.
Example: "Unlimited writing evaluations" → "Every essay comes back marked by a trainer, with the exact sentences costing you marks" → "You stop guessing why Writing is stuck at 6.5".

## Voice
Confident, expert, direct, warm, honest, specific. Short sentences. Strong verbs. Numbers only where real.
Never: "unlock your potential", "dream big", "journey", "transform your life", "best IELTS coaching", "world-class", "India's most trusted", exclamation-mark hype, ALL-CAPS urgency, competitor bashing. Contrast approaches (self-study vs trainer feedback; 40-student batch vs 5), not named institutes.

## Microcopy standards
- Labels say what we'll do with it: "WhatsApp number — we'll send your class link here".
- Errors are kind and specific: "Please enter a 10-digit mobile number".
- Reassurance under every form: what happens next + no payment.
- Loading: "Reserving your seat…" / "Booking your class…". Success: say exactly what happens next and when.

## Free offers — sell the value, not the ₹0
Webinar: "Even if you never join a course, you'll leave knowing where your marks are going and what to fix first." Why free: "It's how most of our students first meet us — we'd rather show you than tell you."
Demo: a live mini-class + your current level assessed + a personal study plan to keep.
