Why are Dehradun learners choosing online IELTS coaching?

Dehradun's universities are spread around the valley: UPES beyond Prem Nagar, Graphic Era at Clement Town, DIT on the Mussoorie road. Getting from any of them to a coaching class in town is slow at the best of times and slower in the rains. Learners in Rishikesh, Haridwar and the hill towns have it harder still.

A live online class works from a hostel room or from home. The trainer still marks your writing and practises speaking with you, and heavy rain does not cancel your lesson.

Joining from across Dehradun

Rajpur Road, Ballupur and Prem Nagar

Ballupur is where the test happens: IDP's centre is in Vasundhara Complex at Ballupur Chowk. For classes, an evening batch at home saves the drive along Rajpur Road or Chakrata Road after college or work.

Students at universities in Dehradun

At UPES, Graphic Era, DIT, Doon University or Uttaranchal University, your timetable changes every semester. Pick a batch that sits outside it, join from the hostel and keep your weekends for mock tests.

Rishikesh, Haridwar and the hill towns

From Rishikesh, Haridwar, Mussoorie or Vikasnagar, the road to Dehradun is the problem, not the class. Study online and you make that journey once, on test day.

Also nearby: IELTS coaching in Delhi NCR, Chandigarh, Ambala and Lucknow.

Why people in Dehradun take IELTS

Dehradun is a student city, so the usual reason is a degree abroad. PR comes second.

  • A master's abroad after UPES, Graphic Era or Doon University: IELTS Academic. Universities often ask for a minimum in each skill as well as overall, so read the course page closely. Our guides cover the UK, the USA, Ireland and New Zealand.
  • Migrating to Canada or Australia: Canada wants General Training, while Australia gives you a choice of tests. See Canada's language rules and the English test for Australia PR.
  • Nurses from AIIMS Rishikesh or Dehradun's medical colleges: registration abroad usually needs IELTS Academic. IELTS for nurses lists the scores by country.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best IELTS coaching in Dehradun?
Our pick is PrepEve. You join live online classes from a hostel or home anywhere in the Doon valley. Your writing is marked by a trainer, your speaking is practised 1-on-1, and you are told which skill deserves your first hours. A free assessment class shows you where you stand.
Is there IELTS coaching near me in Prem Nagar or Rishikesh?
PrepEve works in both, because nothing happens in a classroom. You join a live video class from Prem Nagar, Rishikesh, Haridwar or the city, and the essays you submit are marked by your trainer.
Does PrepEve have an IELTS centre in Dehradun?
No. You will not find a PrepEve office in Dehradun, because it only teaches online. The test is a separate booking, at IDP's centre at Ballupur Chowk.
Where is the IELTS test centre in Dehradun?
IDP's centre is on the 2nd floor of Vasundhara Complex on GMS Road, at Ballupur Chowk. The Rajpur Road address belongs to IDP's student counselling branch, where no tests are held. Our last check of IDP's site was on 10 November 2026.
I live in Rishikesh or Haridwar. Where do I take the test?
We found no IDP test centre listed for either town, only registration partners. The Dehradun centre at Ballupur Chowk is the one IDP lists for the area. Look up your dates on the booking site first, then plan the trip.
Is the Dehradun centre approved for IELTS for UKVI?
Yes. IDP includes Ballupur Chowk among its UKVI-approved centres. The UKVI version costs ₹19,250 in 2026, and you need it only when a UK visa route demands a Secure English Language Test.
