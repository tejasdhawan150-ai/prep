IELTS General Training Letter Samples

In IELTS General Training Writing Task 1 you write a letter of at least 150 words in about 20 minutes. Below are three model letters (formal, semi-formal and informal), with the structure, greetings and closings to use for each.

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Written by the PrepEve team

Formal, semi-formal or informal?

TypeWho toOpeningClosing
FormalSomeone you don't know (a company, a manager you've never met)Dear Sir or Madam / Dear Mr ThompsonYours faithfully (no name) / Yours sincerely (name)
Semi-formalSomeone you know but not closely (your manager, a landlord)Dear Ms CollinsYours sincerely / Kind regards
InformalA friend or relativeDear Sam / Hi SamBest wishes / Love / See you soon

A simple structure that works

  1. Opening line: say why you are writing.
  2. One paragraph per bullet point in the task. Cover all three, or your Task Achievement score drops.
  3. Closing line: what you want to happen next.

Write at least 150 words. Spend about 20 minutes on Task 1 and leave 40 minutes for the essay.

Sample 1: formal complaint letter

Task: You bought a washing machine that has stopped working. Write to the store. Say what you bought and when, describe the problem, and say what you want them to do.

Dear Mr Thompson, I am writing to complain about the washing machine I bought from your store on 12 September, as it has stopped working only three weeks after delivery. From the first week, the machine made a loud knocking noise during the spin cycle. Last Saturday it stopped completely in the middle of a wash and now shows an error code that the manual does not explain. As a result, I have had to use a laundrette, which is both inconvenient and expensive for a family of four. I would therefore like you to arrange for a technician to inspect the machine this week. If it cannot be repaired quickly, I would expect a replacement or a full refund. I have kept the receipt and the warranty card, and I can send copies if needed. I look forward to your prompt reply. Yours sincerely, Ravi Sharma

Sample 2: semi-formal request letter

Task: Write to your manager asking for two days off. Say why you need the leave, explain how your work will be covered, and suggest how to contact you.

Dear Ms Collins, I am writing to request two days of leave on 14 and 15 November in order to attend my sister's wedding in Jaipur. I am aware that this falls close to the end of the quarter, so I have already planned my work around it. The monthly report will be completed and shared with the team by 12 November, and my colleague Priya has kindly agreed to handle any client queries while I am away. I will also be reachable by email in case of anything urgent. I would be very grateful if you could approve this request, and I am happy to discuss it if you have any concerns. Yours sincerely, Anil Mehta

Sample 3: informal letter to a friend

Task: A friend is visiting your city. Congratulate them on their news, suggest places to visit and offer to meet.

Dear Sam, It was so good to get your message, and congratulations on the new job! I'm really pleased for you. You asked about places to visit when you come to Chandigarh next month. You definitely shouldn't miss the Rock Garden. It's full of sculptures made from recycled material, and it's much bigger than it looks from the entrance. Sukhna Lake is lovely in the early evening, when it's cooler and you can take a pedal boat out. If you like food, Sector 17 has some great small cafés. I'd love to show you around myself. I'm free on the weekend of the 18th, so let me know your dates and we'll plan something. Can't wait to see you! Best wishes, Meera

Common mistakes

  • Mixing tone, for example "Dear Sir" followed by "Hey, what's up".
  • Missing one of the three bullet points.
  • Memorised openings that do not fit the task.
  • Writing under 150 words, or spending 30+ minutes on Task 1.

Frequently asked questions

How many words should an IELTS GT letter be?
At least 150 words. Around 160–190 is a comfortable target.
Do I need to write an address or date?
No. Start directly with the greeting, for example "Dear Mr Thompson,".
When do I use Yours faithfully?
When you start with "Dear Sir or Madam" and do not know the person's name. If you use their name, end with "Yours sincerely".
How is the letter scored?
On Task Achievement, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Range and Accuracy, each equally weighted.
