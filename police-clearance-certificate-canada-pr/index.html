Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) for Canada PR from India

For Canada PR, IRCC needs a police certificate from every country where you lived for 6 months or more in a row since age 18; for Express Entry, it asks about the last 10 years. If you live in India, you apply for a Police Clearance Certificate (PCC) on the Passport Seva portal, pay online, book an appointment at a Passport Seva Kendra or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra, and visit with your passport. The PCC is issued after police verification. For the country you live in now, the certificate must be issued no more than 6 months before you submit your PR application.

Who needs a police certificate for Canada PR?

IRCC asks for police certificates from you and from every family member aged 18 or older in your application. You need one from each country or territory where you lived for 6 months or more in a row since you turned 18. In Express Entry, the online form asks about every country where you or a family member stayed for 6 months or longer in the last 10 years.

If you have lived only in India as an adult, you need one PCC from India. If you also worked in, say, the UAE for a year, you need a certificate from the UAE too, issued after your last stay there.

How to get a PCC in India: 5 steps

  1. Register on the Passport Seva portal and log in.
  2. Fill in the Police Clearance Certificate form online.
  3. Pay the fee online and book an appointment. Online payment is needed to book at a Passport Seva Kendra (PSK), Post Office PSK or passport office. PCC applications are not accepted at passport camps.
  4. Visit on your appointment date with your original passport and self-attested copies of its first two and last two pages, plus proof of your present address if it differs from the one in your passport. Use the portal's Document Advisor for your exact list.
  5. Wait for police verification. Once the report is clear, the PCC is sent to your address.

Check the current fee with the portal's fee calculator before you pay: Passport Seva's PCC instruction sheet lists ₹500.

If you live outside India

Indian citizens living abroad usually apply for a PCC through the Indian embassy or consulate where they live. For any other country you have lived in, follow that country's own process. IRCC's police certificate pages list the steps for each country, and some countries will only issue a certificate if you show an official request letter from IRCC.

When to apply: the 6-month rule and the 60-day deadline

After an Express Entry invitation you have 60 days to submit your full application. A PCC for the country where you live now must be no more than 6 months old when you submit, so apply early enough to have it in time, but not so early that it expires. IRCC suggests starting your police checks as soon as you are in the Express Entry pool, so a slow certificate does not cost you your invitation.

What if your police certificate is late?

If you do not have a police certificate by the 60-day deadline, IRCC says you can upload a letter of explanation and proof that you made your best effort to get it, such as a confirmation or payment receipt, a tracking number, or a note from the issuing agency explaining the delay. Your letter must describe every step you took. IRCC says it accepts this only in rare cases, such as when you applied in the first week after your invitation, and an officer can reject an application as incomplete if they are not satisfied.

Other documents you need after an invitation

Police certificates are one part of the application. You also need reference letters for your work, proof of funds if your program requires it, a medical exam, photos and family documents. See the full Canada PR documents checklist and what to do after an Express Entry invitation.

Frequently asked questions

What is a PCC for Canada PR?
A PCC, or Police Clearance Certificate, shows whether you have a criminal record in a country. IRCC needs one from each country where you lived for 6 months or more in a row since age 18.
How do I apply for a PCC in India for Canada PR?
Register on the Passport Seva portal, fill in the PCC form, pay online and book an appointment at a Passport Seva Kendra or Post Office Passport Seva Kendra, then visit with your passport and documents. The PCC is issued after police verification.
How long is a PCC valid for Canada PR?
For the country you live in now, IRCC needs a certificate issued no more than 6 months before you submit your application. For other countries, it must be issued after your last stay of 6 months or more there.
Do my family members need a PCC?
Yes. IRCC asks for police certificates from your spouse or partner and any family member aged 18 or older in your application.
How much does a PCC cost in India?
The fee is shown when you book on the Passport Seva portal. Its PCC instruction sheet lists ₹500, so check the fee calculator for the current amount.
What if my PCC does not arrive within 60 days?
Upload a letter of explanation and proof that you applied, such as a receipt or tracking number. IRCC accepts this only in rare cases, so apply as early as you can.
