Can a Canadian immigration consultant help you while you live in the UAE?

Yes. An RCIC's licence covers Canadian applications wherever the client lives, and IRCC applications are made online. You give biometrics and take any medical exam in person in the UAE; everything else, from your Express Entry profile to the documents after an invitation, can be done online and on WhatsApp. Sanjita works with UAE clients the same way she works with clients in India: online, with no UAE office.

Which police certificates do UAE residents need for Canada PR?

One from the UAE, plus one from every other country where you have lived for 6 months or more in a row since age 18. For most Indian expats, that includes India. For the UAE, IRCC accepts a police clearance certificate issued by an emirate, a national criminal clearance certificate or a good conduct certificate, and one certificate from any one emirate is enough. You can apply at your emirate's police, online through Dubai Police, or through a representative with a power of attorney. Indian citizens in the UAE apply for an Indian police clearance certificate through the Indian Embassy in Abu Dhabi or the Consulate General in Dubai, using their outsourced application centre; check the mission's website for the current provider. For the country you live in now, IRCC wants a certificate issued no more than 6 months before you submit. See police certificates for Canada PR.

Does your work experience in the UAE count for Express Entry?

Yes. Skilled work experience gained outside Canada, including in the UAE, can count. The Federal Skilled Worker program needs at least one year of continuous paid work in the same skilled occupation in the last 10 years, and foreign work experience can add CRS points when combined with a strong language score or Canadian experience.

  • Reference letters: each employer letter should show your job title, duties, hours, salary and dates, on company letterhead, so the officer can match your work to a NOC code.
  • Funds in AED: money in UAE bank accounts can count as proof of funds. Get an official letter from each bank where you hold money. See proof of funds for Canada PR.
  • Your degree: an Indian degree needs an ECA, with sealed transcripts sent by your university. See sending documents to WES.

Where can you take IELTS, CELPIP or TEF Canada in the UAE?

IELTS is run by both IDP and the British Council across the UAE, CELPIP is offered in Dubai, and Alliance Française Dubai runs TEF Canada. IELTS centres are in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Sharjah and Ras Al Khaimah, and IDP's UAE booking page lists AED 1,590 for IELTS on computer. PTE Core is also accepted for Express Entry; check Pearson's test centre finder for UAE dates. IRCC does not accept IELTS One Skill Retake, so plan for the full General Training test. See IELTS in the UAE, CELPIP vs IELTS and TEF Canada.

How do you check a Canada immigration consultant in the UAE?

Search the person's name on the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants public register before you pay, and make sure that same person signs your Use of a Representative form (IMM 5476). The UAE has many immigration agencies; ask for a licence number before you pay or share your passport. Anyone who charges you for Canadian immigration advice must be an RCIC licensed by the College, a lawyer or paralegal in a Canadian law society, or a Quebec notary. See how to check a Canada PR consultant.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a Canada visa application centre in Dubai?
Yes. VFS Global runs Canada visa application centres at Wafi Mall in Dubai and at The Mall, World Trade Centre in Abu Dhabi, where you give biometrics with your biometric instruction letter. You need an appointment.
Do I need a UAE police certificate for Canada PR?
Yes, if you have lived in the UAE for 6 months or more in a row since age 18. IRCC accepts a police clearance certificate from an emirate, a national criminal clearance certificate or a good conduct certificate, and one certificate from any emirate is enough.
Do Indians living in the UAE also need an Indian police certificate?
Usually yes. IRCC needs a certificate from every country where you lived for 6 months or more in a row since age 18. Indian citizens in the UAE apply through the Indian Embassy in Abu Dhabi or the Consulate General in Dubai.
Where can I take CELPIP in the UAE?
CELPIP lists Dubai as its UAE test location. CELPIP-General is accepted for Express Entry.
Can I take TEF Canada in Dubai?
Yes. Alliance Française Dubai runs TEF Canada. NCLC 7 in all four French skills can add up to 50 CRS points for Express Entry.
Does work experience in the UAE count for Canada PR?
Yes. Skilled work experience outside Canada counts for the Federal Skilled Worker program, and foreign experience can add CRS points when combined with a strong language score or Canadian experience.
Who is a licensed Canada immigration consultant for the UAE?
Sanjita, PrepEve's founder, is a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (licence R710792) who works with UAE residents online on PR, study permits and work permits. You can confirm her licence, or any consultant's, on the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants public register.
How much does Canada PR cost from the UAE?
IRCC charges CAD 1,590 for the principal applicant (CAD 990 processing plus the CAD 600 right of permanent residence fee), plus CAD 85 for biometrics. Consultant fees are separate; Sanjita shares hers on request.
Do you have an office in Dubai?
No. Sanjita, RCIC (licence R710792), works with UAE clients online and on WhatsApp.
