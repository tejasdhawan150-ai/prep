CLB 6 and CLB 5 IELTS Score: What They Mean for Canada PR

CLB 6 in IELTS General Training means at least 5.5 in Listening, Writing and Speaking and 5.0 in Reading. CLB 5 means 5.0 in Listening, Writing and Speaking and 4.0 in Reading. Neither is enough for the Federal Skilled Worker Program, which needs CLB 7, but CLB 5 meets the Canadian Experience Class minimum for TEER 2 or 3 jobs and the Federal Skilled Trades minimum for Listening and Speaking.

Reading needs a lower band than the other skills at these levels: 4.0 is CLB 5 and 5.0 is CLB 6. Each skill is converted on its own, so your CLB can differ from skill to skill.

Where they are not enough

  • The Federal Skilled Worker Program needs CLB 7 in every skill.
  • The Canadian Experience Class for TEER 0 or 1 jobs needs CLB 7.
  • A PGWP for university graduates needs CLB 7.

How to move from CLB 6 to CLB 7

CLB 7 needs 6.0 in every skill. From CLB 6, the gap is half a band in Listening, Writing and Speaking, and a full band in Reading, where 6.0 in General Training needs about 30 or 31 correct answers out of 40. Timed practice helps most in Reading and Listening. Writing and Speaking move when someone marks your answers against the band descriptors and shows you what to change.

CLB 5 and 6 for your spouse

If your spouse or partner comes with you, their English adds CRS points too: 1 point per skill at CLB 5 or 6, 3 at CLB 7 or 8, and 5 at CLB 9 or higher. In the FSW grid, a spouse with CLB 4 or higher in all four skills adds 5 adaptability points.

CLB 5 and 6 in CELPIP

CELPIP-General levels match CLB levels directly: a CELPIP score of 5 in each skill is CLB 5, and 6 is CLB 6.

Frequently asked questions

What is CLB 6 in IELTS?
At least 5.5 in Listening, Writing and Speaking and 5.0 in Reading in IELTS General Training.
What is CLB 5 in IELTS?
At least 5.0 in Listening, Writing and Speaking and 4.0 in Reading in IELTS General Training.
Can I get Canada PR with CLB 6?
It is possible through the Canadian Experience Class for TEER 2 or 3 jobs, the Federal Skilled Trades Program or some provincial streams, but not the Federal Skilled Worker Program, which needs CLB 7. Your CRS score will also be lower.
Is IELTS 6 in each skill CLB 7?
Yes. 6.0 in Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking in General Training is CLB 7.
What CLB do I need for Canadian citizenship?
CLB 4 or higher in Speaking and Listening, if you are between 18 and 54.
What is CLB 6 in CELPIP?
A CELPIP-General score of 6 in each skill.
