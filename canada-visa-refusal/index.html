Canada Visa Refused? What to Do Next for Study Permits, Visitor Visas and Work Permits

If your Canada study permit, visitor visa or work permit was refused, don't reapply straight away. Read your refusal letter and the officer's decision note, which IRCC now sends with most of these refusals, then fix each reason with new information or documents. IRCC says applying again with the same information will likely not change the decision. Why do Canada visa applications get refused?

Most refusals come down to two doubts: that you will leave Canada at the end of your stay, and that you can pay for your trip or your studies. Officers weigh your whole file. The reasons we see most often in refusal letters are:

  • Purpose of visit: the officer was not convinced the trip or program is what you say it is.
  • Ties to India: a job, business, property or family that would bring you back were not shown clearly.
  • Finances: funds were too low, or large recent deposits were not explained.
  • Travel history: little or no previous travel can count against a visitor visa.
  • For students: the program does not fit your past studies or work, a study gap is not explained, or the studies do not look like a reasonable expense.
  • Missing documents: something IRCC needed was not in the file.

How do you read your refusal letter and the officer's notes?

The letter lists the reasons in short form; the officer's decision note explains them in more detail. Since 29 July 2025, IRCC sends the officer's decision note with the refusal letter for most temporary resident applications: visitor visas, super visas, study permits, work permits and their extensions. Parts of the note may be removed to protect sensitive information. Write down each reason, then next to it the document or explanation that would answer it. That list becomes your plan.

Should you reapply, or go to the Federal Court?

Most people should reapply once they can answer every reason. The Federal Court is for decisions that were unreasonable or ignored your evidence, and its deadline is short. There is no appeal tribunal for visitor visa or study permit refusals.

What should you change before you reapply?

Answer every reason with new facts, not new words. A rewritten letter on its own rarely changes the result.

  • Study permit: a study plan that explains why this program fits your background, clear proof of where your funds came from, and any gap explained with documents. See how to write your study plan and the study permit guide.
  • Visitor visa: a clear purpose and invitation, proof of your job, business or property in India, and funds for the trip. See Canada visitor visa from India.
  • Work permit: proof the job offer is genuine, the LMIA or offer number, and evidence you meet the job's requirements. See Canada work permit.

What if the refusal mentions misrepresentation?

Take it very seriously: a misrepresentation finding means a refusal and a ban of at least 5 years. Before refusing for misrepresentation, IRCC sends a procedural fairness letter that explains its concerns and gives you a chance to respond, so reply by the deadline with clear evidence. And always declare earlier refusals: Canadian application forms ask whether you have ever been refused a visa or permit by Canada or any other country.

Frequently asked questions

Can I reapply after a Canada visa refusal?
Yes. There is no waiting period, but IRCC says applying again with the same information will likely not change the decision. Fix each refusal reason with new information or documents first.
How do I find out why my Canada visa was refused?
Read the refusal letter and the officer's decision note. Since 29 July 2025, IRCC sends the note with the refusal letter for most visitor visa, super visa, study permit and work permit applications.
What does 'not satisfied that you will leave Canada at the end of your stay' mean?
It is the most common refusal reason. The officer was not convinced by your purpose of travel and your ties to your home country, such as a job, business, property or family.
Can I appeal a Canada visitor visa or study permit refusal?
There is no appeal tribunal for these refusals. You can reapply with new information, or ask the Federal Court for judicial review within 15 days if the decision was made in Canada or 60 days if it was made outside Canada.
How long should I wait before reapplying?
There is no minimum wait. Reapply when you have new information or documents that answer the reasons in your refusal letter.
Do I have to declare a previous visa refusal?
Yes. Canadian application forms ask whether you have ever been refused a visa or permit by Canada or any other country. Not declaring a refusal can be treated as misrepresentation, which brings a ban of at least 5 years.
Do I need to give biometrics again when I reapply?
Usually not, if your biometrics are still valid: they last 10 years for visitor, study and work applications. You do pay the IRCC application fee again.
Can a consultant promise approval after a refusal?
No. Only IRCC decides. A licensed consultant can show you what went wrong and how to answer it; be wary of anyone who promises a visa.
