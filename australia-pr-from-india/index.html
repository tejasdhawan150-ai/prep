Australia PR from India: Skilled Visas, Points and Process

Most skilled workers in India apply for Australian PR through the points-tested skilled visas: subclass 189 (independent, permanent), subclass 190 (nominated by a state or territory, permanent) and subclass 491 (regional, provisional, with a pathway to permanent residence). You need a positive skills assessment for an occupation on the relevant list, at least Competent English, and 65 points or more. You then submit an expression of interest in SkillSelect and wait for an invitation to apply.

Australia PR process, step by step

  1. Check your occupation is on the skilled occupation list for the visa you want.
  2. Get a skills assessment from the assessing authority for your occupation.
  3. Take an English test, such as PTE Academic or IELTS, and aim for Proficient or Superior for extra points.
  4. Count your points with our Australia PR points calculator.
  5. Submit an expression of interest (EOI) in SkillSelect. For 190 and 491, also apply to a state or territory as each one requires.
  6. Get an invitation, then lodge your visa application with documents, health checks and police certificates.

How points work

Points come from age (up to 30), English (up to 20), skilled work (up to 20), education (up to 20), partner skills (up to 10), nomination (5 or 15) and extras such as Australian study. Invitations go to the highest-ranked candidates in each round, so many people need more than 65. See the full table in our points calculator.

English: the points you can still change

Your age and degree are fixed, but your English score is not. For PTE Academic tests taken from 7 August 2025, Proficient English (10 points) needs 58, 59, 69 and 76, and Superior (20 points) needs 69, 70, 85 and 88 in Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking. Most candidates fall short in Writing and Speaking. See PTE score for Australia PR and which English tests Australia accepts.

Is the points test changing?

Australia has announced it will redesign its skilled migration points test. At our check on 18 November 2026, the current table still applied and no new table was in force. Check the Department of Home Affairs website for updates.

Frequently asked questions

How can I get Australia PR from India?
Most skilled workers apply for a subclass 189, 190 or 491 visa: get a skills assessment, take an English test, reach at least 65 points, submit an expression of interest in SkillSelect and wait for an invitation.
What is the minimum points for Australia PR?
65 points for the 189, 190 and 491 skilled visas. Invitations often go to higher scores.
What is the difference between subclass 189 and 190?
Both are permanent. Subclass 189 needs no nomination. Subclass 190 needs a state or territory nomination, which adds 5 points.
Is subclass 491 a PR visa?
No. It is a provisional regional visa with a pathway to permanent residence later. A regional nomination or family sponsorship adds 15 points.
What is the age limit for Australia PR?
You must be under 45 when you are invited to apply for these skilled visas.
Which English test is best for Australia PR?
Home Affairs accepts several tests, including PTE Academic and IELTS. Choose the one you can score highest in. See our PTE vs IELTS comparison.
