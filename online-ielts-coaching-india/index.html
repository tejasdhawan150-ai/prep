How does PrepEve online IELTS coaching work?

Every plan follows the PrepEve Method:

  1. Assess: find out where you are losing marks now.
  2. Diagnose: pinpoint the specific weaknesses behind your score.
  3. Train: targeted practice on those weaknesses.
  4. Correct: a trainer reviews your work and tells you exactly what to change.
  5. Repeat: practise again under real exam conditions until the mistake is gone.

Why choose online coaching over a local centre?

Online coaching works for IELTS when it is live and the trainer marks your work. You save travel time, you can join from anywhere in India, and every class happens on the same screen you will use if you take computer-delivered IELTS. Recorded-only courses cannot correct your speaking or your essays, so check that the course is live. Read how to choose online IELTS coaching in India for a full checklist.
