How does PrepEve online IELTS coaching work?

Every plan follows the PrepEve Method:

  1. Assess: find out where you are losing marks now.
  2. Diagnose: pinpoint the specific weaknesses behind your score.
  3. Train: targeted practice on those weaknesses.
  4. Correct: a trainer reviews your work and tells you exactly what to change.
  5. Repeat: practise again under real exam conditions until the mistake is gone.

Why choose online coaching over a local centre?

Online coaching works for IELTS when it is live and the trainer marks your work. You save travel time, you can join from anywhere in India, and every class happens on the same screen you will use if you take computer-delivered IELTS. Recorded-only courses cannot correct your speaking or your essays, so check that the course is live. Read how to choose online IELTS coaching in India for a full checklist.

Frequently asked questions

Is online IELTS coaching effective?
Yes, when classes are live and a trainer marks your Writing and corrects your Speaking. That feedback loop is what moves a band, not the classroom.
How much does online IELTS coaching cost in India at PrepEve?
Self-Paced is ₹4,999. For Live Coaching and 1-on-1 Mentorship, a counsellor shares the current fees on a call or on WhatsApp. Each plan runs for 30 days.
Can I join from any city in India?
Yes. All classes are online, so you can join from anywhere in India, or from abroad, on your phone or laptop.
Do you coach both Academic and General Training?
Yes, both, for any target band and in every format: live classes, one-to-one or recorded. IELTS Academic is for study abroad and professional registration; General Training is for Canada PR, migration and work visas. Listening and Speaking are the same in both, and your trainer prepares you for the Reading and Writing of the test you are taking.
Can I try a class before paying?
Yes. The first assessment class is free, and a trainer gives you a study plan to keep.
How many hours of live classes are in Live Coaching?
About 22 hours: a 1.5-hour live class every other day for 30 days, which is about 15 classes. You also get unlimited writing evaluations, 1-on-1 speaking mocks and 5 full-length mock tests.
Which IELTS do I need for Canada PR?
General Training. CLB 7, which is 6.0 in each skill, is the minimum for the Federal Skilled Worker Program, and CLB 9 (8777) earns the most language points in Express Entry.
How much is the IELTS exam fee in India?
₹19,000 in 2026, or ₹19,250 for IELTS for UKVI. You pay it to IDP when you book, separately from any coaching fee.
Does PrepEve also help with Canada PR?
Yes. PrepEve's founder, Sanjita, is a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (licence R710792). IELTS coaching and immigration advice are separate services, and you can ask her about PR on WhatsApp.
Do you offer live online IELTS classes with a teacher?
Yes. Live Coaching is a 1.5-hour live online class with a trainer every other day for 30 days, for Academic or General Training and any target band. The trainer marks every essay, runs 1-on-1 speaking mocks and answers doubts on WhatsApp.
Do you offer one-to-one IELTS coaching online?
Yes, for Academic or General Training and any target band. 1-on-1 Mentorship gives you a dedicated coach and a 1.5-hour one-to-one session every other day for 30 days, with a study plan built for you and priority feedback within 4 hours. It includes everything in Live Coaching.
Do you have recorded IELTS classes?
Yes. Self-Paced is the recorded option, for Academic or General Training: 30 recorded video lectures covering all 4 modules, 2 full-length mock tests, study notes and WhatsApp support for queries, with 30-day access. Live Coaching classes are recorded too, so a missed class is never lost, but trainer-marked writing and 1-on-1 speaking mocks come only with the live plans.
Which target bands do you coach for?
Every target: Band 6, 6.5, 7, 7.5, and 8 or above, for Academic and General Training, in live classes, one-to-one or recorded. Your plan starts from where you lose marks today. Students aiming for 7 or more usually choose live or one-to-one coaching, because Writing and Speaking need a trainer's corrections.
