Australia PR Points Calculator (Subclass 189, 190 and 491)

Use this calculator to estimate your points for Australia's skilled visas: subclass 189 (independent), 190 (state nominated) and 491 (regional). The pass mark is 65 points, but meeting it does not guarantee an invitation. Points come from your age, English level, skilled work, education, partner and nomination. English is often the quickest to raise: Proficient English adds 10 points and Superior adds 20, which in PTE Academic means 69 in Listening, 70 in Reading, 85 in Writing and 88 in Speaking.

What happens after you have 65 points?

You get a skills assessment for your occupation, then submit an expression of interest (EOI) in SkillSelect. Home Affairs and the states invite the highest-ranked candidates. Read the full steps in our Australia PR from India guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is the pass mark for Australia PR?
65 points for the subclass 189, 190 and 491 skilled visas. Meeting 65 does not guarantee an invitation; invitations often go to higher scores.
How many points is Superior English for Australia PR?
20 points. Proficient English gives 10, and Competent English gives none.
What PTE score gives 20 points for Australia?
For PTE Academic tests from 7 August 2025, Superior English needs at least 69 in Listening, 70 in Reading, 85 in Writing and 88 in Speaking.
How many points do I get for age?
30 points for ages 25 to 32, 25 for 18 to 24 and 33 to 39, and 15 for 40 to 44. You must be under 45 when invited.
How many points does state nomination give?
5 points for a subclass 190 state or territory nomination, and 15 for a subclass 491 regional nomination or family sponsorship.
Is the Australian points test changing?
Australia has announced a redesign of the points test. At our check on 18 November 2026, the current table still applied, so check Home Affairs before you rely on any calculator.
