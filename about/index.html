The PrepEve Method

  1. 01 Assess. Find out where you are losing marks now.
  2. 02 Diagnose. Pinpoint the specific weaknesses behind your score.
  3. 03 Train. Targeted practice on those weaknesses — not generic lessons.
  4. 04 Correct. A trainer reviews your work and tells you exactly what to change. Every essay is marked by a trainer; Speaking is rehearsed in 1-on-1 mocks.
  5. 05 Repeat. Practise again under real exam conditions, including full mock tests, until the mistake is gone. Then you're exam ready.

Between classes, you can ask your trainer on WhatsApp. The same method runs our PTE preparation.
