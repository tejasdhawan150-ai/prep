How the coaching works

  • Small live batches. Max 5 students, so your trainer actually hears you and corrects you in class.
  • Trainer-marked writing. Every essay comes back marked by a trainer, with the sentences costing you marks.
  • 1-on-1 speaking mocks. You rehearse the real Speaking test with a person, not an app.
  • Full-length mock tests. No surprises on exam day.
  • WhatsApp access to your trainer. Doubts don't wait for the next class.
