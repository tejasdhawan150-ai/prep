CELPIP Exam Fee in India and Canada (2026)

In Canada, the CELPIP-General test costs CA$299 plus taxes in 2026, and CELPIP-General LS, the listening and speaking test used for citizenship, costs CA$199 plus taxes. In India, CELPIP charges a rupee price that shows when you choose a session on celpip.ca. If you cancel at least 7 calendar days before your test, you get 50% of the fee back.

These are CELPIP’s Canadian prices. Outside Canada, CELPIP sets a price for each country, so the amount you pay in India is different.

CELPIP fee in India

In India, CELPIP charges in rupees, and the exact price, including any taxes, shows when you pick a test session in your CELPIP account. Test-preparation websites reported about ₹15,500 per sitting in September 2026, but we could not confirm that figure on CELPIP’s own website, so check the amount at checkout before you pay.

For Canada PR you need CELPIP-General, the full four-skill test. CELPIP-General LS covers only Listening and Speaking, so it cannot be used for Express Entry.

Where you can take CELPIP in India

CELPIP’s India page lists test centres in cities including Chandigarh, New Delhi, Ahmedabad, Chennai and Hyderabad. Chandigarh was the first Indian city to get a CELPIP centre, in 2018. Centres and dates change, so use the test-date search on celpip.ca to see sessions near you.

What the CELPIP fee includes

  • The whole CELPIP-General test on computer: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking, with your speaking answers recorded by the computer.
  • Your scores online in your CELPIP account, 3 to 4 business days after the test.
  • Scores reported as CELPIP levels, which match CLB levels one to one: CELPIP 7 is CLB 7, and CELPIP 9 is CLB 9 (see the CELPIP score chart).

CELPIP vs IELTS for Canada PR

IELTS General Training costs ₹19,000 in India. Compare that with the CELPIP rupee price you see at checkout, but remember that the cheaper test is the one you pass in one attempt. Other differences matter more:

  • Format: CELPIP is fully on computer, including Speaking. In IELTS, Speaking is a conversation with an examiner.
  • Scores: CELPIP levels match CLB directly. IELTS bands are converted to CLB with IRCC’s chart.
  • Results: CELPIP in 3 to 4 business days; IELTS on computer within a few days and on paper after about 13 days.

Read the full comparison in CELPIP vs IELTS. CELPIP is one of four tests you can take instead of IELTS for Canada PR: see Canada PR without IELTS.

How to book CELPIP

  1. Create a CELPIP account on celpip.ca with your name exactly as on your ID.
  2. Choose CELPIP-General, then your country, city, test centre and date.
  3. Pay online by card and keep the confirmation.
  4. Check the reporting time and the ID you must bring on test day.

How to avoid paying the fee twice

  • Check the CLB level you need in each skill, for example CLB 9 for a strong Express Entry score.
  • Book once your practice scores reach that level in every skill, not just on average.
  • Practise speaking to a screen with a timer. It feels different from talking to a person.

Frequently asked questions

What is the CELPIP exam fee in 2026?
In Canada, CA$299 plus taxes for CELPIP-General and CA$199 plus taxes for CELPIP-General LS. Prices outside Canada are set for each country.
How much does CELPIP cost in India?
CELPIP charges in rupees, and the price shows when you book on celpip.ca. Test-prep sites reported about ₹15,500 per sitting in September 2026; confirm the amount at checkout.
Can I get a refund if I cancel CELPIP?
Yes, 50% if you cancel at least 7 calendar days before the test. Within 7 days there is no refund.
How long do CELPIP results take?
Scores are available online in your CELPIP account 3 to 4 business days after the test.
Where can I take CELPIP in India?
CELPIP lists centres in cities including Chandigarh, New Delhi, Ahmedabad, Chennai and Hyderabad. Check celpip.ca for current dates.
Is CELPIP accepted for Canada PR?
Yes. CELPIP-General is one of three English tests IRCC accepts for Express Entry, with IELTS General Training and PTE Core.
