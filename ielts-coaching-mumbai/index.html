Why are Mumbai and Pune learners choosing online IELTS coaching?

In Mumbai, the commute already takes a large part of the day. A coaching class in another suburb means a second journey on the local or by road, and that is the part most people cannot keep up for a month.

Pune has the same problem in a different shape: the IT parks at Hinjewadi, Kharadi and Magarpatta sit on opposite edges of the city. A live online class starts when you reach home, wherever home is.

Joining from across Mumbai & Pune

Mumbai: Andheri, BKC, Powai and the suburbs

If you work in Andheri, BKC or Powai and live further up the Western or Central line, an evening class from home replaces a second commute. On test day, IDP's centres at Chakala in Andheri East and in Lower Parel are the two inside the city.

Thane and Navi Mumbai

Commutes from Thane and Navi Mumbai are long, so a weekend or evening class from home saves the most time here. You can sit the test nearby too: IDP lists centres at Wagle Estate in Thane West and at CBD Belapur.

Pune: Hinjewadi, Kharadi and Viman Nagar

For IT professionals, and for students at Savitribai Phule Pune University, Symbiosis or COEP, a weekend or late-evening batch fits around shifts and lectures. Pune's two IDP centres are in Shivajinagar and on Boat Club Road.

Also nearby: IELTS coaching in Surat, Ahmedabad, Vadodara and Bangalore.

Why people in Mumbai & Pune take IELTS

What you need IELTS for decides the test.

  • A master's or MBA abroad after the University of Mumbai, IIT Bombay, NMIMS, Savitribai Phule Pune University or Symbiosis: IELTS Academic. Each course sets its own minimum, often in every skill.
  • Canada PR while you keep working: IELTS General Training. The level that adds the most points is CLB 9, or 8777. Start with IELTS for Canada PR and check your points with the CRS calculator.
  • A UK visa route that asks for a secure test: IELTS for UKVI. See the UK English requirements.
