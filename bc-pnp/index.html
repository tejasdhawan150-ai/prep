BC PNP 2026: Skilled Worker and Health Authority Streams

The BC Provincial Nominee Program (BC PNP) Skills Immigration category now has two main streams: Skilled Worker and Health Authority. For the Skilled Worker stream you register a profile, get a score, and wait for an invitation; BC holds draws by sector, such as health care, childcare and construction. Once invited, you have 30 days to apply. A registration is removed after 12 months if you are not invited. The Health Authority stream is for people with an eligible job offer from a BC public health authority, and you apply directly. The International Graduate stream closed in 2024 and the Entry Level and Semi-Skilled stream in 2026.

BC PNP Skills Immigration streams in 2026

  • Skilled Worker: for people with a job offer in a skilled occupation in BC. You register online and are scored on factors such as your job, experience, education and language, then invited in a draw.
  • Health Authority: for people with a regular, indeterminate, full-time job offer in an eligible health occupation from a BC public health authority. You do not need to register: you apply directly.
  • Temporary Rural/Remote Health Support initiative: a separate initiative that opened for registration on 15 June 2026.

BC's program guide is updated often, so check the current version for eligibility details and whether you can link your application to Express Entry.

How the BC PNP works, step by step

  1. Check your eligibility for a stream and get your documents ready, including a language test.
  2. Register online (Skilled Worker) or apply directly (Health Authority).
  3. If invited, submit a complete application within 30 calendar days.
  4. After a nomination, apply to IRCC for PR. After a nomination through an Express Entry-linked stream, you accept it in your Express Entry profile and get 600 CRS points. With a base (non-Express Entry) nomination, you apply to IRCC for PR separately. Either way, IRCC makes the final decision.

Frequently asked questions

What are the BC PNP streams in 2026?
The Skills Immigration category has two main streams: Skilled Worker and Health Authority. The International Graduate and Entry Level and Semi-Skilled streams are closed.
How long do I have to apply after a BC PNP invitation?
30 calendar days to submit a complete application.
How long does a BC PNP registration stay in the pool?
12 months. If you are not invited in that time, your registration is removed automatically.
What were the latest BC PNP draws?
At our check on 18 November 2026, the latest draws were on 13 November 2026: health care (minimum score 72, 103 invitations), childcare (95, 153) and construction trades (94, 128).
Do I need a job offer for the BC PNP?
The Skilled Worker and Health Authority streams are built around a job offer in BC. Check the current program guide for your situation.
Is the BC PNP International Graduate stream open?
No. It closed in 2024.
