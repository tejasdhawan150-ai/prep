Alberta PNP (AAIP) 2026: Streams, EOI and Latest Draws

The Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP) selects workers through an expression of interest (EOI) pool. You submit a Worker EOI (CAD 135 since 7 April 2026), are ranked on a points grid out of 100, and may be invited in a draw. The main worker routes are the Alberta Opportunity Stream, the Alberta Express Entry Stream (with pathways for tech, health care and law enforcement), the Dedicated Health Care Pathways and the Rural Renewal Stream. In 2026 Alberta said it would focus draws on health care, technology, construction, manufacturing, aviation, agriculture and Rural Renewal communities. Alberta's 2026 allocation is 6,603 nominations.

AAIP worker streams in 2026

  • Alberta Opportunity Stream: for people already working in Alberta in an eligible job. It has the largest share of Alberta's nominations.
  • Alberta Express Entry Stream: for candidates in the federal Express Entry pool. Alberta runs pathways in this stream for technology workers (the Accelerated Tech Pathway), health care professionals and police occupations, plus draws for priority sectors.
  • Dedicated Health Care Pathways: Express Entry and non-Express Entry routes for health care workers.
  • Rural Renewal Stream: for people with a job offer in a designated rural community. Its criteria changed on 1 January 2026.

How to apply to the AAIP

  1. Check your eligibility with Alberta's Eligibility Explorer tool (launched June 2026).
  2. Submit a Worker EOI in the AAIP portal and pay CAD 135. You can edit it later.
  3. If invited, submit your application in the portal.
  4. After a nomination, apply to IRCC for PR. After a nomination through an Express Entry-linked stream, you accept it in your Express Entry profile and get 600 CRS points. With a base (non-Express Entry) nomination, you apply to IRCC for PR separately. Either way, IRCC makes the final decision.

Frequently asked questions

What is the full form of AAIP?
AAIP stands for Alberta Advantage Immigration Program, Alberta's provincial nominee program.
How much is the AAIP Worker EOI fee?
CAD 135, since 7 April 2026. A Worker EOI stays in the pool for 12 months.
What were the latest Alberta PNP draws?
At our check on 18 November 2026, the latest was a Rural Renewal Stream draw on 2 November 2026 (minimum score 51, 173 invitations). An Alberta Opportunity Stream draw on 30 October had a minimum score of 59.
Can I apply to the Alberta PNP from India?
Yes, through the Alberta Express Entry Stream or its pathways if you are in the Express Entry pool and meet Alberta's criteria. The Alberta Opportunity Stream is for people already working in Alberta.
Which sectors does Alberta prioritise in 2026?
Health care, technology, construction, manufacturing, aviation, agriculture and communities in the Rural Renewal Stream.
How many points is the AAIP grid out of?
The Worker EOI points grid has a maximum of 100 points.
