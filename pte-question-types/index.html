PTE Question Types: All 22 Tasks Explained (2026)

PTE Academic has 22 question types in three parts: Speaking and Writing, Reading, and Listening. Two speaking tasks were added on 7 August 2025: Summarize Group Discussion and Respond to a Situation. The other 20 question types did not change. The whole test takes about two hours on a computer at a test centre.

Tasks that count for two skills

PTE scores many tasks for more than one skill. Read Aloud counts for Speaking and Reading, Repeat Sentence for Speaking and Listening, and Write from Dictation for Listening and Writing. Tasks like these appear several times in a test, so short daily practice on them pays off across two scores.

Where wrong answers cost marks

In Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers and in Highlight Incorrect Words, each wrong choice loses a mark, though the score for a question never goes below zero. Choose only the answers you are sure of. Other question types do not take marks away for wrong answers.

PTE Core question types

PTE Core, the version IRCC accepts for Canada PR, uses everyday and workplace topics, and some of its tasks differ from PTE Academic. If you are taking PTE Core, practise with its own task list on Pearson’s site.

How to practise PTE question types

  • Practise against the clock. Every task is timed, and the timer does not wait.
  • Record your speaking and listen back for pace, pauses and pronunciation.
  • Avoid memorised templates. Answer the actual question or situation in front of you.
  • Take full mock tests to build stamina for two hours on screen.

Frequently asked questions

How many question types are in PTE?
22 in PTE Academic since 7 August 2025: 9 in Speaking and Writing, 5 in Reading and 8 in Listening.
What are the new PTE question types?
Summarize Group Discussion and Respond to a Situation, both in Speaking and Writing, added on 7 August 2025.
How long is the PTE exam?
About two hours. Pearson’s score guide gives a maximum of 2 hours 15 minutes.
Is there negative marking in PTE?
Only in Multiple Choice, Multiple Answers and Highlight Incorrect Words, where wrong choices cost marks.
Which PTE tasks count for two skills?
Several, including Read Aloud (Speaking and Reading) and Write from Dictation (Listening and Writing).
Does PTE Core have the same question types?
Not all. PTE Core uses everyday and workplace tasks, so check its own format.
