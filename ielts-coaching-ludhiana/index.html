Why are Ludhiana learners choosing online IELTS coaching?

Ludhiana has IELTS institutes on almost every main road, so finding a class is easy. Finding one where a trainer corrects your own writing and speaking, line by line, is harder, and that is what moves a band.

The city is also busy. Crossing Ferozepur Road or Chandigarh Road at peak hours can take as long as the class itself. Joining live from home gives that time back to practice.

Why people in Ludhiana take IELTS

Canada is a common goal in Punjab, but the route you take decides the test:

  • A study permit for Canada: your college or university sets the score, usually on IELTS Academic. The fast-track SDS route closed in November 2024. See the IELTS score for a Canada study permit.
  • Canada PR: IELTS General Training, scored as CLB levels. If your spouse is applying with you, their score adds points too: see spouse IELTS score for Canada PR.
  • Nurses from CMC or DMC who plan to register abroad: usually IELTS Academic, with 7.0 in most skills. See IELTS for nurses.
  • The UK or Australia: check whether your route needs IELTS for UKVI, or whether Australia's rules suit you better on PTE.

How do you book the IELTS test in Ludhiana?

IDP runs every IELTS test in India. In Ludhiana it lists one computer-delivered centre.

Test centre
IDP, Tej Complex, Link Road, Model Town: opposite Preet Palace Cinema
Format
IELTS on computer. Speaking is face to face with an examiner, IDP's Ludhiana page says
Available too
IELTS for UKVI, and One Skill Retake
Access
On IDP's list of accessible centres
What you pay
₹19,000 in 2026

Applying for Canada PR? Do not count on One Skill Retake. IRCC does not accept it for Express Entry, so if one skill falls short you sit the full General Training test again.

IDP also has an office in Feroze Gandhi Market, so check the venue on your booking confirmation. Our IELTS booking guide explains each step and the other charges.

Source: IDP's Ludhiana test centre page, checked on 10 November 2026. Venues can change.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best IELTS coaching in Ludhiana?
Our pick is PrepEve. Ludhiana has many institutes, but a band moves only when someone corrects your own work. PrepEve's trainers do that in live online classes: they find your weak skill, mark every essay and take your speaking mocks 1-on-1. The first assessment class is free.
Is there IELTS coaching near me in Model Town or Dugri?
There are many, but you don't have to travel to one. PrepEve's classes are live on video, so you join from Model Town, Dugri, BRS Nagar or any other part of Ludhiana and still get personal feedback from a trainer.
Does PrepEve have an IELTS centre in Ludhiana?
No. PrepEve has no branch in Ludhiana or anywhere else, because every class is online. The test is separate: you sit it at IDP's centre in Model Town.
Where is the IELTS test centre in Ludhiana?
IDP's computer-delivered centre is in Tej Complex on Link Road, Model Town, opposite Preet Palace Cinema. IDP also has an office in Feroze Gandhi Market, so follow the venue on your booking confirmation. Checked on IDP's site on 10 November 2026.
Is the IELTS Speaking test in Ludhiana face to face?
IDP's Ludhiana page says Speaking is face to face with a certified examiner, while Listening, Reading and Writing are on computer. Some IDP pages describe a video-call Speaking test instead, so check your booking confirmation.
Does Canada accept IELTS One Skill Retake?
Not for Express Entry. IDP offers One Skill Retake at its Ludhiana centre, but IRCC does not accept those results for Canada PR. If one skill falls short, you sit the full General Training test again.
