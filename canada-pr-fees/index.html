Canada PR Fees in 2026: What Express Entry Costs

For Express Entry, IRCC charges CAD 1,590 for the principal applicant (a CAD 990 processing fee plus the CAD 600 right of permanent residence fee), the same for a spouse or partner, and CAD 270 for each dependent child. Biometrics cost CAD 85 per person, or CAD 170 for a family applying together. These fees apply to applications received from 30 April 2026. You also pay for a language test, an ECA, a medical exam and police certificates.

Each adult who takes a language test pays the test fee, and every retake costs the full fee again. A spouse’s test and ECA are optional, but they can add CRS points.

When do you pay?

Creating an Express Entry profile is free. You pay the processing fees and the biometrics fee online when you submit your PR application after an invitation. You can pay the RPRF with your application or later, when IRCC asks for it.

Can you get a refund?

The RPRF is refunded if you withdraw your application or it is refused. It is the only fee IRCC can refund once processing has started, so check every document before you submit.

How to keep your total cost down

  • Sit your language test when you are ready. A retake costs ₹19,000 again, and missing your target in one skill can mean a second test.
  • Get your ECA early. It stays valid for five years, so one report can cover your whole time in the pool.
  • Request police certificates early. A missing document can get your application refused, and the processing fee is not refunded.
  • Check IRCC’s fee list on the day you pay, in case fees change again.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Canada PR cost from India?
IRCC’s fees are CAD 1,675 for a single applicant and CAD 3,350 for a couple, including biometrics. Add your language test (₹19,000 for IELTS in India), an ECA, a medical exam and police certificates.
Did Canada PR fees increase in 2026?
Yes. IRCC raised permanent residence fees on 30 April 2026. The right of permanent residence fee went from CAD 575 to CAD 600.
Do children pay the right of permanent residence fee?
No. Dependent children pay only the CAD 270 processing fee.
Is the Canada PR fee refundable?
Only the right of permanent residence fee, if you withdraw or your application is refused. The processing fee is not refunded once processing has started.
Is creating an Express Entry profile free?
Yes. You pay IRCC only when you submit your PR application after an invitation.
Do I pay for biometrics again for my PR application?
IRCC asks people aged 14 to 79 to give biometrics for every permanent residence application, so plan for the biometrics fee.
