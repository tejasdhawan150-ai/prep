IELTS Exam Fee and Booking in India (2026)

The IELTS exam fee in India for 2026 is ₹19,000 for IELTS Academic or General Training, the same at every test centre. You book and pay with IDP, which runs every IELTS test in India: register on its website with your valid passport, choose the test, city, centre and date, and pay online. IELTS exam fee in India (2026)

IDP lists these fees for 2026. They are the same in every city, from Delhi, Mumbai and Bengaluru to Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune and Ahmedabad, and the same whether you take the test on computer or, where it is still offered, on paper.

IELTS exam fees listed by IDP India for 2026
TestFee
IELTS Academic₹19,000
IELTS General Training₹19,000
IELTS on Computer for UKVI₹19,250
IELTS Life Skills (A1 and B1)₹18,000
One Skill Retake (one section, IELTS on computer)₹12,650

Academic and General Training cost the same, so choose by what your university, employer or visa asks for, not by price. IDP’s help page says the fee includes all taxes and that you can ask for a GST-compliant bill. IDP also says fees can change, so check the amount on its booking page before you pay.

What the exam fee covers

  • All four parts of the test: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking. Speaking is a conversation with an examiner.
  • Your result, the Test Report Form, which you can download from your candidate login.
  • Sending your result electronically to up to five organisations, such as universities or employers.

Other IELTS charges: transfer, cancellation, re-mark and retake

The exam fee is only the first cost. These are the other charges IDP India lists:

Cancellations: if you cancel early enough, IDP refunds the fee minus an administration charge of up to 25%. Close to the test date the fee is not refunded, except in serious cases such as a documented medical emergency.

Deadlines: IDP’s terms and conditions ask for transfer and cancellation requests at least 5 weeks before your test date, while some of its other pages give shorter deadlines. Ask as early as you can, and check the current terms in your candidate login before you request a change.

What IELTS really costs: plan for the total

Most people budget for one test. Plan for what happens if one skill falls short of your target:

IELTS exam costs at 2026 IDP India fees
What happensTotal paid to IDP
You reach your score in one attempt₹19,000
One skill falls short and you use One Skill Retake₹31,650 (₹19,000 + ₹12,650)
You sit the full test a second time₹38,000 (₹19,000 × 2)
You ask for a re-mark and your band does not change₹33,250 (₹19,000 + ₹14,250)

Check before you rely on One Skill Retake: not every organisation accepts it. IRCC does not accept One Skill Retake results for Express Entry, so if you need IELTS for Canada PR and one skill falls short, you sit the full General Training test again.

The cheapest plan is to book only when your timed practice scores are already at your target in every skill. Coaching fees are separate from the exam fee and are paid to the coaching provider, not to IDP.

How to pay the IELTS fee

  • Pay online when you book on IDP’s website, by debit card, credit card or net banking.
  • If you book in person at an IDP branch, the branch tells you how to pay.
  • Keep the payment confirmation and booking email. You may need them for a transfer, a refund or a GST bill.
  • IDP’s fee page lists one fee for every candidate. It does not list concessions, for example by category or for students.

Before you book: three decisions

Most booking problems start before the booking form. Settle these three things first, because you cannot change the test type once you have sat it.

1. Which IELTS test do you need?

Which IELTS test to book
Your goalBook this test
Canada PR (Express Entry) and most migrationIELTS General Training
University or college admission abroadIELTS Academic
A UK visa route that asks for a Secure English Language TestIELTS for UKVI (Academic or General Training, as your route requires)
Professional registration (nursing, medicine and others)Usually IELTS Academic; check your regulator

If you are not sure, check the official page of the organisation you are applying to. It will name the exact test it accepts. Booking General Training for a university that wants Academic means paying for a second test.

2. Are you ready to sit it?

Take at least one full, timed practice test under exam conditions before you pay. If your practice scores are not yet at your target in every skill, book a date that gives you enough weeks to close the gap. A test booked too early is the most common way people end up paying the fee twice.

3. What is your deadline?

Work backwards from the date your result is needed: a university application, a visa lodgement or an Express Entry profile. Leave time for the result to arrive, and ideally for one retake in case a single skill falls short.

How to book IELTS: step by step

  1. Check your passport. You need a valid passport to register in India. Make sure it will still be valid on your test day, and enter your name exactly as it appears on it.
  2. Go to IDP’s booking site. Register online at ieltsidpindia.com. IDP also accepts bookings at its branches if you prefer to book in person.
  3. Choose the test and format. Select Academic, General Training or IELTS for UKVI. Check which formats your centre offers: IDP India now promotes IELTS on computer, and some of its pages say the paper test has been discontinued.
  4. Pick your city, centre and date. IDP runs test centres in cities across India. Popular dates and weekend slots fill up first, so book early if you need a specific date.
  5. Upload your passport copy and fill in your details. Your details appear on your result, so check spellings, date of birth and passport number carefully before you submit.
  6. Pay the fee. Pay ₹19,000 (₹19,250 for UKVI) online by debit card, credit card or net banking. Keep the payment confirmation.
  7. Check your confirmation email. It shows your test date, reporting time and venue. Confirm your Speaking slot: depending on the centre, Speaking can be on the same day as the other three parts or on a different day close to it.

If you need special arrangements because of a disability or medical condition, tell IDP when you book, so the centre has time to arrange it.

IELTS on computer or on paper?

The questions, timing, scoring and fee are the same in both formats. The differences are practical:

  • On computer you type your Writing answers and read on screen. Results usually come faster, within a few days.
  • On paper you write by hand. Where it is still offered, results take longer.
  • The Speaking test is a conversation with an examiner in both formats.

If you type quickly and are comfortable reading on screen, computer is usually the easier choice. If your handwriting is fast and clear and you prefer paper, check whether your centre still offers it. One Skill Retake is only available after IELTS on computer.

What to bring and expect on test day

  • Your original passport. It must be the same passport you used to book. Without it you will be refused entry.
  • Arrive early. Your confirmation email gives the reporting time. Check-in includes identity checks and a photo.
  • Personal items such as phones and bags are not allowed in the test room. The centre will tell you where to leave them.
  • Listening, Reading and Writing are taken together, with no breaks between them. Speaking is separate and takes 11–14 minutes.

When should you book?

  • Book once your timed practice tests are consistently at your target in every skill.
  • Leave room for one retake before your deadline, and remember that IRCC needs a full test, not a One Skill Retake.
  • Allow for result time, and for any extra days the organisation needs to verify your result.

Common booking mistakes

  • Booking Academic when you need General Training for Canada PR, or the other way round.
  • A name, date of birth or passport number that does not match the passport exactly.
  • A passport that expires before the test date.
  • Booking regular IELTS when your UK visa route needs IELTS for UKVI.
  • Choosing a date that leaves no time for a retake before your deadline.

Booking checklist

  1. Confirm which test your university, employer or visa needs.
  2. Check your passport is valid on the test date.
  3. Take a full timed practice test and compare with your target.
  4. Choose the format, city, centre and date.
  5. Enter your details exactly as on your passport, and pay.
  6. Save the confirmation email and note your Speaking slot.

Frequently asked questions

What is the IELTS exam fee in India in 2026?
IDP lists ₹19,000 for IELTS Academic and General Training, ₹19,250 for IELTS on Computer for UKVI and ₹18,000 for IELTS Life Skills. Check the booking page before you pay, as fees can change.
Is the IELTS fee the same in every city?
Yes. IDP lists the same fee at all its test centres in India.
Do IELTS Academic and General Training cost the same?
Yes, both cost ₹19,000. Choose the one your university, employer or visa asks for. Canada PR needs General Training.
Does the IELTS fee include GST?
IDP’s help page says the ₹19,000 fee includes all taxes and that you can ask IDP for a GST-compliant bill.
How do I book the IELTS exam in India?
Register online on IDP’s website with your valid passport, choose the test, format, city, centre and date, and pay the fee. IDP runs all IELTS tests in India.
Can I book IELTS with British Council in India?
No. IDP bought the British Council’s IELTS business in India in 2021, so all IELTS tests in India are booked with IDP.
How much does it cost to change my IELTS date?
IDP’s fee page lists ₹4,750 for IELTS on computer and ₹4,850 for IELTS for UKVI. Its terms ask for at least 5 weeks’ notice, and some IDP pages give shorter deadlines, so check your candidate login and ask early.
Is the IELTS fee refundable?
Partly, if you cancel early enough: IDP keeps an administration charge of up to 25%. Close to the test date the fee is not refunded, except in serious cases such as a documented medical emergency.
How much is an IELTS re-mark in India?
IDP India lists ₹14,250, or ₹14,400 for IELTS for UKVI. The fee is refunded if your band goes up.
Can I book IELTS without a passport?
No. You need a valid passport to register in India, and you must bring the same original passport on test day.
How soon do I get IELTS results?
For IELTS on computer, IDP says results typically come within a few days. Paper results take longer.
