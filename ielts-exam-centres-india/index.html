IDP runs computer-delivered IELTS centres across India. We have checked 26 cities so far, from Delhi NCR (five centres) and Mumbai (four) to single centres in cities like Bhopal, Kochi and Rajkot. Use the address on your booking confirmation: IDP's own pages sometimes show older addresses, and several cities also have IDP counselling offices that are not test venues.

Which IELTS centre should you choose?

The nearest one that offers the test you need: General Training for Canada PR, Academic for university admission, or IELTS for UKVI for some UK visas. A few more points:

  • One Skill Retake is offered at many centres, but IRCC does not accept it for Canada PR, so plan for a full General Training test if you need it for Express Entry.
  • Accessibility: IDP points candidates who need better access to centres such as Chandigarh Sector 34 and Karnal, and lists limited access at some others. Tell IDP about any needs when you book.
  • Counselling offices are not test venues: in Bhopal, Rajkot, Dehradun and Bathinda, IDP has a separate office from its test centre.
  • Weekend dates fill first at busy centres, so a weekday is often easier to get.

How much does IELTS cost at Indian centres?

The same at every centre: ₹19,000 for IELTS Academic or General Training in 2026, and ₹19,250 for IELTS on computer for UKVI. See how to book the IELTS exam for the steps, date changes and re-marks, and IELTS exam dates for how often tests run.

Frequently asked questions

Who conducts IELTS in India?
IDP. Every IELTS test in India is run by IDP, so you book on IDP's website with your passport.
How many IELTS centres are there in Delhi NCR?
IDP lists five: Nehru Place, Netaji Subhash Place, Rajouri Garden, Noida Sector 18 and Gurugram Sector 26, all close to a metro station.
Where are the IELTS centres in Mumbai?
IDP lists Andheri East (Chakala), Lower Parel, Thane (Wagle Estate) and CBD Belapur in Navi Mumbai. Pune has two more, in Shivajinagar and on Boat Club Road.
Is there an IELTS exam centre in Mohali?
We found no IDP test centre listed in Mohali. The nearest is Chandigarh Sector 34, which IDP also describes as its more accessible centre.
Where is the IELTS centre in Kolkata?
IDP lists a computer-delivered centre at PS Arcadia Centrum on Park Street. Confirm the venue on your booking confirmation.
Is the IELTS fee different at different centres?
No. In 2026 IDP charges ₹19,000 for IELTS Academic or General Training at every Indian centre, and ₹19,250 for IELTS on computer for UKVI.
Which IELTS centres in India offer IELTS for UKVI?
Most centres in our table do, including both Chandigarh centres, both Chennai centres, Ahmedabad, Jaipur and Lucknow. Check the UKVI option for your venue when you book.
Can I use One Skill Retake for Canada PR?
No. IRCC does not accept IELTS One Skill Retake for Express Entry, so if one skill falls short you need a full General Training test.
