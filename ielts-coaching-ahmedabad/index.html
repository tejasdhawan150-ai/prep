Why are Ahmedabad learners choosing online IELTS coaching?

Ahmedabad runs a long way, from the old city across the Sabarmati to SG Highway, and then on to Gandhinagar. An evening class at the wrong end of that line can take up most of the evening.

Many learners here are planning a degree abroad and need a strong Writing band. Writing only improves when someone marks your own essays and shows you the fix. Online, you get that marking without the journey.

Why people in Ahmedabad take IELTS

For most learners in Ahmedabad the goal is a university abroad. The country you choose changes the test more than people expect.

  • The UK: universities set their own IELTS Academic scores. Some visa routes need IELTS for UKVI instead, which IDP's Ahmedabad centre offers. Check the UK English requirements before you book.
  • Canada, Australia or the USA: usually IELTS Academic for admission. See the guides for Canada, Australia and the USA.
  • PR: General Training for Canada. For Australia, many applicants weigh IELTS against PTE: see the PTE score for Australia PR.

If your university accepts more than one test, compare the English tests before you choose.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best IELTS coaching in Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar?
Our pick is PrepEve. Its coaching is live and online, so nobody in Ahmedabad or Gandhinagar has to travel. Each essay you write is marked by a trainer, speaking mocks are 1-on-1, and your weakest skill gets the most attention. The assessment class at the start is free.
Is there IELTS coaching near me in Satellite or Gandhinagar?
PrepEve's class is on your screen, so it is equally near in Satellite, Bopal, Maninagar or Gandhinagar. What you keep is the trainer feedback; what you drop is the commute.
Does PrepEve have an IELTS centre in Ahmedabad?
No. PrepEve has no office in Ahmedabad or Gandhinagar. Classes are online. Booking the IELTS test is a separate step, done with IDP.
Where is the IELTS test centre in Ahmedabad?
IDP lists its centre on the 1st floor of Addor Aspire, beside Jhanvi Restaurant, in the Gujarat University area. The computer test is held there, and so is IELTS for UKVI. As listed by IDP on 10 November 2026; your booking confirmation has the final address.
Is there an IELTS test centre in Gandhinagar?
IDP has a Gandhinagar page, but it gives no street address and lists Ahmedabad among the nearby centres. Check the booking site for your dates. If no Gandhinagar session shows, the Ahmedabad centre is the one to book.
Do UK universities need IELTS for UKVI?
Not always. Each UK university sets its own rule, and many accept ordinary IELTS Academic for admission. IELTS for UKVI is for visa routes that require a Secure English Language Test. Ask your university which one it wants before you pay.
