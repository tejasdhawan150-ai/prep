Ontario PNP (OINP) 2026: The New Ontario Workforce Priority Stream

In 2026 Ontario closed all eight of its old OINP streams, including Human Capital Priorities, and replaced them with one Ontario Workforce Priority stream. It is built around a full-time, permanent job offer from an Ontario employer: the employer registers the job first, and you then have 30 days to register an expression of interest. Jobs in TEER 0 to 3 need CLB 6 (CLB 5 for some occupations) and a post-secondary credential; TEER 4 and 5 jobs need CLB 4. Without a job offer, the only route is for self-employed physicians. TEER 0 to 3 applicants can choose to be nominated through Express Entry, which adds 600 CRS points.

What changed in the OINP in 2026

A regulation change on 26 June 2026 removed Ontario's eight existing streams, such as Human Capital Priorities, French-Speaking Skilled Worker, the Employer Job Offer streams and the Master's and PhD Graduate streams, and introduced the Ontario Workforce Priority stream. Ontario said its expression of interest portal for the new stream opened on 4 August 2026. People who already held a notification of interest under a former stream could still apply under the old rules.

The big change for applicants in India: Ontario no longer selects people from the Express Entry pool without a job offer, as Human Capital Priorities used to. You now need an Ontario employer first.

Self-employed physicians in good standing with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario, who can bill through OHIP, can apply without a job offer. An LMIA is not required, but an employer without one must show reasonable recruitment efforts.

How the new OINP process works

  1. Your employer registers in the OINP Employer Portal and submits the job offer, and pays CAD 1,500 (outside the Greater Toronto Area) or CAD 2,000 (inside it).
  2. You get a job offer ID by email and have 30 days to register your expression of interest, or the offer expires.
  3. If you are invited, your employer has 14 days to submit the application and documents through the portal.
  4. After a nomination, you apply to IRCC for PR. After a nomination through an Express Entry-linked stream, you accept it in your Express Entry profile and get 600 CRS points. With a base (non-Express Entry) nomination, you apply to IRCC for PR separately. Either way, IRCC makes the final decision.

Your language score still matters

CLB 6 is the floor for most skilled jobs, and a higher score helps both in Express Entry and with employers. See CLB 7 in IELTS and our IELTS to CLB calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the full form of OINP?
OINP stands for Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program, Ontario's provincial nominee program for permanent residence.
Is the OINP Human Capital Priorities stream still open?
No. Ontario closed Human Capital Priorities and its other old streams in 2026 and replaced them with the Ontario Workforce Priority stream, which needs a job offer.
Can I apply to the Ontario PNP without a job offer?
Under the 2026 stream, only self-employed physicians can apply without a job offer. Everyone else needs a full-time, permanent job offer from an Ontario employer.
What CLB do I need for the OINP?
CLB 6 for jobs in TEER 0 to 3 (CLB 5 for certain occupations), and CLB 4 for jobs in TEER 4 or 5.
Are there still OINP draws?
Ontario invites candidates from its expression of interest pool for the new stream. Check Ontario's invitations page for the latest rounds.
Does an Ontario nomination add CRS points?
Yes, if you have a TEER 0 to 3 job offer and choose the Express Entry option: a provincial nomination adds 600 CRS points.
