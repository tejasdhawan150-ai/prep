Why are Karnal and Kurukshetra learners choosing online IELTS coaching?

Along the GT Road, IELTS is a normal step after school or college, and it is common to travel to the next town for a class: Kurukshetra to Karnal, Kaithal to Kurukshetra, Panipat to Karnal. Each of those is a round trip of an hour or more.

With live online coaching nobody travels. The class, the marked essays and the speaking practice reach you at home, in whichever town that is.

Joining from across Karnal & Kurukshetra

Karnal

In Karnal you are in the same town as the test centre, so only the classes need planning. An evening batch at home saves the drive across town after college or work, and your essay comes back marked.

Kurukshetra and Kurukshetra University

At Kurukshetra University or NIT Kurukshetra, you can join from a hostel room after lectures. IDP's page for Kurukshetra gives no venue of its own and lists Karnal as the nearby centre, under an hour away by road.

Panipat and Kaithal

Panipat and Kaithal do not appear on IDP's list of test centres as far as we could see, so the test means a trip to Karnal either way. Keeping the classes online makes that the only trip.

Why people in Karnal & Kurukshetra take IELTS

Canada comes first for most families here, then Australia and the UK. What you plan to do there decides the version of IELTS.

  • A college course in Canada: usually IELTS Academic, at the score the college sets. Study in Canada to PR shows how the route continues after graduation.
  • Canada PR from India: IELTS General Training. Start with IELTS for Canada PR, then check your eligibility with the 67-point calculator.
  • Australia: IELTS and PTE Academic are both accepted, and the score for each level differs: see PTE vs IELTS.
