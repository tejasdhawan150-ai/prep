IELTS for PR from British Columbia

If you are already in Canada, your route to PR may be Express Entry (for example the Canadian Experience Class) or British Columbia's provincial nominee program (BC PNP). Both use CLB levels from an approved test such as IELTS General Training, and minimums vary by stream. A higher score can make you more competitive, so check the current requirements for your stream on the official program website.

Applying for citizenship later? Adults aged 18 to 54 also need to show at least CLB 4 in speaking and listening.

What IELTS score do you need?

IELTS General Training scores needed for each CLB level (IRCC)
CLBListeningReadingWritingSpeaking
CLB 108.58.07.57.5
CLB 98.07.07.07.0
CLB 87.56.56.56.5
CLB 76.06.06.06.0

For Express Entry, IELTS 8777 (CLB 9) is the level where language points jump. Listening needs 8.0, so it is usually the skill to fix first.

Which IELTS test do I need for PR?
IELTS General Training. IRCC does not accept IELTS Academic for Express Entry or provincial nominee applications.
What score do I need for CLB 9?
In IELTS General Training, CLB 9 means Listening 8.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 7.0 and Speaking 7.0, often written as 8777.
