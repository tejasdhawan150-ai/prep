New TOEFL iBT in 2026: What Changed

ETS updated the TOEFL iBT on 21 January 2026. Reading and Listening are now adaptive, the tasks are new and shorter, and scores are reported on a 1 to 6 scale in half points, aligned with the CEFR. Until January 2028, your score report also shows a comparable score on the old 0 to 120 scale. Scores now arrive in about 72 hours.

What changed in the TOEFL iBT?

  • Adaptive Reading and Listening. Each section has two stages, and the second stage adjusts to how you did in the first.
  • New task types in all four sections, with more short, everyday tasks.
  • Newer topics, including texts like magazine and website excerpts.
  • A new score scale from 1 to 6, aligned with the CEFR.
  • Faster results: about 72 hours, against 4 to 8 days before.

The new 1–6 score scale

Each section gets a score from 1 to 6 in half points, and your overall score is the average of the four, rounded to the nearest half point. Each level lines up with the CEFR:

What the changes mean for you

  • Old scores still count for two years from your test date.
  • Check your university’s requirement on the scale it now uses, 1–6 or 0–120.
  • Use 2026 practice material. Older books and tests use the old task types.
  • Do not ease off early. In adaptive sections, the first stage decides which questions you get next.

Is the new TOEFL easier?

It is shorter, and results come faster, but it still tests the same four skills, and the adaptive sections adjust to your level. Prepare with the new task types and timed practice, just as you would for any version of the test.

Frequently asked questions

When did the TOEFL change?
On 21 January 2026.
What is the new TOEFL score scale?
1 to 6 in half points, for each section and overall, aligned with the CEFR. A comparable 0–120 score is shown until January 2028.
What is 5 in the new TOEFL?
C1 on the CEFR, comparable to 95 or more on the old scale and to IELTS 7.0.
How long is the new TOEFL test?
About two hours including setup. The four sections take roughly an hour and a half.
Are old TOEFL scores still valid?
Yes, for two years from the test date.
How soon do TOEFL scores come now?
About 72 hours after the test.
