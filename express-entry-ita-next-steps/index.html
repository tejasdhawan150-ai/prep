Express Entry Invitation to Apply (ITA): What to Do in the Next 60 Days

After an Express Entry invitation to apply (ITA), you have 60 days to submit your complete permanent residence application online, or to decline the invitation. Recheck your CRS score first: if your details have changed and your score is now below the cut-off for your round, decline. If you neither apply nor decline in time, the invitation expires and your profile leaves the pool. IRCC says it processes most complete Express Entry applications in 6 months or less.

First, check before you start

  • Recalculate your CRS score with your details as they are today. A new job, an expired test, a change in marital status or a new child can all change it. If your score is now below the lowest score in your round, IRCC advises you to decline. Use the CRS calculator.
  • Check your language test dates. Your result must still be less than 2 years old on the day you submit. If it will expire, IRCC suggests you can decline, retest and come back to the pool. See IELTS validity for Canada PR.
  • Check your ECA. It must be less than 5 years old.
  • Check the program you were invited under. It decides whether you need proof of funds.

Police certificates often take the longest. You need one for each country where you spent 6 months or more in a row since age 18. In India you apply through the passport office (Passport Seva), or an embassy, consulate or BLS centre if you live abroad. See the Canada PR documents checklist.

Documents IRCC usually asks for

IRCC builds a personal checklist in your account from your answers, and that list is the final word. Most applicants from India need: passport pages, language test results and ECA, a reference letter for every job you claim, proof of funds if your program needs it, police certificates, the medical exam confirmation, a digital photo, and documents for your spouse and children.

Reference letters are where many problems start. Each one should be on company letterhead and show your job title, duties, dates, hours per week and salary, and the duties should match the occupation code (NOC) in your profile.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Submitting with a language test result that has expired, even by a day.
  • Reference letters that do not show hours, salary or duties, or duties that do not match your NOC.
  • Missing a police certificate for a country where you lived for 6 months or more.
  • Proof of funds below IRCC's amount for your family size, or money borrowed for the purpose.
  • Leaving out a family member. Your spouse and dependent children must be declared even if they are not moving with you.
  • Any detail that is wrong or missing. IRCC can treat false or misleading information as misrepresentation, which can mean a refusal and a ban of at least 5 years.

What happens after you submit

  1. Acknowledgement of receipt (AOR). IRCC confirms your application is complete. An incomplete application is rejected and the processing fee is refunded.
  2. Biometrics. If you are 14 to 79, IRCC sends a letter and you have 30 days to give your fingerprints and photo.
  3. Background and medical checks, and any requests for more documents. Answer these by the date IRCC gives.
  4. Decision. If you are approved, you receive your confirmation of permanent residence and instructions for landing.

You pay the processing fees and the biometrics fee when you submit. The right of permanent residence fee can be paid with the application or later, when IRCC asks for it. See Canada PR fees.

Frequently asked questions

How long do I have to apply after an Express Entry ITA?
60 days from the date of the invitation. You must submit your complete application, or decline the invitation, within that time. If you do neither, the invitation expires and your profile is removed from the pool.
What happens if I decline the invitation?
Your profile goes back into the pool. IRCC says declining does not affect whether you are invited again, but you will need to be invited in a later round, and your score must still be competitive.
What if my IELTS expires during the 60 days?
Your result must still be valid on the day you submit. If it will expire first, IRCC suggests you can decline the invitation, take a new test and update your profile.
Do I need a medical exam before I apply?
Yes. For Express Entry, you get an upfront medical exam from an IRCC-approved panel physician after your invitation and before you apply, and upload the confirmation with your application.
When do I pay the Canada PR fees?
You pay the processing fees and the biometrics fee online when you submit your application. The right of permanent residence fee can be paid then or later, when IRCC asks for it.
How long does PR take after I submit?
IRCC says it processes most complete Express Entry applications in 6 months or less, including the time to give biometrics. Check IRCC's processing times page for the latest figure.
Can a consultant help after I have been invited?
