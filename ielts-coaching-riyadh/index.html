Why people in Riyadh take IELTS

  • Canada PR: Express Entry needs IELTS General Training or another approved test, and each skill is converted to a CLB level.
  • UK visas: some routes need IELTS for UKVI, a Secure English Language Test. See IELTS for the UK.
  • Australia and New Zealand: skilled visas set minimum IELTS scores for each skill. See IELTS for Australia and IELTS for New Zealand.
  • Study abroad: universities usually ask for IELTS Academic, with an overall band and a minimum in each skill.
  • Nursing registration abroad: regulators usually ask for IELTS Academic with 7.0 in most skills. See IELTS for nurses.

Check which version you need before you book. General Training and Academic share the Listening and Speaking tests but have different Reading and Writing papers.

How do you book IELTS in Riyadh?

In Riyadh, IELTS is run by both the British Council and IDP, so you can book with either; it is the same test. Venues are spread across the city, so check the booking pages for the one nearest you.

IELTS fees in Saudi Arabia are charged in Saudi riyals (SAR). The price depends on the venue and the test you choose (Academic, General Training or IELTS for UKVI), and it can change, so check it on the British Council or IDP booking page when you book. IELTS on computer and IELTS on paper are offered at some venues, so check the format as well.

  • Results: for IELTS on computer, results usually come within a few days. IELTS on paper takes about 13 days.
  • One Skill Retake: some venues offer it for IELTS on computer, but Canada does not accept it for PR, so plan for a full test if you need IELTS for Express Entry.
  • Your ID: book with the same name and ID details you will show on test day.

IELTS scores for Canada PR from Riyadh

Express Entry converts each IELTS General Training skill to a CLB level. These are the minimum bands for each level:

IELTS General Training scores needed for each CLB level (IRCC)
CLBListeningReadingWritingSpeaking
CLB 108.58.07.57.5
CLB 98.07.07.07.0
CLB 87.56.56.56.5
CLB 76.06.06.06.0

For Express Entry, IELTS 8777 (CLB 9) is the level where language points jump. Listening needs 8.0, so it is usually the skill to fix first. Your results must be less than 2 years old when you create your profile and when you apply.
