Why are Amritsar learners choosing online IELTS coaching?

Amritsar has no shortage of IELTS institutes, so finding a class is not the hard part. The hard part is finding one where somebody corrects your own essays and your own speaking. The roads around the old city are slow as well, and learners from Tarn Taran or Batala lose a good part of the day getting in and out.

A live class on your phone or laptop removes the journey and keeps the correction. You send in your writing, a trainer marks it, and the next session picks up from what went wrong.

Joining from across Amritsar

Ranjit Avenue, Lawrence Road and Green Avenue

These central areas have many coaching options, and the test is here too: IDP holds it in B-Block of Ranjit Avenue, opposite the BSNL office. Living close to an institute matters less than the feedback you get there, so ask any class how often a trainer marks your work.

Students at GNDU and city colleges

At Guru Nanak Dev University, Khalsa College or DAV College, lectures and practicals take up the day. An evening class from your hostel or home comes after them, and weekend batches cover the weeks when exams are near.

Tarn Taran, Batala and nearby towns

From Tarn Taran, Batala, Ajnala or Majitha, a class in Amritsar means a bus or a drive in both directions. Online, the only journey left is the one on test day.

Why people in Amritsar take IELTS

Most learners here are aiming at Canada, the UK or Australia. The country and the visa decide the test, so check before you pay for anything.

  • A Canadian college or university: usually IELTS Academic, at whatever score the institution asks for. Now that the SDS route is closed, there is no single visa score to aim at. Our guide to the IELTS score for a Canada study permit explains.
  • Canada PR: IELTS General Training. Your four bands turn into CLB levels, and the IELTS to CLB calculator shows yours.
  • The UK: universities choose their own scores, and some visa routes need IELTS for UKVI, which the Amritsar centre offers. Read IELTS for the UK first.
  • Australia: IELTS and PTE Academic are both accepted. See IELTS for Australia.
