IELTS Band Descriptors Explained Simply

Examiners score IELTS Writing and Speaking on four criteria each, worth 25% apiece. This page explains, in plain English, what band 6 and band 7 look like for each criterion, so you can see exactly what to improve.

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Written by the PrepEve team

Writing: the four criteria

Each criterion is worth 25% of your Writing score.

CriterionBand 6 (simplified)Band 7 (simplified)
Task Response / AchievementAnswers the question, but some ideas are not fully developed or the position is unclear in places.Answers all parts with a clear position throughout; main ideas are extended and supported.
Coherence & CohesionOrganised, but linking can be mechanical or overused.Logical progression, clear paragraphs, a range of linking used appropriately.
Lexical ResourceEnough vocabulary for the task; some errors in word choice or spelling.Some less common words with awareness of style and collocation; occasional errors.
Grammar Range & AccuracyMix of simple and complex sentences; errors are noticeable but rarely block meaning.A variety of complex structures; many error-free sentences.

Speaking: the four criteria

CriterionBand 6 (simplified)Band 7 (simplified)
Fluency & CoherenceWilling to speak at length, but some repetition, self-correction or hesitation.Speaks at length without noticeable effort; some hesitation to search for ideas, not words.
Lexical ResourceWide enough vocabulary to discuss topics; some inappropriate choices.Uses vocabulary flexibly, including some less common words and idioms.
Grammar Range & AccuracyMix of simple and complex forms with frequent mistakes in complex ones.Range of complex structures used with some flexibility; frequent error-free sentences.
PronunciationGenerally understood; some mispronunciation reduces clarity at times.Easy to understand throughout; uses features like stress and intonation effectively.

This is a simplified summary to help you understand the jump from 6 to 7. Read the official public band descriptors on ielts.org.

How to use the descriptors

  • Find the criterion that holds you back. Most 6.5 essays fail on Task Response (ideas not developed) or Grammar accuracy, not vocabulary.
  • Your overall band is the average. One weak criterion can pull the whole skill down by 0.5.
  • Get marked against the criteria. Ask for feedback per criterion, not just a band.

Frequently asked questions

What are IELTS band descriptors?
The public criteria examiners use to score Writing and Speaking. Each skill has four criteria, each worth 25%.
What is the difference between band 6 and band 7 in Writing?
Mainly development and control: at 7, ideas are clearly extended and supported, linking is natural, and many sentences are error-free.
Where can I find the official descriptors?
IELTS publishes the public band descriptors on its official website, ielts.org.
