Before you book

Questions people ask first

How many CRS points does French actually add?

With NCLC 7 or higher in all four French skills, IRCC adds 50 points if your English test is CLB 5 or higher in all four skills, or 25 points if it isn't (or you have no English test). That's on top of any second-language points. NCLC 7 also makes you eligible for French-language category draws, which have closed far below general draws (as of July 2026).

I have zero French. Is this realistic?

Yes. Most of our French students start at absolute zero. A1 assumes no French, and instructions are in English until you don't need them.

How long will it take me to reach NCLC 7?

From zero, most working adults need 9–14 months at 4–5 hours a week, including exam prep. With some French already, it's shorter. You'll get your own estimate in the demo class.

I work full-time. Can I fit this in?

That's who the batches are built for: morning, evening and weekend slots, about 4–5 hours a week. Every class is recorded, so a late shift doesn't cost you a lesson.

TEF Canada or TCF Canada?

Both are accepted for Express Entry. The better choice depends on your deadline and the test dates available to you. We'll recommend one in your demo class. DELF is a lifetime certificate but is not the exam for Express Entry.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on your starting level and whether you choose group or one-to-one classes. Ask at the end of your demo class, or WhatsApp us for current prices.

Do you file immigration applications?

No. PrepEve teaches French and prepares you for the exam. Our founder is a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant, but PrepEve itself does not file applications. CRS rules and draw cutoffs are set by IRCC and change often, so always check the official IRCC website.
