The levels

A1 to B2, and what
each one actually gets you

French is graded on the CEFR scale. Every level unlocks something in daily life — and something on a visa file. Here's the whole ladder, both sides.

A1
Getting off zero ≈ 8–10 weeks
In lifeIntroduce yourself, order, ask directions, handle numbers, dates and small talk without freezing.
AbroadEnough to start a Quebec or France file with confidence, and to stop being scared of the exam format.
DELF A1
A2
Holding a conversation ≈ 10–12 weeks
In lifeTalk about your work, your past, your plans. Travel independently. Put "French — A2" on a CV and defend it in an interview.
AbroadClears the language bar for several visitor, spouse and lower-tier work routes, and for some school admissions.
DELF A2 · TCF
B1
Working in French ≈ 12–16 weeks
In lifeHandle meetings, emails and customer calls. This is the level most French-speaking job roles in India ask for.
AbroadMeets many French university entry requirements and several provincial and European work-permit thresholds.
DELF B1 · TEF / TCF
B2
The one that moves files ≈ 16–20 weeks
In lifeArgue, negotiate, present and follow fast native speech. Fluent enough to work and study entirely in French.
AbroadNCLC 7 sits here. All four skills at NCLC 7 unlocks Canada's French-category draws and up to 50 bonus CRS points, plus Quebec's PSTQ and French-taught degrees.
DELF B2 · TEF Canada · TCF Canada · NCLC 7
Honest timing: zero to B2 takes most working adults 9–14 months at 4–5 hours a week, including exam prep. Intensive batches compress it. Anyone promising B2 in eight weeks from scratch is selling you something.

Questions

The things people ask
before booking

Who is this course for?

Two groups, taught on the same ladder. People who want French for their career, college, travel or their own satisfaction — and people who need a certified level for Canada PR, Quebec, France, Belgium, Switzerland or a study or work visa. The grammar is identical; the practice tasks and exam prep are aimed at your goal.

Can I join as a complete beginner?

Yes, and most people do. A1 assumes zero French. Instructions are in English at the start and shift into French as you go. You'll be speaking simple sentences in the first fortnight.

Is this useful for Canada PR?

Directly. NCLC 7 in all four skills — roughly CEFR B2 — is the threshold for Canada's French-language category draws and for up to 50 additional CRS points when your English is CLB 5 or above. Those draws have consistently closed far below general draws. We prepare you for TEF Canada and TCF Canada specifically, not just "French".

What about Quebec?

Quebec now requires French across its economic immigration streams. The PSTQ's Stream 1 asks for level 7 in both speaking skills and level 5 in writing on the Quebec scale, with a lower bar for an accompanying spouse. We'll map your target level in the free session — and confirm current requirements with Quebec's official site, since they change.

Do you prepare for DELF?

Yes — DELF A1, A2, B1 and B2. DELF is a lifetime certificate from the French Ministry of Education and is the one to take if your goal is academic, professional or personal. If your goal is Canadian immigration, you want TEF or TCF Canada instead. We'll tell you which in the demo.

Is it useful for studying in France?

Yes. French-taught programmes usually ask for B2, and even English-taught ones go far more smoothly with B1. Public universities in France are dramatically cheaper than the English-speaking alternatives, which is the real reason B2 is worth the months.

How long does it take?

Each level is roughly 8–20 weeks depending on the rung — see the ladder above. Zero to B2 takes most working adults 9–14 months at 4–5 hours a week. Intensive batches are faster. We'd rather quote you an honest number than a marketable one.

Is it fully online?

Yes — live on video, from anywhere in India or overseas. Nothing is pre-recorded, though every class is recorded for you to revise.

What are the class timings?

Early morning, evening and weekend batches, so it fits around a job or college. You pick a slot when you enrol and can move batches if your schedule changes.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the level and whether you take group or one-to-one classes. We'll give you the exact figure on WhatsApp or at the end of your demo — no obligation, and no pressure to decide on the call.

What if I miss classes?

Every class is recorded and available the same week, and the doubt session catches you up. If life gets in the way for longer, you can move to a later batch at the same level.
