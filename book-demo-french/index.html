Questions

Asked before booking

How does French help my Canada PR?

NCLC 7 in all four skills — roughly CEFR B2 — is the threshold for Canada's French-language category draws and for up to 50 additional CRS points when your English is CLB 5 or above. Those draws have consistently closed far below general draws. We prepare you for TEF Canada and TCF Canada specifically.

Can I join as a complete beginner?

Yes, and most people do. A1 assumes zero French. Instructions are in English at the start and shift into French as you go.

How long does it take to reach NCLC 7?

Zero to B2 takes most working adults 9–14 months at 4–5 hours a week, including exam prep. Intensive batches are faster. We'd rather quote you an honest number than a marketable one.

TEF Canada or TCF Canada?

Both are used for Canadian immigration. Which one suits you depends on your pathway and deadline — we'll tell you in the demo. DELF is a lifetime certificate but is not the exam for Express Entry.

What about Quebec?

Quebec now requires French across its economic immigration streams. We'll map your target level in the free session — and confirm current requirements with Quebec's official site, since they change.

Is it fully online? What are the timings?

Yes — live on video from anywhere in India or overseas. Early morning, evening and weekend batches, and every class is recorded so you can catch up the same week.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the level and whether you take group or one-to-one classes. We'll give you the exact figure on WhatsApp or at the end of your demo — no obligation.
