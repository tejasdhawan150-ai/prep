IELTS General vs Academic for Canada PR

By the PrepEve team · Published 28 September 2026

If you are applying for Canada PR through Express Entry, you need IELTS General Training. This guide explains why, how IRCC turns your bands into CLB levels, and what else to check before you book.

Which IELTS test does Express Entry accept?

For Express Entry, IRCC accepts IELTS General Training. It does not accept IELTS Academic for this purpose. This applies to all three Express Entry programs: Federal Skilled Worker, Canadian Experience Class and Federal Skilled Trades.

Common mistake: people book Academic because a friend took it for study abroad. If you plan to apply for PR through Express Entry, book General Training.

When is Academic used?

Academic is mainly for university and college admission. If you plan to study in Canada first, your school may ask for Academic. That is a different requirement from PR.

How IRCC converts IELTS to CLB

IRCC does not use your IELTS bands directly. It converts each skill to a Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) level. Your CLB levels then decide your eligibility and part of your CRS points.

Each skill is converted on its own. You need at least the band shown in each skill to reach that CLB level in that skill.

IRCC can update these levels, so check the current language test equivalency chart on canada.ca before you apply.

How to read the table

Example: you score Listening 8.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 6.5, Speaking 7.0.

  • Listening 8.0 → CLB 9
  • Reading 7.0 → CLB 9
  • Writing 6.5 → CLB 8
  • Speaking 7.0 → CLB 9

So Writing is the skill holding you below CLB 9 in all four. Your overall IELTS band does not matter here. What matters is each skill.

Your CLB is typically judged per skill; many programs require a minimum in each. For example, Federal Skilled Worker needs at least CLB 7 in all four skills. Check the exact rule for your program on canada.ca.

Your results must be under 2 years old

IRCC only accepts language test results that are less than 2 years old. They must be valid when you create your Express Entry profile and also on the day you submit your PR application. Plan your test date with this in mind.

What about CELPIP?

CELPIP-General is the other English test IRCC accepts for Express Entry. It is a different test with its own CLB table.

Adding French: up to 50 extra CRS points

If you already have good English, French can help. Under IRCC rules, if you reach NCLC 7 in all four French skills, you can get up to 50 extra CRS points, depending on your English level.
