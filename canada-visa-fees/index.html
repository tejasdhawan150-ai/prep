Canada Visa Fees 2026: Visitor, Study, Work and PR (IRCC Fees)

IRCC charges its fees in Canadian dollars, paid online when you apply. In 2026, a visitor visa costs CAD 100, a study permit CAD 150, a work permit CAD 155 (CAD 255 for an open work permit) and biometrics CAD 85 per person. For permanent residence through Express Entry, each adult pays CAD 1,590 after the 30 April 2026 increase, and sponsoring a spouse costs CAD 1,260. Your bank converts the amount to rupees on the day you pay. Other costs, such as a language test or medical exam, are paid separately.

Permanent residence fees went up on 30 April 2026: the right of permanent residence fee rose from CAD 575 to 600, and the principal applicant processing fee from CAD 950 to 990. IRCC adjusts some fees regularly, so check the fee list before you pay.

Other costs to budget for

  • Language test: IELTS costs ₹19,000 and PTE ₹18,900 in India. See IELTS exam fee and PTE exam fee.
  • Education assessment (for PR): a WES ECA costs C$264 plus delivery and tax. See sending documents to WES from India.
  • Medical exam: paid to the panel physician, not IRCC. See the medical exam.
  • Police certificate: applied for through Passport Seva in India. See PCC for Canada PR.
  • Proof of funds: not a fee, but money you must show for some programs. See proof of funds.

How to pay IRCC fees from India

You pay online in Canadian dollars when you submit your application, usually by card. Your bank converts the amount to rupees and may add a foreign transaction charge. Keep the receipt and upload it if IRCC asks. For permanent residence, you can pay the right of permanent residence fee with your application or later, when IRCC asks.

Are IRCC fees refundable?

Processing fees are generally not refunded once IRCC has started processing, even if the application is refused. The right of permanent residence fee is refunded if you do not become a permanent resident. Check IRCC's refund rules for your case.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Canada visitor visa fee from India?
CAD 100 per person, plus CAD 85 for biometrics. A family of 5 or more applying together pays at most CAD 500 for visas and CAD 170 for biometrics.
What is the Canada study permit fee?
CAD 150, plus CAD 85 for biometrics.
What is the Canada work permit fee?
CAD 155 for a work permit. An open work permit, such as a PGWP or spouse open work permit, costs CAD 255 in total. Biometrics cost CAD 85.
How much are Canada PR fees in 2026?
For Express Entry or PNP, CAD 1,590 for the principal applicant, CAD 1,590 for a spouse or partner and CAD 270 for each dependent child, plus CAD 85 per person for biometrics.
How much does spousal sponsorship cost?
CAD 1,260 in IRCC fees from 30 April 2026, plus CAD 85 for biometrics.
Are Canada visa fees refundable if refused?
Processing fees are generally not refunded once processing has started. The right of permanent residence fee is refunded if you do not become a permanent resident.
