Why are Jaipur learners choosing online IELTS coaching?

Jaipur has grown far beyond the walled city. Mansarovar, Vaishali Nagar, Jagatpura and Sitapura are a long way from each other, so a class on the other side of town after college or work can take up most of an evening.

Online, the class starts when you open your laptop. The evening goes into timed practice and corrected essays, not Tonk Road traffic.

Joining from across Jaipur

Malviya Nagar, Tonk Road and Mansarovar

This side of the city is the closest to IDP's test centre, which is in Jaipur Centre Mall where Tonk Road meets the B2 Bypass. You prepare at home and make one short trip on test day.

Vaishali Nagar and C-Scheme

From Vaishali Nagar, Jhotwara or C-Scheme, an evening or weekend class from home saves a cross-city drive on class days. IDP has an office in C-Scheme too, but its listed test centre is the one on Tonk Road.

Students at universities and colleges across Jaipur

If you study at the University of Rajasthan, MNIT, Manipal University Jaipur or one of the Sitapura and Jagatpura colleges, pick a batch outside your lecture hours and join from your hostel or home.

Why people in Jaipur take IELTS

Start with the reason, because it decides the test.

  • A degree abroad after the University of Rajasthan, MNIT or Manipal University Jaipur: IELTS Academic, at the score your course asks for. We have guides for the USA, the UK and Ireland.
  • Nursing or medical registration abroad, for graduates of SMS Medical College and other colleges: usually IELTS Academic, so check your regulator. Nurses can compare countries in IELTS for nurses.
  • PR for Canada or Australia: General Training for Canada (see the language requirements). Australia also accepts PTE: see the English test for Australia PR.

Universities and visa offices often set a minimum in each skill, so one weak skill matters. The band calculator shows how your four scores combine.

No. PrepEve has no branch in Jaipur; all teaching is online. The IELTS test is a separate booking with IDP, at its centre on Tonk Road.
