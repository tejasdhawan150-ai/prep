Why are Mohali learners choosing online IELTS coaching?

Mohali is no longer one town. It runs from the older phases beside Chandigarh, through the IT City and Aerocity sectors along Airport Road, out to Kharar and Zirakpur. Many people here study or work in a different part of the Tricity, so a class after work can mean crossing two or three cities.

Joining live from home removes that. The time saved goes into mock tests, and nothing is lost on feedback: a trainer goes on correcting your essays and your speaking.

Joining from across Mohali

The older phases near Chandigarh

From Phase 3B2, Phase 7 or Phase 10, Chandigarh's coaching is close, but two short trips a day still add up over a month. An evening class at home, with your essay marked the same night, is easier to keep going. On test day, IDP's Sector 34 centre is a short drive away.

Kharar, Zirakpur and the new sectors

Kharar, Zirakpur and the Airport Road sectors are the far ends of the Tricity. Reaching a class in central Chandigarh from there takes the longest, so this is where studying online saves the most. Weekend batches help when the week is full.

Students at Chandigarh University and colleges around Landran

Chandigarh University at Gharuan and the college campuses at Landran are a long way from Sector 17 or Sector 34. An online batch fits after lectures, and you go into Chandigarh only for the test.

Also nearby: IELTS coaching in Chandigarh, Ambala, Patiala and Ludhiana.

How do you book the IELTS test near Mohali?

You book IELTS with IDP, which runs the test across India. IDP has a branch office in Phase 5, Mohali, but we did not find a test centre listed in Mohali itself. The two nearest centres are in Chandigarh:

IDP IELTS test centres nearest to Mohali (checked 10 November 2026)
CentreGood to know
Chandigarh, Sector 34The nearer one for most of Mohali. IDP describes it as the more accessible centre for candidates with disabilities.
Chandigarh, Sector 17In the city centre. IDP notes limited accessibility here.

Both centres test on computer, both are UKVI-approved and both list One Skill Retake. In 2026 the fee is ₹19,000. The steps are in how to book the IELTS exam.

Source: IDP's list of UKVI-approved test centres and its contact pages, checked on 10 November 2026. Venues can change.
