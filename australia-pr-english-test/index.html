What are Competent, Proficient and Superior English?

They are the English levels Home Affairs uses for skilled visas. According to the Department of Home Affairs, each level is defined by a minimum score on each accepted test.

Australian English levels and why they matter
LevelWhy it matters
Competent EnglishCommonly the minimum for skilled visa eligibility
Proficient EnglishA higher level that can add points in the skilled points test
Superior EnglishThe highest level, which can add more points

So your English score does two jobs. It can make you eligible, and a higher level can raise your points score. Points and cut-offs are set by Home Affairs and change; check the official visa page for your subclass.

Which English tests are accepted for Australia PR?

According to the Department of Home Affairs, several tests are accepted, including IELTS and PTE Academic. The Home Affairs English language page lists every accepted test and the score each needs for each level.

Check three details before you book:

  • The test version your visa accepts (for example, IELTS General Training or Academic).
  • How recent your results must be for your visa.
  • Whether your score must meet the minimum in each skill, not just overall.

What IELTS or PTE score do I need for Australia PR?

It depends on the English level you are aiming for. Home Affairs changed the score thresholds in 2024–25, so older blog posts and videos may show outdated numbers. We don't repeat them here.

Use the official Home Affairs English language page to find the current IELTS and PTE Academic scores for Competent, Proficient and Superior English. Then set your target one level above your minimum if the extra points matter to you.

When people search "PTE score for Australia", they usually mean the PTE Academic score that equals Proficient or Superior English. The idea is the same as for IELTS: find the level you need, then read that level's PTE score on the official page.

Should I take IELTS or PTE for Australia?

Both are accepted. Choose the one where you can reach your target level in every skill most reliably.

IELTS versus PTE Academic for Australia
IELTSPTE Academic
FormatPaper or computerComputer only
SpeakingFace to face with an examinerRecorded into a microphone
MarkingExaminers for Writing and SpeakingComputer-scored
  • Choose IELTS if you speak more naturally to a person, write better with time to plan, or already know the IELTS format.
  • Choose PTE Academic if you type quickly, are comfortable speaking to a microphone, and prefer short, fast tasks.

Frequently asked questions

Is IELTS accepted for Australia PR?
Yes. According to the Department of Home Affairs, IELTS is one of several accepted English tests for skilled visas.
Is PTE accepted for Australia PR?
Yes. PTE Academic is among the tests accepted by the Department of Home Affairs.
What PTE score do I need for Australia?
It depends on whether you need Competent, Proficient or Superior English. The thresholds changed in 2024–25, so read the current PTE Academic scores on the Home Affairs English language page.
What IELTS score do I need for Australia PR?
It depends on the English level you need. Check the current IELTS scores for each level on the Home Affairs English language page.
Does a higher English level give more points?
Yes. In the skilled points test, Proficient and Superior English can add points. Check the official page for the current points.
