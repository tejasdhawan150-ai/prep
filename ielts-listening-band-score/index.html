How IELTS Listening is marked

  • 40 questions, one mark each. The test has four parts, usually with 10 questions in each.
  • No negative marking. A wrong answer scores the same as a blank, so never leave a question empty.
  • Spelling counts. A misspelt answer is marked wrong. British and American spellings are both accepted.
  • Word limits are strict. If the instruction says NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS, a three-word answer is wrong, even if the idea is right.
  • No half marks. Each answer is either right or wrong.
  • You hear each recording once.

Where Listening marks usually go

  • Plurals and small words. Writing "ticket" when the speaker said "tickets".
  • Corrections in the recording. A speaker says Tuesday, then changes it to Thursday. The second one is the answer.
  • Numbers and dates. Mixing up 15 and 50, or losing track of a phone number read in groups.
  • Losing your place. You miss one answer, keep thinking about it, and miss the next two as well.
  • Maps and plans. Not following directions such as "opposite", "next to" and "at the far end".

Why your practice score may differ from the real test

Practice tests from different books vary in difficulty, and each real test version has its own conversion. Mark your practice tests strictly: a wrong spelling, too many words or a missing plural is a wrong answer. If you are lenient at home, your practice band will be higher than your real one.

Frequently asked questions

Is there negative marking in IELTS Listening?
No. A wrong answer scores zero, the same as a blank, so always write an answer.
How many correct answers do I need for Band 7 in Listening?
About 30 out of 40. The exact number can vary slightly between test versions.
How many correct answers do I need for Band 8 in Listening?
About 35 out of 40, so you can miss around five questions.
Is the Listening band score chart the same for Academic and General Training?
Yes. Both take the same Listening test, so the same conversion is used.
What band is 30 out of 40 in IELTS Listening?
About Band 7.0. 32 correct answers is about 7.5.
Do spelling mistakes count in IELTS Listening?
Yes. A misspelt answer is marked wrong. British and American spellings are both accepted.
