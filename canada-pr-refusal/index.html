Canada PR Application Refused: Common Reasons and What to Do Next

If your Canada PR application is refused, start with the refusal letter: it gives the reason and says whether you can apply again. Most people can create a new Express Entry profile and reapply once they fix the problem. To see the officer's full reasons, ask IRCC for your file and notes (often called GCMS notes). A Federal Court review is possible, but the deadline is short: 15 days if the decision arose in Canada and 60 days if it arose outside Canada.

Refused, rejected or returned?

  • Rejected: IRCC found the application incomplete and did not process it. The processing fees are refunded.
  • Returned: for example, when an application names a paid representative who is not authorised.
  • Refused: an officer assessed the application and decided you do not meet a requirement. The letter gives the reason.

Common reasons Express Entry PR applications are refused

  • Work experience that does not match the NOC: reference letters with missing duties, hours or salary, or duties that do not match the occupation in your profile.
  • Points that cannot be proved: a test score, education level, job offer or relationship status in your profile that your documents do not support, so your real score falls below the cut-off.
  • Language results: a test that expired before you submitted, or scores below the minimum for your program.
  • Proof of funds: less than IRCC's amount for your family size, or money that was not available to you.
  • Missing documents or replies: a police certificate, medical exam or document IRCC asked for and did not receive in time.
  • Misrepresentation: false or misleading information, even by mistake, which can lead to a ban of at least 5 years.
  • Inadmissibility: medical, criminal or security reasons.

What to do next, step by step

  1. Read the letter closely. Note the reason, the date and whether it says you cannot apply again.
  2. Get the officer's notes. Ask IRCC for your file under the Privacy Act. IRCC says foreign nationals can request their own personal information wherever they live, and there is no fee. The notes often explain the refusal in more detail than the letter.
  3. Decide your route. If the problem can be fixed, a new application is usually the faster path. If you believe the officer made a legal error, speak to a lawyer about a Federal Court review straight away, because of the deadline.
  4. Fix the root cause before reapplying. Better reference letters, a valid test, correct funds, or a higher CRS score from a stronger language result.

Can you apply again?

Yes, in most cases. IRCC says you can apply again at any time unless your decision letter says you cannot, and that you should only reapply if you can include information you did not include before. For Express Entry, that means a new profile that meets the requirements in force at the time. If you still have a profile in the pool, IRCC advises waiting until it expires, or withdrawing it first.

Raising your score can change your chances in the next round. Moving from CLB 7 to CLB 9 in all four skills adds 56 points for a single applicant. See IELTS 8777 for Canada PR.

Federal Court judicial review

There is no appeal tribunal for Express Entry refusals. The route is an application for leave and for judicial review at the Federal Court. You must file within 15 days if the decision arose in Canada, or 60 days if it arose outside Canada. A judge first decides whether to grant leave, and grants it only if there is a serious issue to be heard. Only a lawyer, or you yourself, can represent you in the Federal Court.

If IRCC suspects misrepresentation

Before refusing for misrepresentation, IRCC sends a procedural fairness letter that explains its concerns and gives you a chance to respond. Take it seriously and reply by the deadline with clear evidence. A finding of misrepresentation means a refusal and a ban of at least 5 years.

Frequently asked questions

Can I reapply after my Canada PR is refused?
Yes, in most cases. IRCC says you can apply again at any time unless your refusal letter says you cannot. For Express Entry you create a new profile, and you should only reapply once you can fix the reason for the refusal.
Is there an appeal for an Express Entry refusal?
There is no appeal tribunal for Express Entry refusals. You can ask the Federal Court for leave and for judicial review, within 15 days if the decision arose in Canada or 60 days if it arose outside Canada.
What are GCMS notes?
They are the notes in IRCC's case management system, including what the officer wrote about your application. You can ask IRCC for your file under the Privacy Act, and there is no fee.
Do I get my fees back if my PR is refused?
The right of permanent residence fee is refunded. The processing fee is not refunded after IRCC has assessed your application. If the application was rejected as incomplete, the processing fees are refunded.
What is the ban for misrepresentation?
If IRCC finds that you gave false or misleading information, your application is refused and you can be banned from applying for at least 5 years.
