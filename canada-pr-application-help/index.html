What help is included?

You can use as much or as little of this as you need. Everything is handled online and on WhatsApp, wherever you live.

  • Profile assessment: which PR route you qualify for, and your realistic CRS score today.
  • A CRS plan: which changes would add the most points: a higher CLB, French, a spouse's score, more experience or a provincial nomination.
  • Language coaching: IELTS General Training, CELPIP, PTE Core, TEF Canada or TCF Canada, aimed at the exact CLB or NCLC level your plan needs.
  • ECA and documents guidance: what to get, in what order, and what IRCC expects to see.
  • Your Express Entry profile: created and checked so the details match your documents.
  • Provincial nominee programs: advice on streams that fit your occupation and province.
  • The full PR application after an invitation: prepared and submitted within IRCC's 60-day window.
  • Follow-up after you submit: IRCC requests, biometrics and updates until a decision.

How it works

  1. Message us on WhatsApp. Tell us your age, education, work experience and any test scores you have.
  2. Get your assessment. Sanjita checks your eligibility and CRS score and explains your options.
  3. Build your score and your file. Language coaching if you need it, plus ECA and document guidance.
  4. Profile, invitation and application. Your Express Entry or PNP profile, then the full PR application after an invitation.
  5. Follow-up until a decision. Responses to IRCC requests and updates while your application is processed.

Why use a consultant who also coaches the language test?

For most people applying from India, the language test is the part of the CRS score they can still change. Moving from CLB 7 to CLB 9 adds 56 points for a single applicant, and French at NCLC 7 can add a 50-point bonus. Because PrepEve also coaches IELTS, CELPIP, PTE Core and French, your score target and your application plan come from the same team. See IELTS 8777 for Canada PR and French for Canada PR.

Why a licensed consultant matters

IRCC says anyone you pay to represent you, or to give you immigration advice, must be authorised: a lawyer or paralegal who is a member of a Canadian law society, a notary in Quebec, or a consultant licensed by the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants. IRCC may return or refuse an application that uses an unauthorised paid representative.

Sanjita is licensed by the College, licence R710792. Verify Status with the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants.

What we can and cannot promise

We can make sure your profile and application are complete, accurate and filed on time. We cannot promise an invitation or an approval: IRCC makes every decision, and the cut-off score changes from round to round. Be wary of anyone who guarantees PR.
