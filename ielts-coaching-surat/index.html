Why are Surat learners choosing online IELTS coaching?

Surat works long hours. Many families here run businesses in diamonds, textiles or trade, the day often ends late, and a class on the far side of the Tapi does not fit after it. Students have a different squeeze: college in the day and a study-abroad application to prepare in the evening.

Live online batches in the evening and at weekends suit both. You study where you are, and the travel time becomes practice time.

Joining from across Surat

Adajan, Pal and Rander

West of the Tapi, every trip into the city starts with a bridge. A class you join from home in Adajan or Pal skips the bridge traffic, and your essay goes for marking the same day.

Vesu, Piplod and Athwa

This side of Surat is nearest to the test: IDP's centre is in Piplod, on Dumas Road. VNSGU and SVNIT are close too, so students there can fit a batch around lectures and reach the exam without crossing the city.

Varachha, Katargam and Udhna

If your day is spent in the business areas of Varachha, Katargam or Udhna, weekday evenings are short. Weekend batches, and a trainer you can message on WhatsApp, keep a busy week from breaking your preparation.

Why people in Surat take IELTS

In Surat the reason is usually one of two: a degree abroad, or PR for the family.

  • Studying abroad after VNSGU, SVNIT or another Surat college: universities abroad want IELTS Academic and each one names its own score. Start with IELTS for the UK or our guide to Canada study permits.
  • PR for Canada: IELTS General Training. If your spouse takes the test too, their result adds points: see spouse IELTS score for Canada PR.
  • PR for Australia: IELTS or PTE Academic. Compare them in PTE vs IELTS.

No. PrepEve has no branch in Surat. Everything is taught online. For the test, you go to IDP's centre in Piplod.

IDP's centre is in the Solaris The Address building on Surat–Dumas Road in Piplod. IDP's pages give two different floors, and it has another office in the International Business Centre nearby, so the address on your booking confirmation is the safe one. Date of our check: 10 November 2026.

Yes, IDP lists it for Surat. You can resit a single skill within 60 days of the full test, on computer only, for ₹12,650. Not every organisation accepts the result. Canada's IRCC, for one, does not accept it for Express Entry.
