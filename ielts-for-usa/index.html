IELTS for the USA: What Score Do You Need?

There is no single US-wide IELTS score. Each American university sets its own English requirement, and many accept IELTS Academic as well as TOEFL iBT, Duolingo English Test or PTE Academic. The score you need is the one on your university’s admissions page for your programme, sometimes with minimums in individual skills.

Who decides the IELTS score for the USA?

The university. Unlike Canada or Australia, the US has no immigration points test for students. Admission offices decide which English tests they accept and the minimum scores, and a strong score can be the difference between a conditional and an unconditional offer.

Requirements can differ inside the same university. A graduate programme may ask for more than the undergraduate college, and some departments set a separate minimum for Speaking, for example for teaching assistant roles.

How to read a university’s English requirement

  • Overall and per-skill. Note both the overall band and any minimum in each skill.
  • Academic version. US universities ask for IELTS Academic. IELTS General Training is not used for admission.
  • Test date. Check how recent the score must be on your application date.
  • Waivers. Some universities waive the test if you studied in English. Check whether yours does before you book.

IELTS or TOEFL for the USA?

Many US universities accept both, so choose the test that suits how you perform:

IELTS Academic and TOEFL iBT for US admission
IELTS AcademicTOEFL iBT
SpeakingFace to face with an examinerRecorded into a microphone
ScoringBands 0–9Score out of 120
Best forPeople who speak better to a personPeople comfortable with on-screen, timed tasks

If a university lists only one test, take that one. If it lists several, a practice test in each will show which gives you the higher score.

How to prepare for a US admission score

  • Find the highest requirement across your shortlisted universities and aim for that, so one test result covers all of them.
  • Give Writing the most time. It is the skill that most often misses a per-skill minimum.
  • Book your test early enough to retake once before deadlines if you need to.

Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score is required for the USA?
There is no single US-wide score. Each university sets its own minimum, so check the admissions page for your programme.
Do US universities accept IELTS?
Many do, along with TOEFL iBT. Check each university’s list of accepted tests.
Is IELTS General Training accepted in the USA?
Not for university admission. US universities ask for IELTS Academic.
Is IELTS or TOEFL better for the USA?
Neither is better in general. If your universities accept both, take the one where you score higher.
Do I need IELTS for a US student visa?
The English requirement comes from your university’s admission. There is no separate US-wide IELTS score for the student visa.
