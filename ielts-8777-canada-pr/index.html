IELTS 8777 (CLB 9) for Canada PR

IELTS 8777 means Listening 8.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 7.0 and Speaking 7.0 in IELTS General Training. That is CLB 9, the level where Express Entry language points jump. For a single applicant it is worth 124 CRS points for language alone, and it unlocks extra skill-transferability points.

What does IELTS 8777 mean?

IRCC converts your IELTS General Training scores into Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) levels, one skill at a time. You need the score shown for every skill to get that CLB level in it.

IELTS General Training scores needed for each CLB level (IRCC)
CLBListeningReadingWritingSpeaking
CLB 108.58.07.57.5
CLB 98.07.07.07.0
CLB 87.56.56.56.5
CLB 76.06.06.06.0

Notice the uneven row: CLB 9 asks for 8.0 in Listening but 7.0 in the other three. That is why people write the target as "8777". It must be IELTS General Training; Academic scores are not accepted for Express Entry.

Why does CLB 9 matter so much for CRS?

Language is one of the few CRS factors you can still change. Points per skill rise with each CLB level, and CLB 9 is where the bigger bonuses start.

CRS points for your first official language (single applicant, per IRCC)
Level in all four skillsPoints per skillTotal (4 skills)
CLB 10+34136
CLB 931124
CLB 82392
CLB 71768

Moving from CLB 8 to CLB 9 in all four skills adds 32 points on its own. On top of that, CLB 9 in every skill unlocks the maximum skill-transferability points when combined with post-secondary education or foreign work experience. With a spouse included, the per-skill points are slightly lower, but the jump at CLB 9 is similar.

Points tables can change. Check IRCC's CRS criteria page for the current figures, and see our guide to Canada PR language requirements.

Which skill usually stops people at 8777?

Listening. CLB 9 needs 8.0 in Listening, a full band higher than the other skills. Many candidates score 7.5 in Listening and 7.0+ everywhere else, which drops them to CLB 8 in Listening and costs points.

Writing is the second blocker. 7.0 in General Training Writing needs a well-organised letter in Task 1 and a clearly developed essay in Task 2, with few grammar errors. Most people cannot see their own Writing mistakes, which is why trainer marking matters.

How do you reach 8777?

  1. Find your real gap. Take a timed mock and see which skill is below its CLB 9 score. Do not prepare all four equally.
  2. Listening to 8.0. Train spelling, number and plural accuracy, and the Section 3–4 question types where marks are usually lost. 8.0 leaves very little room for careless errors.
  3. Writing to 7.0. Get every letter and essay marked by a trainer, with the exact sentences that hold your band down, then rewrite them.
  4. Speaking to 7.0. Practise with 1-on-1 mock interviews so you extend answers naturally without long pauses.
  5. Repeat under test conditions until the weak skill is consistently at target.

Frequently asked questions

What does IELTS 8777 mean?
It means Listening 8.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 7.0 and Speaking 7.0 in IELTS General Training. Those are the minimum scores IRCC treats as CLB 9 in each skill.
Is 8777 the same as CLB 9?
Yes. With IELTS General Training, 8.0 in Listening and 7.0 in Reading, Writing and Speaking is CLB 9 in all four skills.
How many CRS points does CLB 9 give?
For a single applicant, CLB 9 gives 31 points per skill, 124 in total, compared with 92 at CLB 8 and 68 at CLB 7. CLB 9 in all four skills also unlocks the highest skill-transferability points when combined with education or foreign work experience.
Which skill usually stops people reaching 8777?
Listening is the usual problem, because CLB 9 needs 8.0 in Listening but only 7.0 in the other skills. Writing at 7.0 is the second most common blocker.
Can I use CELPIP instead of IELTS for CLB 9?
Yes. IRCC accepts CELPIP-General, where CLB 9 means a score of 9 in each skill, and PTE Core. Choose the test that suits you; the CLB level is what counts.
How long is an IELTS result valid for Express Entry?
IRCC accepts language test results for two years from the test date. Your result must be valid when you create your profile and when you apply.
