Canada Study Permit from India: Requirements and How to Apply

To study in Canada for more than 6 months, students from India need a study permit. You need an acceptance letter from a designated learning institution (DLI), usually a provincial or territorial attestation letter (PAL/TAL), proof of funds for tuition and living costs, and the English score your school asks for, such as IELTS. The IRCC fee is CAD 150, plus CAD 85 for biometrics. The Student Direct Stream ended in November 2024, so students from India now use the regular process. Study permit requirements

  • Acceptance letter from a designated learning institution (DLI).
  • Provincial or territorial attestation letter (PAL/TAL): most students need one, confirming a place under Canada's study permit cap. Some, such as certain graduate students at public institutions, are exempt.
  • Proof of funds: enough for your first year of tuition, travel and IRCC's minimum amount for living expenses, which IRCC updates. Evidence can include bank statements, a Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC), an education loan letter or a scholarship. Explain any large deposits.
  • A valid passport, and a medical exam or police certificate if IRCC asks.

English test for a Canada study permit

Your college or university sets the English requirement for admission. Many accept IELTS Academic, and IRCC looks at the same proof when it reviews your application. See IELTS score for a Canada student visa, and if you need a higher band, PrepEve coaches IELTS live online.

What happened to SDS?

IRCC ended the Student Direct Stream (SDS) on 8 November 2024. Students from India now apply through the regular study permit process, with the documents above.

How to apply from India

  1. Get your acceptance letter and PAL/TAL.
  2. Arrange your proof of funds and gather your documents.
  3. Apply online and pay CAD 150 plus CAD 85 for biometrics.
  4. Give biometrics at a visa application centre. See Canada biometrics in India.
  5. Answer any IRCC requests, then travel with your approval letter.

Working while you study, and after

Eligible students can work off campus up to 24 hours a week during classes. After graduating from an eligible program, you may get a post-graduation work permit (PGWP), which needs an English or French test result. Your spouse may get an open work permit only in some programs: see spouse visa options. Planning PR later? See study in Canada to PR.

Frequently asked questions

What documents are needed for a Canada study permit from India?
An acceptance letter from a DLI, a provincial or territorial attestation letter unless you are exempt, proof of funds, a valid passport and your English test result, plus any medical exam or police certificate IRCC asks for.
What is the Canada study permit fee?
CAD 150, plus CAD 85 for biometrics.
Is SDS still open for Indian students?
No. IRCC ended the Student Direct Stream on 8 November 2024. Students from India apply through the regular study permit process.
How much money do I need for a Canada study permit?
Enough for your first year of tuition, travel and IRCC's minimum amount for living expenses, which IRCC updates. Check the current amount on IRCC's proof of financial support page.
How many hours can international students work in Canada?
Eligible students can work off campus up to 24 hours a week while classes are on.
Is IELTS needed for a Canada study permit?
Your school sets the English requirement, and many ask for IELTS Academic. Check your offer letter for the score you need.
