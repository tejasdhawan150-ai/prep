How does Australia measure English for visas?

The Department of Home Affairs uses named English levels, not one band score. The main ones for skilled migration are Competent, Proficient and Superior English. Each level needs a set score in each part of the test.

The test scores behind each level have changed in recent years, so we do not repeat them here. Check the current numbers on the official pages:

  • Competent English
  • Proficient English
  • Superior English

Why does a higher English level matter for skilled visas?

For points-tested skilled visas, a higher English level can add points or open more options. Competent English is usually the starting point; Proficient and Superior can add to your score. Many applicants find English the quickest place to gain points, compared with changing age, work experience or education.

Check how many points each level gives for your visa on the Home Affairs site before setting your target.

Academic or General Training for Australia?

For visa purposes, Home Affairs accepts both IELTS Academic and General Training. Your choice may depend on other needs, such as a university place or a skills assessment body, which can ask for a specific test. Check the Home Affairs English language page and any assessing body you are dealing with.

What IELTS score do you need for an Australian student visa?

Two rules apply: your university's requirement and the student visa requirement. Universities set their own IELTS Academic scores, often with a minimum in each skill, and these differ by course. For the visa minimum, check the current rule on immi.homeaffairs.gov.au, as it has changed recently.

Why does every skill count?

Because the English levels need a minimum score in each part of the test. A strong overall band does not help if one skill falls short. The weakest skill decides your level. That is why coaching should find your lowest skill first. See how IELTS band scores are calculated.

What should IELTS coaching for Australia focus on?

Reaching the score you need in every skill, on one test date. Practice should match the test you are taking and aim at a per-skill target.

  • Per-skill targets: set from the level your visa or university needs.
  • Writing: essays and Task 1 (letters for General Training, graphs for Academic) marked by a trainer.
  • Speaking: practice with a person in the real three-part format.
  • Timed mock tests: so you can hold your level through the whole exam.

Frequently asked questions

What IELTS score is Competent English for Australia?

The score has changed in recent years, so check the current figure on the Home Affairs Competent English page. Each part of the test must meet the minimum.

Is IELTS General Training accepted for Australian visas?

Yes. Home Affairs accepts both IELTS Academic and General Training for visa purposes. A university or skills assessment body may need a specific test.

Does a higher English level give more points?

For points-tested skilled visas, Proficient and Superior English can give more points than Competent English. Check the current points on the Home Affairs site.

Do Australian universities set their own IELTS score?

Yes. Each university and course sets its own score, often with a minimum in each skill. The student visa has its own requirement too.
