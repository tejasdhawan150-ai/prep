Canada Spouse Visa: Spousal Sponsorship and Spouse Open Work Permit

There are two common "spouse visa" routes. If you are a Canadian citizen or permanent resident, you can sponsor your spouse or partner for permanent residence; IRCC's fees total CAD 1,260, plus biometrics. If you are in Canada on a work or study permit, your spouse may get an open work permit, but only in some cases: for workers, mainly jobs in TEER 0 or 1 with at least 16 months left on the permit, and for students, mainly master's programs of 16 months or more and doctoral programs. Route 1: Sponsor your spouse or partner for permanent residence

A Canadian citizen or permanent resident aged 18 or older can sponsor a spouse, common-law partner or conjugal partner for PR. You must show the relationship is genuine, and you sign an undertaking to support your partner financially for 3 years after they become a permanent resident.

IRCC fees to sponsor a spouse or partner (from 30 April 2026)
FeeAmount (CAD)
Sponsorship fee90
Principal applicant processing fee570
Right of permanent residence fee600
Total1,260
Biometrics (per person)85

Common evidence includes your marriage certificate, photos together over time, proof of communication and visits, and joint documents such as bank accounts or a lease. IRCC lists the exact forms for your case in its application package.

Open work permit for a sponsored spouse in Canada

If your spouse or partner lives with you in Canada and you are sponsoring them, they can apply for an open work permit once they have an acknowledgement of receipt for their own PR application. This lets them work while the sponsorship is processed.

Route 2: Spouse open work permit for workers and students

  • Spouses of workers: IRCC's rules for workers not on a PR pathway mainly cover principal workers in a TEER 0 or 1 job whose permit is valid for at least 16 months after IRCC receives the spouse's application. Since 21 January 2025, family members of workers in TEER 4 or 5 jobs are not eligible under that measure. Workers on some PR pathways have separate rules.
  • Spouses of students: mainly limited to spouses of students in master's programs of 16 months or more, doctoral programs and certain professional programs.

These rules have changed several times since 2024, so check your exact case before you apply. If you are moving to Canada through Express Entry, your spouse can also be included in your PR application, and their language score can add CRS points: see spouse IELTS score for Canada PR.

How long does spousal sponsorship take?

It depends on where your partner lives and how complete the application is. Check the current figure on IRCC's processing times page, and see our guide to IRCC processing times.

Frequently asked questions

Who can sponsor a spouse to Canada?
A Canadian citizen or permanent resident aged 18 or older can sponsor a spouse, common-law partner or conjugal partner, if they meet IRCC's rules and sign an undertaking to support them.
How much does it cost to sponsor a spouse to Canada?
IRCC's fees total CAD 1,260 from 30 April 2026: a CAD 90 sponsorship fee, a CAD 570 processing fee and a CAD 600 right of permanent residence fee, plus CAD 85 for biometrics.
Can my spouse work while I sponsor them?
Yes, if they live with you in Canada and have an acknowledgement of receipt for their PR application, they can apply for an open work permit.
Can my spouse get an open work permit if I work in Canada?
Possibly. IRCC's rules mainly cover workers in TEER 0 or 1 jobs whose permit has at least 16 months left. Spouses of workers in TEER 4 or 5 jobs are not eligible under that measure since January 2025.
Can my spouse get an open work permit if I study in Canada?
Mainly if you are in a master's program of 16 months or more, a doctoral program or certain professional programs.
How long is the sponsorship undertaking for a spouse?
For a spouse or partner, the sponsor promises to support them financially for 3 years after they become a permanent resident.
