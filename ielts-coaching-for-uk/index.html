Which IELTS do you need for the UK?

It depends on why you need it. For a UK visa application that needs an English test, you need a Secure English Language Test (SELT) from an approved provider. For IELTS, that is IELTS for UKVI. For university admission, the university decides which tests and scores it accepts.

  • Visa applications: book IELTS for UKVI (Academic or General Training, as your route needs) at an approved test centre. A regular IELTS test is not a SELT.
  • University admission: most universities ask for IELTS Academic. Some accept regular IELTS for admission, but your visa may still need a SELT. Check both.

See the official gov.uk guidance on SELTs for the current list of approved tests and test centres.

What English level does a UK visa need?

Each visa route sets a CEFR level, and the level can change. Do not rely on an old blog post. Check the current level for your route on gov.uk before you book.

  • Skilled Worker visa: check the current level on gov.uk's Skilled Worker knowledge of English page.
  • Any other route: find your route in gov.uk's English language requirement levels for immigration applications.

Once you know the CEFR level, check which IELTS for UKVI band in each skill meets it, using the official tables.

What IELTS score do UK universities ask for?

Each university sets its own score, usually as an overall band plus a minimum in each skill. The requirement often differs by course, so check the exact course page, not just the university's general page.

A common trap: your overall band is high enough, but one skill is 0.5 below the minimum. The offer then stays conditional. Our guide to how IELTS band scores and rounding work explains why one skill matters so much.

What should IELTS coaching for the UK focus on?

Most UK applicants need IELTS Academic with strong per-skill scores. So coaching should target your weakest skill first, not just your overall band.

  • Academic Writing Task 1: describing graphs, tables, processes and maps accurately.
  • Task 2 essays: marked by a trainer against the four official criteria. See common Task 2 mistakes.
  • Speaking: practice with a person in the real three-part format.
  • Academic Reading timing: full, timed mock tests.
  • Test booking: make sure you book IELTS for UKVI if your visa route needs a SELT.

Is IELTS for UKVI a different test?

The content and marking are the same as regular IELTS. The difference is how and where it is taken: at an approved centre, under SELT security rules. So the preparation is the same; only the booking is different.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need IELTS for UKVI or regular IELTS?

For a UK visa application that needs an English test, you need a Secure English Language Test (SELT), which for IELTS means IELTS for UKVI. For university admission, check what your university accepts.

What IELTS score do I need for a UK Skilled Worker visa?

The route sets a CEFR level, and it can change. Check the current level on the gov.uk Skilled Worker knowledge of English page, then match it to IELTS for UKVI bands.

What IELTS score do UK universities need?

Each university and course sets its own score, usually an overall band plus a minimum in each skill. Check the exact course page.

Is IELTS for UKVI harder than regular IELTS?

No. The content and marking are the same. It is taken at an approved centre under SELT rules.
