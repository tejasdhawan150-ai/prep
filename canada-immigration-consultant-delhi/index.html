How does a Canada PR consultant in Delhi help with Express Entry?

By checking your points, planning your language test and education assessment, building your profile, and preparing your documents within the 60 days you get after an invitation.

  1. Check your points. Estimate your Comprehensive Ranking System score with the CRS calculator and see what would raise it.
  2. Plan the language test. IELTS General Training, CELPIP or PTE Core for English; TEF Canada or TCF Canada for French, which can add points. See language requirements for Canada PR.
  3. Get your degree assessed. Education from outside Canada needs an ECA from WES or another designated body; the University of Delhi or GGSIPU sends your sealed transcripts. See WES ECA for Canada PR.
  4. Create your profile and watch the draws. IRCC invites the highest-ranked profiles in regular Express Entry draws.
  5. Submit within 60 days of an invitation. That means a police certificate through the Passport Seva portal, a medical exam with an IRCC panel physician, proof of funds and reference letters. See the Canada PR documents checklist.
  6. Give biometrics at the VFS Global Canada centre in New Delhi when IRCC asks.

How do Delhi families get a Canada visitor visa or super visa?

Apply online, give biometrics at the VFS Global centre in New Delhi, and show why you are going, how you will pay for the trip and your ties to India, such as a job, a business or property. Many Delhi families apply to visit children or siblings in Canada. A visitor visa costs CAD 100 per person plus CAD 85 for biometrics. Parents who want to stay longer than 6 months at a time can apply for a super visa, which allows up to 5 years per entry but needs private medical insurance of at least CAD 100,000 and a minimum income for the child in Canada. See Canada visitor visa from India and super visa for parents.

What should you ask a study visa consultant in Delhi?

Four questions: are you licensed, does my program need a PAL, who explains my funds, and what happens after I graduate?

  • "Are you licensed, and will you sign my IMM 5476?" A college admission counsellor is not the same as a licensed immigration representative.
  • "Does my program need a PAL, and do I have one?" Most students need a provincial or territorial attestation letter.
  • "Who explains my funds?" Your bank statements, education loan and any large deposits must make sense together.
  • "What happens after I graduate?" A post-graduation work permit now needs CLB 7 (university) or CLB 5 (college) in English or French.

Students from the University of Delhi or GGSIPU will also need sealed transcripts later if they apply for PR. See Canada study permit and PGWP rules.

How do you check a Canada immigration consultant in Delhi?

Search the person's name on the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants public register before you pay, and make sure that same person signs your Use of a Representative form (IMM 5476). Delhi has the widest choice of visa agents in the country, and the widest range of quality. Anyone who charges you for Canadian immigration advice must be an RCIC licensed by the College, a lawyer or paralegal in a Canadian law society, or a Quebec notary. See how to check a Canada PR consultant.

RCIC, visa agent or doing it yourself: what is the difference?

Only an authorised representative can charge you to deal with IRCC for you: an RCIC, a Canadian lawyer or paralegal, or a Quebec notary. IRCC says it will not deal with representatives who charge a fee but are not authorised, and it may return or refuse an application that uses one. Friends or family can still help you for free.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the Canada VFS centre in Delhi?
VFS Global runs Canada's visa application centre in New Delhi. Book an appointment on the VFS Canada India site and bring your biometric instruction letter and passport.
How do I know if a visa agent in Delhi is genuine?
Ask for their full name and licence number and search the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants public register. Unlicensed agents cannot legally charge you for Canadian immigration advice.
Where can I take IELTS in Delhi NCR?
IDP lists five centres: Nehru Place, Netaji Subhash Place, Rajouri Garden, Noida Sector 18 and Gurugram Sector 26. The fee is ₹19,000 at every centre in 2026.
Is CELPIP available in Delhi?
Yes. CELPIP lists New Delhi among its test cities in India. CELPIP-General is accepted for Express Entry.
Can my parents in Delhi stay in Canada for more than 6 months?
Yes, with a super visa. It allows up to 5 years per entry, with private medical insurance of at least CAD 100,000 and a minimum income for the child or grandchild in Canada.
