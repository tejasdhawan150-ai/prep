Information collected automatically

When you visit the site, advertising and analytics tools (described in Cookies and tracking) may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, the ad or link that brought you to the site, and whether you completed a form.

How we use it

  • To contact you about the demo class or webinar you booked and send you the session details.
  • To recommend a suitable program and answer your questions.
  • To deliver coaching, give feedback on your work and track your progress if you enrol.
  • To process payments and, where applicable, refunds under our Refund Policy.
  • To measure which of our ads and pages work, so we can improve them.

We do not sell your personal data.

How form submissions are handled

When you submit the demo or webinar form:

  1. Your details are sent to a Google Apps Script and stored in a Google Sheet that PrepEve controls, so our team can follow up.
  2. You are then taken to a thank-you page and on to WhatsApp (wa.me/919877714284) with a message containing your details pre-filled. The message is only sent if you press send in WhatsApp.

Once you message us on WhatsApp, that conversation is also handled under WhatsApp’s (Meta’s) own terms and privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

  • Google Ads (gtag.js). Loaded on every page to measure visits and conversions (for example, a completed booking) from our Google ads. Google may set cookies for this purpose.
  • Google Analytics 4 and Meta Pixel. These may be used to understand site usage and measure ad performance, including counting a completed booking as a lead. [TO CONFIRM: whether GA4 and/or Meta Pixel are currently switched on]
  • YouTube videos. Videos are embedded using YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and only load when you click play. Once played, YouTube (Google) may collect data under its own policies.
  • Fonts are hosted on our own server, so no font provider receives your data.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. The site still works if cookies are blocked.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run PrepEve, and only as needed:

  • Google (Sheets / Apps Script for storing form submissions; Google Ads and possibly Analytics for measurement; YouTube for videos).
  • Meta (WhatsApp for communication; Meta Pixel if enabled).
  • Our trainers, who need your details to teach you.
  • Our website host, which processes basic request data to serve the site.

We may also disclose data where required by law or to protect our legal rights. Some of these providers may store data outside India.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you can:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update it.
  • Ask us to erase it, where we no longer need it for a lawful purpose.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time (for example, by asking us to stop contacting you). This does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.
