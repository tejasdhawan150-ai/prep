Which Canada PR routes suit tech professionals in Bangalore?

Mostly Express Entry, with no job offer needed; a provincial nomination or, from 3 November 2026, a job offer can add points.

  • Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker): no job offer needed. You are ranked on CRS points for age, education, language and experience. Find your NOC code first: for example, many software roles fall under 21231 (software engineers and designers) or 21232 (software developers and programmers), but your duties decide, not your title.
  • Provincial routes: Alberta runs an Accelerated Tech Pathway within its Express Entry stream. Ontario's 2026 program now needs an Ontario job offer.
  • Job offers: from 3 November 2026, a new CRS factor gives points for Canadian work experience or a job offer, whichever scores higher, and an LMIA is no longer needed. Offers in eligible high-wage occupations earn extra points.

See the CRS calculator, NOC code guide, the November 2026 Express Entry changes and Alberta PNP.

Can French help Bangalore tech professionals get Canada PR?

Yes. NCLC 7 in all four French skills adds up to 50 CRS points and makes you eligible for French-language rounds, and you can take TCF Canada at Alliance Française de Bangalore (age 16 and over). Most tech candidates compete in general rounds with high cut-offs, while French-language rounds in October 2026 had cut-offs of 407 and 435. See French for Canada PR.

How do you check a Canada immigration consultant in Bangalore?

Search the person's name on the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants public register before you pay, and make sure that same person signs your Use of a Representative form (IMM 5476). Bangalore professionals get frequent calls from immigration agencies; ask every caller for a licence number. Anyone who charges you for Canadian immigration advice must be an RCIC licensed by the College, a lawyer or paralegal in a Canadian law society, or a Quebec notary. See how to check a Canada PR consultant.

Frequently asked questions

Do software engineers in Bangalore need a job offer for Canada PR?
Not for Express Entry. You can be invited on CRS points alone. From 3 November 2026, a job offer can also earn CRS points without an LMIA, with extra points for eligible high-wage occupations.
Is there a VFS Canada centre in Bangalore?
Yes. VFS Global runs a Canada visa application centre in Bengaluru for biometrics, by appointment.
Can I take TCF Canada in Bangalore?
Yes. Alliance Française de Bangalore runs TCF Canada for candidates aged 16 and over. Check its page for dates and the fee.
Which NOC code is a software engineer?
Many software roles fall under NOC 21231 (software engineers and designers) or 21232 (software developers and programmers). Choose the one whose main duties match what you actually do.
Where are the IELTS centres in Bangalore?
IDP lists three: St. Mark's Road (Papanna Street), Kasturba Road and Whitefield (Hoodi). Go by your booking confirmation for the exact address.
Do you have an office in Bangalore?
No. Sanjita, RCIC (licence R710792), works with Bangalore clients online and on WhatsApp.
Who is a licensed Canada immigration consultant for Bangalore?
Sanjita, PrepEve's founder, is a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (licence R710792) who works with applicants in Bangalore online on Express Entry PR, provincial nominations, study permits and visitor visas. You can confirm her licence, or any consultant's, on the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants public register.
Does a software job in Bangalore count as skilled work for Express Entry?
Usually yes. Software roles such as NOC 21231 and 21232 are TEER 1 occupations, and Federal Skilled Worker applicants need at least one year of continuous paid skilled work in the last 10 years. Your duties, not your job title, decide the NOC code.
