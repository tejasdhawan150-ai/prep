Manitoba PNP (MPNP) 2026: Streams, EOI and Draws

The Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program (MPNP) selects skilled workers through an expression of interest (EOI) pool. In regular draws, Manitoba sends Letters of Advice to Apply (LAA) to the highest-ranked candidates, often by occupation. The main streams are Skilled Worker in Manitoba (for people working in Manitoba), Skilled Worker Overseas (for people abroad with a connection to Manitoba) and the International Education Stream for Manitoba graduates. The latest draw we found, #283 on 5 November 2026, invited Skilled Worker in Manitoba candidates in selected occupations.

MPNP streams

  • Skilled Worker in Manitoba: for people already working in Manitoba. Most draws invite candidates from this pathway, often in selected occupations.
  • Skilled Worker Overseas: for skilled workers outside Canada who have a connection to Manitoba, such as family there or an invitation from Manitoba.
  • International Education Stream: for graduates of Manitoba institutions. Its Graduate Internship Pathway is for master's and doctoral graduates in Manitoba who completed a Mitacs internship. Since June 2026, Career Employment Pathway candidates with 6 months of Manitoba work can be considered in Skilled Worker in Manitoba draws.
  • Business Investor Stream: for entrepreneurs and farm investors.

How MPNP draws work

Manitoba ranks candidates in the EOI pool and holds draws, sending Letters of Advice to Apply to the highest-ranked candidates in the pathways and occupations it selects. In draw #283 on 5 November 2026, it selected Skilled Worker in Manitoba candidates in an occupation-specific selection. Candidates who say they work in a regulated occupation must be fully licensed, or show they have taken the licensing steps. See every draw on Manitoba's EOI draws page.

How to apply to the MPNP

  1. Check which stream you fit and prepare your documents, including a language test.
  2. Create an expression of interest profile in the MPNP pool.
  3. If you get a Letter of Advice to Apply, submit your full application.
  4. After a nomination, apply to IRCC for PR. After a nomination through an Express Entry-linked stream, you accept it in your Express Entry profile and get 600 CRS points. With a base (non-Express Entry) nomination, you apply to IRCC for PR separately. Either way, IRCC makes the final decision.

Frequently asked questions

What is the full form of MPNP?
MPNP stands for Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program, Manitoba's provincial nominee program.
What is a Letter of Advice to Apply?
It is Manitoba's invitation to submit a full MPNP application, sent to selected candidates from the expression of interest pool in a draw.
What was the latest Manitoba PNP draw?
At our check on 18 November 2026, the latest draw we found was #283 on 5 November 2026, which selected Skilled Worker in Manitoba candidates in selected occupations.
Can I apply to the Manitoba PNP from India?
Through the Skilled Worker Overseas stream, if you have a connection to Manitoba, such as close family there or an invitation from the province.
Do I need to be licensed for a regulated job?
Manitoba says candidates in regulated occupations must be fully licensed or show they have taken the necessary licensing steps, or their application may be refused.
What is the MPNP Graduate Internship Pathway?
A pathway in the International Education Stream for master's and doctoral graduates in Manitoba who completed a Mitacs internship.
