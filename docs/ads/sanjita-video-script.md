# Sanjita — Meta video ad script (IELTS free webinar)

**Length:** 20–30 seconds · **Format:** vertical 9:16, phone camera, natural light, face the window · **Look at the lens, not the screen.**
Record 3 takes of each version. Speak a little slower than normal. Add captions in Instagram/CapCut (most people watch on mute).

---

## Version A — "Stuck at 6.5" (lead with this one)

| Time | Say | On screen (caption) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | "Stuck at Band 6.5? Listen to this." | **Stuck at 6.5?** |
| 3–10 s | "Most students I meet are losing marks in one skill — and they don't know which one." | **Most lose marks in ONE skill** |
| 10–20 s | "This weekend I'm running a free 60-minute live IELTS session. I'll show you how examiners mark you, and what to fix first." | **Free live session · 60 min** |
| 20–27 s | "Saturday and Sunday, 7 PM. Tap below to reserve your seat. The link comes on WhatsApp." | **Sat & Sun · 7 PM IST · Tap below** |

## Version B — "Before you pay again"

| Time | Say | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | "Before you pay for another IELTS attempt —" | **Before your next attempt…** |
| 3–12 s | "— spend one free hour finding out where your marks are actually going." | **Find where your marks go** |
| 12–22 s | "I'm Sanjita, founder of PrepEve. Every weekend we run a free live session on how IELTS is really marked." | **Free · Live · Every weekend** |
| 22–28 s | "Saturday or Sunday, 7 PM. Reserve your free seat below." | **Reserve your free seat ↓** |

---

**Don't say:** guaranteed band, "100% result", "best coaching", "only few seats left", or any score promise. (Meta and Google reject these, and we never claim them.)
**Do say if natural:** "4.7 stars on Google", "15,000+ students", "live, not recorded".

**Ad settings when uploading:** headline "Free IELTS Webinar · Sat & Sun 7 PM", button **Sign Up**, link `https://www.prepeve.com/webinar`.
