## The audit questions
1. **What's genuinely different?** A fixed loop (assess → diagnose → train → correct → repeat) in which a named trainer, not software, corrects your essays and runs 1-on-1 speaking mocks.
2. **What proof do we have?** 4.7★ from 300+ Google reviews; three video stories with before → after bands; three linked Google reviews (8.5 / 8.0 / 7.5); 15,000+ trained; Sanjita's RCIC credential.
3. **Weak claims?** "Certified trainers" (no source, removed). "Max 5 per batch" (removed by owner decision). "Most students choose this" on two cards (merged away).
4. **Generic copy?** "More material won't fix your band" was fine, but "Two ways to prepare", "What students say" and "Honest answers" could belong to any institute. All are rewritten around the method or the evidence.
5. **Underselling?** Before → after bands appeared only in the hero panel; the video badges showed the end score alone. The trainer credential was not tied to the feedback. The method did not exist as a named system.
6. **Friction?** Two equal-weight hero CTAs; the same plans appeared twice (Courses + Pricing); an 11-row FAQ; a heavy table on mobile.
7. **Category leader vs generic?** Leaders show a named method, named people, and checkable numbers linked to sources. Generic sites show adjectives and stock stats. The page now leads with the method and linked evidence and uses no superlatives.
8. **What would stop a visitor booking?** "Is it just videos?" and "Will anyone see my work?" Both are now answered in the method, the "What you get" section and the first FAQ items.
