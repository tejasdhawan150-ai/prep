IRCC Processing Times 2026: How to Check and Read Them

IRCC posts processing times for every application on its Check processing times page. Choose your application type and, for visitor visas, study and work permits, the country you apply from (India). For PR programs, the figure shows how long IRCC took to finish 80% of applications in the past 6 months, or a forward-looking estimate such as the one for Express Entry. The clock starts when IRCC receives a complete application and stops at the decision. IRCC's service standard is to process most complete Express Entry applications in 6 months or less.

How to check IRCC processing times for India

  1. Open IRCC's Check processing times page.
  2. Choose the type of application, such as Express Entry, family sponsorship, visitor visa, study permit or work permit.
  3. For a visitor visa, study permit or work permit, choose India as the country you are applying from.
  4. Read the time shown, and note the date it was last updated.

How IRCC calculates processing times

  • Backward-looking times show how long it took to finalise 80% of past applications. For permanent residence programs, IRCC looks at the past 6 months; for temporary residence programs, recent weeks.
  • Forward-looking times estimate how long a new application submitted today would take, based on the number of applications waiting, processing capacity and expected volume. IRCC uses these for programs such as Express Entry and updates them regularly.

Processing times are averages, not promises. Your application can be faster or slower depending on how complete it is and what checks it needs.

Why applications take longer, and how to avoid it

  • Missing documents: an incomplete application can be returned or delayed while IRCC asks for more.
  • Biometrics: IRCC moves forward once it has your biometrics, so book the earliest appointment you can. See Canada biometrics in India.
  • Medical exams and police certificates: get them done early. See the PR medical exam and police certificate guides.
  • Requests from IRCC: reply before the deadline in your account.
  • Admission targets: when applications exceed IRCC's yearly targets for a program, some wait longer for space.

How to check your own application status

Sign in to the IRCC account or portal you applied through to see messages and requests. Some PR and family applications can also be followed in IRCC's application status tracker. If your application has gone well past the posted time, a licensed representative can review it with you and suggest next steps.

Frequently asked questions

How long does Canada PR take from India?
IRCC's service standard is to process most complete Express Entry applications in 6 months or less after you submit. Check the current figure on IRCC's processing times page.
What is the Canada visitor visa processing time from India?
It changes often. Check IRCC's processing times page and choose visitor visa and India for the current figure. Give your biometrics quickly to avoid delays.
How are IRCC processing times calculated?
For PR programs, IRCC shows how long it took to finish 80% of applications in the past 6 months, or a forward-looking estimate for new applications. Temporary residence times are based on recent weeks.
When does the processing time start?
When IRCC receives your complete application. It ends when a decision is made.
Why is my application taking longer than the posted time?
Common reasons are missing documents, waiting for biometrics or medical results, extra checks, or more applications than IRCC's yearly targets allow.
How do I check my IRCC application status?
Sign in to the IRCC account or portal you applied through. Some PR and family applications can also be followed in IRCC's application status tracker.
