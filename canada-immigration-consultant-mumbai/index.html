Can a consultant in Mumbai get you a job in Canada?

No. Real job offers come from Canadian employers, and no consultant can sell you one. Some Mumbai agencies advertise "Canada jobs" alongside visas. Keep three facts in mind:

  • Search for jobs yourself on Job Bank and employers' own career pages.
  • The employer pays for the LMIA (CAD 1,000 per position) and cannot recover it from you. For LMIA-exempt jobs, the employer pays a CAD 230 compliance fee.
  • You pay only for your own permit (CAD 155), biometrics and medical exam.

See jobs in Canada for Indians and Canada work permit from India.

How do University of Mumbai graduates send documents to WES?

WES only accepts University of Mumbai documents issued and attested by the designated official at the institution that conducted the exam, such as the Controller of Examinations or the Registrar's office. The rule applies if you studied at the university or one of its colleges, and attestation by a professor or a notary is not accepted. Getting this right avoids weeks of delay in your Express Entry plan. See how to send documents to WES from India.
