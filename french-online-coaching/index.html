Who is this French coaching for?

It is for adults who want French for Canada PR and need a clear path to NCLC 7. Most of our students work full-time and study around their job.

  • Complete beginners who have never studied French.
  • Learners with some French (school French, A2 or B1) who stalled and need structure.
  • Test-ready learners who need focused TEF Canada or TCF Canada practice to push one weak skill to NCLC 7.

How does the online French course work?

You start with a free class and a level check, then join a batch that fits your schedule.

  1. Book a free French class. A trainer runs a live class and checks your level.
  2. Get a realistic plan. You learn which test suits your deadline and roughly how long NCLC 7 should take you.
  3. Join a batch. Choose a morning, evening or weekend batch. Most learners study about 4–5 hours a week.
  4. Move to test practice. Once your French is strong enough, classes focus on your chosen test.

How long will it take me to reach NCLC 7?

From zero, in our experience most working adults need around 9–14 months at 4–5 hours a week, including test preparation. If you already have some French, it is shorter.

We give you your own estimate in the free class. We would rather quote you an honest number than a marketable one. For what each level means, read NCLC levels explained.

Should I prepare for TEF Canada or TCF Canada?

Both are accepted for Express Entry, so choose based on test dates near you, your deadline and which format you prefer. We recommend one in your free class. For the detail, see our side-by-side TEF and TCF comparison.

DELF is a French diploma that does not expire, and it is useful for study and work. It is not the test for Express Entry, though, so most PR candidates prepare for TEF Canada or TCF Canada.

Why learn French for Canada PR?

According to IRCC, NCLC 7 or higher in all four skills can add up to 50 additional CRS points (50 if your English is CLB 5 or higher, 25 if it is CLB 4 or lower). IRCC also holds French-language rounds for candidates with strong French.

No course can promise an invitation, because draws are decided by IRCC. What coaching can do is get your French to the level that counts. If you also need English, we coach that too: see IELTS coaching for Canada PR and the language rules for Canada PR.
