IELTS for PR from Alberta

Your IELTS General Training result is converted to CLB levels, and those levels are what each PR route in Alberta checks.

  • Canadian Experience Class (Express Entry): for people with at least a year of skilled work in Canada. Higher CLB levels mean more CRS points: try the CRS calculator.
  • AAIP, Alberta's provincial nominee program: each stream sets its own minimum, so read the official page for your stream before you book a test. See how a nomination works.
  • Graduating from NAIT, NorQuest, MacEwan or the University of Alberta? A post-graduation work permit needs CLB 5 for college graduates and CLB 7 for university graduates, on General Training, not Academic. See the language rule for the PGWP.

For citizenship, adults aged 18 to 54 must show CLB 4 in speaking and listening.

IELTS 8777 (CLB 9) is the level where Express Entry language points jump. Listening is the strict one: 7.5 leaves it at CLB 8, even with 7.0 or more in the other three skills.

Does IRCC accept IELTS One Skill Retake?
Not for Express Entry. Edmonton's IDP centre offers One Skill Retake, but for Canada PR you need a full test result, so a low skill means sitting General Training again.
What score do I need for a PGWP after NAIT or MacEwan?
College graduates need CLB 5 and university graduates need CLB 7, in all four skills. In IELTS General Training, CLB 5 is 5.0 in Listening, Writing and Speaking and 4.0 in Reading; CLB 7 is 6.0 in each. Check which level applies to your programme.
