Saskatchewan PNP (SINP) 2026: Streams and Job Offer Rules

The Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program (SINP) now centres on a Saskatchewan job offer. Its International Skilled Worker: Employment Offer category needs a job offer in a TEER 0 to 3 occupation, at least one year of work experience in the last 10 years, and CLB 4. Since 2025, applications under the Saskatchewan Express Entry and Occupations In-Demand sub-categories without a Saskatchewan job offer are returned. For 2026, Saskatchewan named priority sectors such as health care, agriculture, skilled trades, energy, mining, manufacturing and technology, and capped some others.

SINP categories in 2026

  • International Skilled Worker: Employment Offer: for skilled workers with a Saskatchewan job offer in TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3. Truck drivers and most health care occupations are excluded here and use other categories.
  • International Skilled Worker: Saskatchewan Express Entry and Occupations In-Demand: these sub-categories still appear on the SINP site, but Saskatchewan announced in 2025 that applications without a Saskatchewan-based job offer would be returned.
  • Saskatchewan Experience: for people already living and working in Saskatchewan, with sub-categories such as health and students. Post-graduation work permit holders are now limited to certain sub-categories.

Saskatchewan's 2026 rules: priority and capped sectors

  • Applicants in priority sectors (health care, agriculture, skilled trades, energy, mining, manufacturing and technology) can apply at any time, including from overseas.
  • Nominations in accommodation and food services, retail trade and trucking are capped at 25% of the 2026 total, with six intakes for employers.
  • At least 750 nominations in 2026 are reserved for graduates of Saskatchewan post-secondary institutions with a job offer in a priority sector.

How to apply to the SINP

  1. Get a job offer from a Saskatchewan employer; the employer gets SINP approval for the position.
  2. Check you meet the category's experience, education and language rules.
  3. Apply through the SINP online system.
  4. After a nomination, apply to IRCC for PR. After a nomination through an Express Entry-linked stream, you accept it in your Express Entry profile and get 600 CRS points. With a base (non-Express Entry) nomination, you apply to IRCC for PR separately. Either way, IRCC makes the final decision.

Frequently asked questions

What is the full form of SINP?
SINP stands for Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program, Saskatchewan's provincial nominee program.
Do I need a job offer for the Saskatchewan PNP?
In practice, yes. The Employment Offer category needs one, and Saskatchewan said in 2025 that Express Entry and Occupations In-Demand applications without a Saskatchewan job offer would be returned.
What CLB do I need for the SINP Employment Offer category?
CLB 4 minimum, with at least one year of work experience in the last 10 years and a TEER 0 to 3 job offer.
Which sectors does Saskatchewan prioritise in 2026?
Health care, agriculture, skilled trades, energy, mining, manufacturing and technology.
Is the SINP Occupations In-Demand stream open?
It still appears on the SINP site, but applications without a Saskatchewan job offer are returned. Check the official SINP pages before applying.
Can international students in Saskatchewan get nominated?
Yes, through the Saskatchewan Experience student sub-category, and at least 750 nominations in 2026 are reserved for Saskatchewan graduates with a job offer in a priority sector.
