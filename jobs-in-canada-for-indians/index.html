Jobs in Canada for Indians: How to Find Work and Avoid Scams

To work in Canada from India you usually need a job offer and a work permit. For most jobs, the employer first gets a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA), or uses an exemption, and you then apply for an employer-specific work permit. Search on Job Bank, the Government of Canada's free job site, and on employers' own websites. A real employer never charges you for a job and cannot pass its recruitment or LMIA fees on to you: you pay only for your work permit, medical exam and biometrics. Anyone asking you to pay for a job offer is a red flag.

How hiring from India works

  1. An employer offers you a job. Most employers must first show they need a foreign worker, through an LMIA from Employment and Social Development Canada, unless the job is LMIA-exempt.
  2. You apply for a work permit with the job offer and the LMIA or offer of employment number.
  3. You give biometrics and, if asked, take a medical exam.
  4. You travel and start work for that employer, in that job.

Some people can work without a job offer on an open work permit, such as eligible spouses of some workers and students, and graduates on a post-graduation work permit. See Canada work permit from India and spouse open work permits.

Where to look for jobs in Canada

  • Job Bank (jobbank.gc.ca), the Government of Canada's free job site. It also shows wages and job outlooks by occupation and province.
  • Employers' own career pages, especially large hospitals, universities, banks and technology companies.
  • Provincial programs: several provinces now nominate workers with a job offer from an employer in that province. See Ontario, BC and Alberta.

Which jobs are in demand?

Look at two official signals rather than viral "top 10 jobs" lists. Job Bank's job outlooks rate prospects for each occupation by province. And IRCC's Express Entry category-based draws show the fields Canada is selecting for permanent residence, such as healthcare and social services, trades and transport. Jobs that need a Canadian licence

Some jobs, such as nursing, medicine, engineering and teaching, are regulated by each province. You usually need to be licensed or registered there before you can work in the job, which can take months, so start early. Your language score often counts here too: see IELTS for nurses.

Part-time jobs for Indian students in Canada

Eligible international students can work off campus up to 24 hours a week while classes are on, and full time during scheduled breaks. You can only start working once your study program has begun. Common student jobs include retail, food service and campus jobs. See Canada study permit from India.

How to spot a Canada job scam

  • You are asked to pay a fee for the job offer, the LMIA or "processing" before you are hired.
  • You are asked for bank details or passwords before a real interview.
  • Someone sends you a cheque and asks you to cash it and return part of it.
  • The "employer" will not give a company name, address or a real interview, or promises a visa.

Under Canada's rules, employers cannot make you pay to get or keep a job, or pass their recruitment fees on to you. Job Bank says legitimate Canadian job ads will not ask for banking or personal information.

Frequently asked questions

Can Indians get a job in Canada without a work permit?
No, not usually. Most people need a work permit, and most work permits need a job offer backed by an LMIA or an exemption. Some people qualify for an open work permit, such as eligible spouses and graduates.
How can I find a job in Canada from India?
Search Job Bank, the Government of Canada's free job site, and employers' own career pages. Check whether the employer can hire from abroad, then apply for a work permit once you have an offer.
Do I need to pay for a Canadian job offer?
No. Employers cannot charge you for a job or pass their recruitment or LMIA fees on to you. You pay only for your own work permit, medical exam and biometrics. A request to pay for a job is a red flag.
What is an LMIA job?
A job where the employer got a Labour Market Impact Assessment from Employment and Social Development Canada, showing it needs a foreign worker. You use it to apply for an employer-specific work permit.
How many hours can Indian students work in Canada?
Eligible international students can work off campus up to 24 hours a week during classes, and full time during scheduled breaks.
How do I check the salary for a job in Canada?
Job Bank shows wages for each occupation by province and region. Search your occupation on Job Bank's wages and job outlook pages.
