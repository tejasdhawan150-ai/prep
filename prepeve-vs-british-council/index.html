Which type of learner may prefer each option?

Different learners need different things. Neither option is right for everyone.

  • British Council may suit: Learners who want a course from an organisation that runs the test, and who like booking individual group or private classes with credits [VERIFY].
  • PrepEve may suit: learners whose Writing or Speaking is stuck and who want a trainer to mark every essay and run 1-on-1 speaking mocks until their specific weaknesses are fixed.
  • Either may suit: learners who need structure and practice tests. Compare the price, hours and conditions that matter to you.

What should you ask before choosing?

  • Who marks my writing, and how specific is the feedback?
  • Who marks my writing, how many essays, and how fast?
  • Do I practise speaking 1-on-1 with a person?
  • How many full-length mock tests are included?
  • What exactly does the fee include — live hours, marked essays, speaking mocks and mock tests — in writing?
