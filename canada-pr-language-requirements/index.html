Canada PR Language Requirements: English and French Tests Explained

For Canada PR through Express Entry, you need an IRCC-approved language test less than 2 years old. English: IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General or PTE Core. French: TEF Canada or TCF Canada. Scores convert to CLB (English) or NCLC (French) levels for each skill, and those levels decide eligibility and part of your CRS points.

Which language tests are approved for Canada PR?

According to IRCC, Express Entry accepts three English tests and two French tests. Any other test, or another version of the same brand, does not count.

Approved language tests for Express Entry

EnglishIELTS General TrainingIELTS Academic is not accepted
EnglishCELPIP-GeneralCELPIP-General LS is not enough for Express Entry
EnglishPTE CorePTE Academic is not accepted
FrenchTEF CanadaTake all four skills
FrenchTCF CanadaTake all four skills

Your results must be less than 2 years old when you create your Express Entry profile and on the day you submit your PR application. Plan your test date with this in mind.

How do test scores convert to CLB and NCLC?

IRCC does not use your raw test scores. It converts each skill to a Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) level for English, or a Niveaux de compétence linguistique canadiens (NCLC) level for French. Program rules and CRS points are written in CLB/NCLC.

Each test has its own conversion table. CELPIP levels match CLB levels one-to-one: CELPIP 7 in a skill is CLB 7 in that skill. IELTS and PTE Core use different score scales, so they need a table. For PTE Core, TEF Canada and TCF Canada, use the official IRCC test equivalency charts; we don't repeat those numbers here because IRCC updates them.

Many people search for "IELTS for Express Entry" and expect one pass mark. There isn't one. Each program sets a minimum CLB, and higher CLB levels add CRS points. For example, Federal Skilled Worker needs at least CLB 7 in all four skills. Try your own bands in the IELTS band calculator with the "Canada PR" option switched on. For the difference between the two IELTS versions, read IELTS General vs Academic for Canada PR.

Why does each skill count separately?

Because IRCC converts Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking to CLB one at a time. Your overall band is not used.

Example: Listening 8.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 6.5, Speaking 7.0 gives CLB 9, 9, 8 and 9. Writing holds you below CLB 9 in all four skills. One weak skill can cost CRS points or even eligibility, so find your weakest skill first.

What is a first and second official language?

Canada has two official languages, English and French. In Express Entry, the language you score higher in is usually your first official language. The other can count as your second official language if you take an approved test in it.

Most points come from the first official language. A strong second language can add more. See the CRS criteria on canada.ca for the exact points.

How many extra points does French give?

According to IRCC, NCLC 7 or higher in all four French skills gives up to 50 additional CRS points:

  • 50 points if your English is CLB 5 or higher in all four skills.
  • 25 points if your English is CLB 4 or lower (or you have no English test).

This is on top of points for your second official language. Read French for Canada PR and TEF vs TCF to pick a French test.

What IELTS score do I need for a PNP?

Provincial Nominee Programs (PNPs) set their own language minimums. They vary by province and by stream, and they change. Check the province's official program page, and the IRCC Provincial Nominee Program page, before you book a test. If a PNP stream is linked to Express Entry, the Express Entry test rules above also apply.

Should I take IELTS, CELPIP or PTE Core?

All three give CLB levels IRCC accepts. Choose the one where you will score highest in your weakest skill.

  • Choose IELTS General Training if you prefer speaking face to face with an examiner, you write well by hand or on paper-style tasks, or you already know the format.
  • Choose CELPIP-General if you are comfortable with North American English, like a fully computer-based test, and want scores that read directly as CLB levels.
  • Choose PTE Core if you type quickly, are comfortable speaking into a microphone, and prefer computer-scored tasks.

Frequently asked questions

Which English tests are accepted for Canada PR?
According to IRCC, Express Entry accepts IELTS General Training, CELPIP-General and PTE Core. IELTS Academic and PTE Academic are not accepted.
What IELTS score do I need for Express Entry?
There is no single pass mark. Each program sets a minimum CLB level, and higher CLB levels add CRS points. For example, Federal Skilled Worker needs at least CLB 7 in all four skills, which is IELTS General 6.0 in each skill.
How long is my language test valid for Canada PR?
IRCC only accepts results less than 2 years old. They must be valid when you create your Express Entry profile and when you submit your PR application.
Is CELPIP the same as CLB?
CELPIP levels match CLB levels one-to-one. A CELPIP 7 in a skill is CLB 7 in that skill.
How much does French add to my CRS score?
According to IRCC, NCLC 7 or higher in all four French skills gives up to 50 additional CRS points: 50 if your English is CLB 5 or higher, 25 if it is CLB 4 or lower.
What IELTS score do I need for a PNP?
Each province sets its own language minimum for each stream. Check the province's official program page before you book a test.
