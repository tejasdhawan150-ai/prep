How to Increase Your CRS Score With Language Tests

Language is the CRS factor most candidates can still change. Moving from CLB 7 to CLB 9 in all four IELTS skills adds 56 points for a single applicant. French at NCLC 7 in all four skills adds a 50-point bonus (with English at CLB 5 or higher) plus 12 second-language points. A spouse's test can add up to 20 more.

With a spouse coming with you, your own first-language points are slightly lower: CLB 7 to CLB 9 adds 52 instead of 56.

Why CLB 9 matters more than the table shows

CRS skill-transferability points combine your language level with your education and with your foreign work experience. Each combination is capped at 50 points, and all of them together at 100. CLB 9 in all four skills is what unlocks the higher values. What CLB 9 needs.

French: the biggest single jump

  • NCLC 7 or higher in all four French skills, with English at CLB 5 or higher: 50 bonus points.
  • NCLC 7 or higher in French, with English at CLB 4 or lower or no English test: 25 bonus points.
  • Second-language points: 1 per skill at NCLC 5–6, 3 at NCLC 7–8, 6 at NCLC 9 or higher. Up to 24 points (22 with a spouse).

NCLC 7 in all four skills is also the level IRCC uses for French-language proficiency rounds. Calculate your French points or read how to reach NCLC 7.

Retake strategy: fix the skill that holds your CLB back

Your CLB is set skill by skill. If three skills are at 7.0 and one is at 6.5, that one skill decides whether you reach CLB 9. Put most of your preparation into it before you book the retest.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to increase my CRS score?
For many candidates it is a retake that moves IELTS from CLB 7 or 8 to CLB 9 in all four skills. If your English is already high, French at NCLC 7 adds the most.
How many CRS points does French give?
A 50-point bonus for NCLC 7 in all four skills with English at CLB 5 or higher (25 otherwise), plus up to 24 second-language points.
Does my spouse's IELTS increase our CRS score?
Yes, by up to 20 points: up to 5 per skill, with the full 5 at CLB 9.
Is CLB 10 worth it over CLB 9?
It adds 3 points per skill for a single applicant, 12 in total. CLB 9 is the bigger jump.
Which IELTS do I need for Express Entry?
IELTS General Training. IELTS Academic is not accepted for Express Entry.
