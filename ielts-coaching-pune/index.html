Why are Pune learners choosing online IELTS coaching?

Pune's working day is split across IT parks on opposite edges of the city: Hinjewadi in the west, Kharadi and Magarpatta in the east. A coaching class in Deccan or on FC Road means a second trip across town after work, and that is the trip most people stop making by the second week.

A live online class starts when you reach home, wherever home is. The hour you would have spent on the road goes into practice, and into the corrections your trainer made on your last essay.

Joining from across Pune

Hinjewadi, Wakad and Baner

If you work at the Hinjewadi IT park and live in Wakad, Baner or Aundh, an evening or late batch from home fits around your shift. On test day, the Shivajinagar centre on Ganeshkhind Road is the closer of Pune's two.

Kharadi, Viman Nagar and Magarpatta

From the eastern IT parks, a class in the city centre means crossing the river in rush hour. Study online and keep that journey for test day: the Boat Club Road centre is the one closest to Koregaon Park, Kharadi and Viman Nagar.

Kothrud, Deccan and Pimpri-Chinchwad

For students at Savitribai Phule Pune University, COEP, Symbiosis or MIT-WPU, and for people in Pimpri-Chinchwad, weekend batches fit around lectures and long commutes, and every class happens at your desk.

Why people in Pune take IELTS

What you need IELTS for decides the test.

  • A master's or MBA abroad after Savitribai Phule Pune University, Symbiosis, COEP or MIT-WPU: IELTS Academic. Each course sets its own minimum, often in every skill.
  • Canada PR while you keep working: IELTS General Training. The level that adds the most points is CLB 9, or 8777. Start with IELTS for Canada PR and check your points with the CRS calculator.
  • A UK visa route that asks for a secure test: IELTS for UKVI, which the Boat Club Road centre offers. See the UK English requirements.

IDP runs all IELTS tests in India, and you book on its website. In Pune it lists two computer-delivered centres:
