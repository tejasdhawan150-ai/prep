Before you book

Questions students ask us

Is the demo just a sales pitch?
No. It's a real class: you speak, you write, a trainer corrects you and builds your plan. We don't pitch during the class and we don't ask for payment. If you want to hear about courses, ask at the end. If not, keep the plan and go.
Is it really free? What's the catch?
It's free, with no card and no payment. There's no catch: it's how most students first meet us. We'd rather show you how we teach than tell you.
I'm a beginner. Is this for me?
Yes. The class starts from where you are today. Your trainer checks your level and builds a plan that begins with the basics you need, not with advanced tricks.
I've already taken IELTS and missed my band. Will this help?
This is who the demo helps most. Bring your last scores. Your trainer will find the skill pulling your band down and show you what to fix first, so the next attempt isn't a repeat of the last one.
I work full-time. Can I fit this in?
The demo is 60 minutes, online, on your phone or laptop. When we confirm on WhatsApp, tell us what time suits you and we'll arrange your slot. Your study plan is built around the hours you actually have.
Why pay for coaching when YouTube is free?
YouTube teaches everyone the same thing. It can't hear your speaking or read your essay and tell you which sentences cost you marks. That personal feedback is what moves a stuck band — and the demo lets you try it free.
Does online coaching actually work?
Our classes are live, not recorded, and they start by finding where you lose marks. A trainer corrects your answers on the spot and tells you exactly what to change. You don't need to travel, and you can join from anywhere.
What if I join a course and don't improve?
Your trainer pinpoints exactly what is costing you marks and adjusts your practice around it, with extra trainer-marked essays and speaking feedback. You can message your trainers on WhatsApp between classes, and 1-on-1 Mentorship is there if you need every session built around your gap.
What happens after I book?
Within 30 minutes we message you on WhatsApp to confirm your slot and send the class link. The class runs on Google Meet — no download needed, and it works on any phone.
