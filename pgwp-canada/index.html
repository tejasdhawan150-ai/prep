Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) Canada: Rules for 2026

A post-graduation work permit (PGWP) is an open work permit for students who graduate from an eligible program at an eligible Canadian school. You must apply within 180 days of confirmation that you completed your program. Since November 2024 you also need a language test: CLB 7 in all four skills for university degree graduates, or CLB 5 for college graduates. College graduates of non-degree programs must also be in an eligible field of study. A PGWP usually matches your program length, up to 3 years, and IRCC's fees total CAD 255. Graduating soon? Book your language test early: CLB 7 or CLB 5 is needed before you apply.

What it is
An open work permit after graduating in Canada
Deadline
Apply within 180 days of confirmation that you completed your program
Language
CLB 7 (university degree) or CLB 5 (college), all four skills
Accepted tests
CELPIP-General, IELTS General Training, PTE Core, TEF Canada, TCF Canada
Field of study
Required for college non-degree programs; not for bachelor's, master's or doctoral degrees
IRCC fees
CAD 255 (CAD 155 work permit + CAD 100 open work permit holder fee)

PGWP eligibility

  • You graduated from an eligible program at an eligible designated learning institution.
  • Your program was at least 8 months long (900 hours in Quebec).
  • You studied full time in Canada in every semester, except that your final semester may be part time.
  • You apply within 180 days of confirmation that you completed your program.
  • You meet the language and, if it applies to you, the field of study requirement below.

The PGWP language requirement

PGWP language levels (applications from 1 November 2024)
You graduated fromMinimum level in all four skills
A bachelor's, master's or doctoral degree program at a universityCLB 7 (English) or NCLC 7 (French)
Other programs, such as college diplomas and certificatesCLB 5 (English) or NCLC 5 (French)

IRCC accepts the same tests it uses for Express Entry: CELPIP-General, IELTS General Training, PTE Core, TEF Canada and TCF Canada. Results must be less than 2 years old when you apply. Note that IELTS Academic is not accepted for the PGWP. Check your level with our IELTS to CLB calculator or the PTE Core CLB calculator.

Field of study rule

Graduates of college certificate and diploma programs must have studied in a field linked to occupations in long-term shortage, from IRCC's list. Graduates of bachelor's, master's and doctoral degree programs do not have to meet this rule, as long as they meet the language requirement.

How long is a PGWP?

A PGWP usually matches the length of your program, up to 3 years. Graduates of a master's program that is shorter than 2 years, and at least 8 months long, may get a 3-year PGWP. Your permit cannot run past your passport's expiry date, so renew your passport first if it expires soon. You can get only one PGWP in your lifetime, so apply carefully.

PGWP to PR

Skilled work on your PGWP can make you eligible for the Canadian Experience Class after 1 year, and a higher language score adds CRS points. See study in Canada to PR and check your score with the CRS calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What language test is needed for a PGWP?
CLB 7 in all four skills for university degree graduates, or CLB 5 for other programs, from CELPIP-General, IELTS General Training, PTE Core, TEF Canada or TCF Canada. Results must be less than 2 years old.
Is IELTS Academic accepted for the PGWP?
No. IRCC accepts IELTS General Training, along with CELPIP-General, PTE Core, TEF Canada and TCF Canada.
When should I apply for a PGWP?
Within 180 days of receiving confirmation that you completed your program, such as your final transcript or a letter from your school.
How long is a PGWP valid?
Usually as long as your program, up to 3 years. Graduates of master's programs of at least 8 months but under 2 years may get 3 years.
What is the PGWP fee?
CAD 255: CAD 155 for the work permit and CAD 100 for the open work permit holder fee.
Does every program need to be in an eligible field of study?
No. Only college certificate and diploma programs must be in an eligible field. Bachelor's, master's and doctoral degree graduates are exempt from the field rule.
