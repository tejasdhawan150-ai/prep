PTE Exam Fee and Booking in India (2026)

The PTE exam fee in India for 2026 is ₹18,900, including 18% GST, for PTE Academic, PTE Academic UKVI and PTE Core. It went up from ₹18,000 on 16 March 2026 and is the same at every test centre. You book online through a myPTE account with your passport, choose a centre, date and time, and pay. Pearson shows the final price at checkout.

The fee includes 18% GST and is the same in every city. Pearson sets prices for each country and can change them, so check the amount at checkout. A retake costs the full fee again.

What the PTE fee includes

  • The whole test in one sitting of about two hours: Speaking and Writing, Reading, and Listening.
  • Your score report in your myPTE account, usually within 48 hours.
  • Sending your scores to universities and immigration authorities through myPTE.

Rescheduling and cancellation rules

PTE rescheduling and refund rules
When you make the changeWhat happens
More than 14 days before the testReschedule free, or cancel for a full refund
14 to 7 days beforeYou cannot reschedule; cancel for a 50% refund and book again
Less than 7 days beforeNo refund

Refunds do not apply to tests paid for with a voucher. Pearson has changed these rules before, so read its current cancellation policy before you book.

How to book the PTE exam: step by step

  1. Choose the right test. PTE Academic for study abroad, Australia and New Zealand; PTE Core for Canada PR; PTE Academic UKVI for UK visas that need it.
  2. Create a myPTE account with your name exactly as it appears on your passport.
  3. Pick a test centre, date and time. Popular weekend slots fill first, so book early if you need a specific date.
  4. Pay the fee online and keep the payment confirmation.
  5. Read your confirmation email for the centre address and reporting time.
  6. Bring your original passport on test day. It must match the details on your booking.

PTE fee vs IELTS fee

In 2026, PTE costs ₹18,900 and IELTS Academic or General Training costs ₹19,000 in India, so the fees are almost the same. The bigger cost difference comes from retakes: the cheaper test is the one you pass in one attempt. PTE results usually arrive within 48 hours, which helps if you are close to a deadline.

Which PTE test should you book?

  • Canada PR (Express Entry): PTE Core. IRCC does not accept PTE Academic for Express Entry.
  • Australian visas: PTE Academic, with Home Affairs’ minimum scores for each skill.
  • Study abroad: PTE Academic, if your university accepts it.
  • UK visas: PTE Academic UKVI or a PTE Home test, depending on the visa.

When should you book PTE?

Book once your timed practice scores reach your target in every skill. Because results usually come within two days, you can test fairly close to a deadline, but leave room for one retake in case a single skill falls short. If you need a weekend slot or a particular centre, book earlier, as those dates fill first.

How to avoid paying the fee twice

  • Book only when your timed practice scores are at your target in every skill, not just overall.
  • Check the version your university or visa needs before you pay.
  • Make sure your passport is valid on test day and matches your booking exactly.

Frequently asked questions

What is the PTE exam fee in India in 2026?
₹18,900 including GST for PTE Academic, PTE Academic UKVI and PTE Core. It rose from ₹18,000 on 16 March 2026.
Is the PTE Core fee the same as PTE Academic?
Yes. PTE Core costs ₹18,900 in India, the same as PTE Academic.
Is PTE cheaper than IELTS?
Only slightly. In 2026, PTE costs ₹18,900 and IELTS Academic or General Training ₹19,000 in India.
Can I reschedule my PTE exam?
Yes, free of charge up to 14 days before your test. Within 14 days you have to cancel and book again.
Do I get a refund if I cancel PTE?
A full refund more than 14 days before the test, 50% between 14 and 7 days, and nothing within 7 days.
How soon do PTE results come?
Usually within 48 hours, and sometimes up to five days.
