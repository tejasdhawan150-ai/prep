CELPIP vs IELTS for Canada PR: Which Test Is Easier for You?

Neither test is easier for everyone. According to IRCC, CELPIP-General and IELTS General Training are both approved for Express Entry. CELPIP is fully on computer, uses Canadian English and maps directly to CLB levels. IELTS General Training has a face-to-face speaking test and converts to CLB through a table. Choose the format you perform best in.

What are the main differences between CELPIP and IELTS?

Delivery, speaking format, accent and how scores convert to CLB.

How do CELPIP and IELTS scores map to CLB?

CELPIP maps one-to-one: each CELPIP skill level equals the same CLB level. IELTS General Training uses a per-skill table.

IELTS General Training minimum band per skill for each CLB level:

IRCC can update these levels, so check the current language test equivalency chart on canada.ca before you apply.

Notice that for IELTS the band needed differs by skill — Listening usually needs a higher band than Reading for the same CLB. With CELPIP you read the CLB straight from your score. Try your IELTS scores in the IELTS band calculator.

Where can you take CELPIP and IELTS?

IELTS is offered widely around the world. CELPIP is Canada-focused and is also offered in some other countries. Check the current test locations on celpip.ca before you plan around CELPIP — especially if you are outside Canada.

What mistakes do people make when choosing?

  • Taking IELTS Academic for Express Entry. IRCC lists IELTS General Training, not Academic.
  • Looking at the overall score. CLB is judged per skill, so your weakest skill decides the result.
  • Switching tests without a reason. If you have prepared for one format, switching late means learning new task types. Take a timed sample first.
  • Booking before checking validity. Results must be less than 2 years old when you use them, so plan the test date around your application.

Should you choose CELPIP or IELTS?

Both count the same way for Express Entry, so choose the test where you expect your weakest skill to score higher.

Choose CELPIP if…

  • You are comfortable with North American accents and Canadian everyday topics.
  • You are fine speaking to a computer and typing your writing.
  • You want to read your CLB level directly from your score.
  • A test centre is available where you live.

Choose IELTS General Training if…

  • You speak better with a real person in front of you.
  • You want a paper option or many test dates and locations.
  • You may also need a test for other countries.
  • You have already prepared for IELTS and know the format.

Frequently asked questions

IELTS vs CELPIP: which is easier?
Neither is easier for everyone. CELPIP suits people comfortable with North American English and speaking to a computer; IELTS General Training suits people who prefer a face-to-face speaking test. Try a sample of each.
Are both CELPIP and IELTS accepted for Canada PR?
Yes. According to IRCC, CELPIP-General and IELTS General Training are both approved English tests for Express Entry, along with PTE Core. IELTS Academic is not accepted for Express Entry.
How long does CELPIP or IELTS preparation take?
It depends on your current level, the CLB level you need, your familiarity with the format and your weekly practice time. A diagnostic mock in each format is the best guide.
What score should I target?
It depends on your program. Check IRCC's minimum for your program; since higher CLB levels can add points, many applicants aim above the minimum.
Can I take CELPIP in India?
CELPIP is Canada-focused and is also offered in some other countries. Check test locations on celpip.ca, because the list can change.
PTE vs IELTS?
PTE is computer-based and machine-scored; IELTS is examiner-marked. For Canada, the approved versions are PTE Core and IELTS General Training.
